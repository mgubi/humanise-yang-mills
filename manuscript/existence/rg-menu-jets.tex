\section{The single-menu derivative supplies the field jet}
\label{sec:ex-lattice-menu-jets}
\label{subsec:ex-lattice-menu-jet-proof}

Fix one selected step and its finite menu family indexed by $i\in I$. Let
$M_i$ be the leaf attached to menu $i$, let $r_i\geq0$ be its tuning weight,
and let $\phi_i$ be its charged fee. The selected menu profile has
$r_i=\phi_i$. For each activity label $j$ and configuration $U$, let
$K(t,F)_j(U)$ be the final response code with switch parameter $t$ and
selected menu set $F\subseteq I$. The activity payment supplies a score
family $g_{j,U,t}(A)$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 K(t,F)_j(U)=\sum_{A\subseteq F}t^{|A|}g_{j,U,t}(A),\qquad
 |g_{j,U,t}(A)|\leq b_{j,U}(A),\qquad
 \sum_{A\subseteq I}b_{j,U}(A)<\infty,
 \label{eq:ex-lattice-menu-score}
\end{equation}
with each score converging as $t\downarrow0$. The exact selected
interpolation identities are
\begin{equation}
 K(t,\varnothing)_j(U)=P_i(0)_j(U),\qquad
 K(t,\{i\})_j(U)=P_i(r_i t)_j(U),
 \label{eq:ex-lattice-menu-singleton-path}
\end{equation}
where $P_i(\theta)$ is the final response produced from the single leaf
$M_i$ at amplitude $\theta$. These are not approximations: with no active
switch the selected color is the vacuum color, and with just switch $i$ its
color is the single-leaf color at amplitude $r_i t$.

The path $P_i$ is differentiable at zero. Before the last branch, its
color has the form $c+\theta a$ for fixed $c,a$, so every activity entry
is affine in $\theta$. Each selected fragrance entry is a finite product
of such entries, raised to fixed integer powers and divided by a fixed
factorial. If $f_1,\ldots,f_N$ are differentiable at zero, then
\[
 \left(\prod_{k=1}^N f_k\right)'(0)
   =\sum_{k=1}^N f_k'(0)\prod_{\ell\ne k}f_\ell(0),
\]
by repeated use of the product rule. The final response at a fixed activity
coordinate is itself a product over a finite set of labels; branches outside
the selected address range are identically zero, and branches inside it are
these finite products. Thus every coordinate $P_i(\theta)_j(U)$ is
differentiable at zero. Denote its derivative by
\[
 D_{i,j}(U)=\left.\frac{d}{d\theta}P_i(\theta)_j(U)\right|_{\theta=0}.
\]
This is exactly the hinge of the selected gauge door, since that door is
defined by differentiating the same one-leaf final response.

For completeness, the score extraction from \eqref{eq:ex-lattice-menu-score}
is triangular. For $t>0$ and any $F\subseteq I$,
\[
 g_{j,U,t}(F)=t^{-|F|}\left(K(t,F)_j(U)
       -\sum_{A\subsetneq F}t^{|A|}g_{j,U,t}(A)\right).
\]
Induction on $|F|$ therefore shows that all scores are uniquely determined
by the switched values. The payment model also gives convergence to the
zero-parameter score; the uniform summable majorant in
\eqref{eq:ex-lattice-menu-score} permits passage to that limit.

Take $F=\{i\}$. From \eqref{eq:ex-lattice-menu-score},
\[
 K(t,\{i\})_j(U)=g_{j,U,t}(\varnothing)
                      +t\,g_{j,U,t}(\{i\}).
\]
At $F=\varnothing$ the same representation gives
$K(t,\varnothing)_j(U)=g_{j,U,t}(\varnothing)$. Subtract these equations
and use \eqref{eq:ex-lattice-menu-singleton-path}:
\[
 t\,g_{j,U,t}(\{i\})=P_i(r_i t)_j(U)-P_i(0)_j(U).
\]
Dividing by $t$ and letting $t\downarrow0$, differentiability at zero
implies
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{t\downarrow0}g_{j,U,t}(\{i\})=r_iD_{i,j}(U)
       =\phi_iD_{i,j}(U).
 \label{eq:ex-lattice-menu-jet-proof}
\end{equation}
This is the exact menu-to-field jet identity required in
Section~\ref{subsec:ex-lattice-rg-canopy}. It supplies the fee-weighted
hinge for each menu separately; the subsequent finite marker-algebra
calculation and absolutely convergent barcode sum produce the per-box
singleton coefficient.
