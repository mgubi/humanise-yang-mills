\section{Local Gaussian prices in the common reader frame}
\label{sec:actual-reader-local-gaussian-prices}

We combine the actual local-window comparison with the exact tree
identity of Section~\ref{sec:actual-reader-tree-covariance}. This
gives both the mean and every cross-entry of the curvature covariance
in the one frame used by the reader family.

Fix the patch, predictor kernel, path bounds and adjacent tower data.
Their numerical costs are fixed before the finite period is chosen.
Let $x$ be the large reader parameter and $t$ its recording parameter,
chosen with
\[
 x\le t\le x^2,\qquad B=\lceil(\log t)^2\rceil.
\]
The recording parameter is chosen so that its coarse flag threshold
equals the reader's healthy threshold. All sector matrices below use
this same $t$ and its flag set. Assume the coarse torus side is at least
$100(1+B)$ and retain the predictor branch and fine-side conditions
of Section~\ref{sec:actual-reader-tree-covariance}.

Use a pose of the coarse field on a room of radius $W$ around $o$:
one gauge makes that field equal there to the exponential of one
real direction $v$, whose supremum norm is at most half the
selected logarithmic clearance allowance. For every interior coarse
address $z$ with
\[
 \operatorname{dist}(o,z)+w(x)\le W,
\]
assume its $B$-neighbourhood is in that same logarithmic chart,
the replica has the prescribed clearance bound there, and no seed
lies within $11B+1$ of $z$. Here $w(x)$ is the reader's fixed room
margin. These are the local conditions of the pose, not separate
choices of gauges at each address.

Let $\mathcal U$ be the actual active link-coordinate set for the
chosen store and forest, and let
$\iota:\mathcal U\to V_F$ insert its coordinates into the complete
forest coordinate set. Let $H$ be the actual ordered window inverse,
and let $H^0$ be the ordered window inverse with empty store and
identity coarse field, using the same forest, tag and window geometry.
Write $C=(H+H^{\mathsf T})/2$ for the actual Gaussian covariance,
and $C^0=(H^0+(H^0)^{\mathsf T})/2$ on $V_F$ for the bare one.
The prescribed mean with arbitrary frozen links is denoted $m$;
the stationary mean in the single direction $v$ is $m^0$.

Assume the small-window specialization in
Section~\ref{sec:ex-lattice-actual-reader-small-window} has supplied its
mean and column estimates for these actual data. These state, for every
interior column $j$ and every actual active row $i$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reader-local-column-price}
 |m_j-m^0_{\iota(j)}|\le t^{-7/8},\qquad
 |H_{ij}-H^0_{\iota(i),\iota(j)}|\le t^{-1/4}.
\end{equation}
Its hypotheses are precisely the chart and clearance conditions just
listed. In particular its frozen-source cancellation holds for every
choice of the frozen field and auxiliary channel parameters; no
bound on those arbitrary frozen values is introduced here.
If both $i,j$ are interior, the two column estimates imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reader-local-symmetric-price}
 |C_{ij}-C^0_{\iota(i),\iota(j)}|\le t^{-1/4}.
\end{equation}
This follows by adding the two inequalities and dividing by two.

\subsection{Removing the bare cut and window}

Let $A_F$ be the full flat precision on $V_F$ identified in
Section~\ref{sec:actual-reader-tree-covariance}. The bare reference
still uses a path cutoff and a principal window. We show, at the
fixed patch, that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reader-bare-full-price}
 \|C^0-A_F^{-1}\|_0\le K e^{-cB}
\end{equation}
for constants $K,c>0$ independent of the period, forest and tag.
Here $\|\cdot\|_0$ is the maximum of absolute row and column sums.

The actual path cutoff is $r=\lfloor B/100000\rfloor$. Its
exponentially weighted path bound gives a row tail at most
$C_1e^{-\sigma r/4}$ in a narrower weighted norm, with
$\sigma>0$ fixed. Taking Gram products preserves such a bound:
if $L$ is the full row map and $L_r$ its cut version, then
\[
 L^{\mathsf T}L-L_r^{\mathsf T}L_r
 =(L-L_r)^{\mathsf T}L+L_r^{\mathsf T}(L-L_r).
\]
The weighted row-and-column norm is submultiplicative, and both
row maps have fixed bounds. Thus the corresponding precisions
$A_F,A_{F,r}$ differ by at most $C_2e^{-\sigma r/4}$.
The cut does not remove the direct curl or predictor terms, and
every remaining path term is a nonnegative Gram form. Retaining
just the identity channel in the normalized signed average gives
a lower bound equal to $1/|\mathcal S|$ times its direct-curl and
predictor form, where $\mathcal S$ is the finite signed-channel set.
Apply the tree inequality of Section~\ref{sec:actual-reader-tree-covariance}
with direct coefficient $\alpha>0$ from
\eqref{eq:reader-flat-factor}: the cut precision has lower bound
$\min(\alpha,\kappa)/[|\mathcal S|(1+b_1+b_2)]$. Thus both
cut and uncut precisions have a common fixed positive gap, without
using the uncut metric factorization for the cut rows.
The weighted inverse estimate of Section~\ref{sec:local-covariance}
and the identity
\[
 A_{F,r}^{-1}-A_F^{-1}
 =A_{F,r}^{-1}(A_F-A_{F,r})A_F^{-1}
\]
therefore give the same exponential order for the inverse difference.
All norms can be taken at one smaller fixed positive width.

For the actual bare principal windows the inner radius is
$\lfloor B/60\rfloor$, by the panel inclusion in
Section~\ref{sec:principal-comparison-jets}. The window-error proof
of Section~\ref{sec:local-covariance} then bounds the difference
between the ordered bare window inverse and $A_{F,r}^{-1}$ by
$C_3e^{-\gamma\lfloor B/60\rfloor}$, with $\gamma>0$.
The empty store has all forest coordinates active. Hence restriction
to these coordinates identifies its windows and cut flat precision
with the ones just used; it introduces no further projection of an
inverse. Symmetrization does not increase the zero-width norm.
For $B\ge200000$,
\[
 B\le120\lfloor B/60\rfloor,\qquad
 B\le200000\lfloor B/100000\rfloor.
\]
Taking $c\le\min(\gamma/120,\sigma/800000)$ and adding the two
errors proves \eqref{eq:reader-bare-full-price}. Since
$B\ge(\log t)^2$, eventually $e^{-cB}\le t^{-1/4}$.

\subsection{Reading curvature and preserving every cross-entry}

Let $L=DI_F$ be the full forest-coordinate curl matrix, with colour
identity included. Each of its absolute row sums is bounded by a
fixed number $U$, uniformly in period and forest. A nonzero entry
$L_{ai}$ places the parent anchor of $i$ within one coarse step
of the plaquette anchor of $a$. Therefore, if a plaquette $a$
satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{dist}(o,\operatorname{anchor}(a))+w(x)+1\le W,
\]
every coordinate used by its curl row is interior. The clearance
condition makes all these forest coordinates active. This is why
the actual restricted curl and $L$ read precisely the same
coordinates on the plaquettes under consideration.

For two such plaquettes $a,b$, expand their covariance as a finite
double sum. Applying \eqref{eq:reader-local-symmetric-price} to
each pair of used coordinates gives
\[
 |\operatorname{Cov}_{\rm actual}(a,b)
                 -(LC^0L^{\mathsf T})_{ab}|
 \le t^{-1/4}\sum_i|L_{ai}|\sum_j|L_{bj}|
 \le U^2t^{-1/4}.
\]
No independence between coordinates or plaquettes was used.
The same calculation and \eqref{eq:reader-bare-full-price} give
\[
 |(LC^0L^{\mathsf T})_{ab}
                 -(LA_F^{-1}L^{\mathsf T})_{ab}|
 \le U^2Ke^{-cB}\le U^2Kt^{-1/4}.
\]
By \eqref{eq:reader-tree-conditional-covariance}, the last full
covariance is the colour extension of $P_f-JP_cJ^{\mathsf T}$.
Consequently, eventually in $x$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reader-strong-scope-price}
 \left|\operatorname{Cov}_{\rm actual}(a,b)
       -(P_f-JP_cJ^{\mathsf T})_{ab}\right|
 \le U^2(1+K)t^{-1/4}\le x^{-1/8}.
\end{equation}
For the last inequality, $t\ge x$ and the fixed constant
$U^2(1+K)$ is eventually at most $x^{1/8}$. The mean estimate
in \eqref{eq:reader-local-column-price} similarly implies
$|m_j-m^0_{\iota(j)}|\le x^{-7/8}$.

Finally, the actual covariance $C$ is positive definite with a
uniform entry bound, whether or not a pose is available. Indeed the
sector gap and row bounds hold for the actual flag store, and the
positive local covariance construction of
Section~\ref{sec:local-covariance} applies to either admitted window
geometry. Its row bound bounds each entry. Enlarge this constant to
at least one and take the maximum of the finitely many thresholds
used above. This one choice supplies positivity and bounded entries
for every permitted store, together with the mean and curvature
comparison on every admissible pose, forest, tag and period.

These are the quantitative local Gaussian estimates. The nonlinear
comparison between a group-valued field read and its linear Gaussian
coordinate, the lost-mask probability and the selected-history
telescoping error are additional steps; none is inferred from a
covariance identity alone.
