The four-dimensional Yang–Mills construction
Foundations and the existence argument

by Humanise Yang–Mills contributors

Working draft, 16 November 2026

Status. This working draft presents the construction-bound existence theorem and its same-family proof. The final assembly identifies the quantitative producers, the all-period limits and the reconstruction in every character. The source correspondence and scoped independent agent reviews are recorded in the accompanying blueprint; they do not constitute independent human acceptance. The mathematical exposition remains a draft for review. No mass gap or uniqueness of the infinite-volume limit is claimed.

Source and attribution. The argument follows the Lean formalisation at https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028, pinned to revision

3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464.

The initial source clustering and declaration maps used to organize this exposition come from Scott Armstrong's Yang–Mills informalization project, https://github.com/scottnarmstrong/yang-mills-informalization.
