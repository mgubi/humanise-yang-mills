Index of notation

Two dictionaries. Geometric objects: a site is a point of \(\Lambda_s\); a plaquette is an elementary square, with holonomy \(U_{y ; ab}\); a path is a finite chain of links along which curvatures are transported; the stencil \(y + \Sigma_v\) is the finite site set an insertion depends on; the support \(X_i\) or \(Y_i\) is the set on which an activity is local; the anchor \(o_i \in X_i\) is the site at which a regular activity is counted in its weighted sum; the complete read support consists of all links and flags a local term depends on, including those inspected by its tests; a cover is an exact cover \(\mathfrak{a} \in \mathfrak{F}_m (U)\) of the flagged sites by disjoint forced supports; the inventory \(I_i\) is the set of flagged sites in a forced support; a cluster is a connected graph of polymers in a logarithmic expansion, or a group of nearby insertions in the product expansion. Field objects: a label is \(c \in V_d\) and an insertion label is \((d, c) \in \mathcal{T}\); the coefficient vector \(\mathbf{x}_m (\mathbf{v})\) is the label of a blocked insertion; the lattice insertion is \(I_{N, p} (v, y ; U)\); the quotient field is \([c] \in F_d\); its response is \(\iota_d [c]\), its correlations against all spectators; a field label is \(\ell \in \mathcal{L}\).
