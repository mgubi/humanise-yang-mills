Common author instructions

Use the central notation registry. In this specification, a_N is the spacing, R_p the physical period (source L_p), L a block ratio, b>0 the block penalty, and beta an inverse coupling. Time is coordinate 4, not the zero-based time coordinate 3 occurring in some outline descriptions. Use V_d for polynomial insertion labels of scaling dimension at most d, N_d(S) for correlation-null labels, F_d=V_d/N_d(S) for fields, and F_d^0 for free fields. Do not identify different filtration levels without the inclusion map iota_{d,e}. Write theta for antilinear reflected words and reserve capital Theta for the centered trace. Use B_h(w) for the slab insertion operator (thickness h), avoiding the degree index d as a thickness. Use Xi for phase space and xi for a phase. The registry uses M_d^Max, S^Max for the free spaces and correlations; the shorthand F_d^0, S^0 in the contracts below denotes those same objects. In typeset prose use the registry names. A superscript 0 in these contracts denotes the free reference, not a zero-energy projection.

Every output below must be stated with its hypotheses, quantifier order, constant dependence and choice of underlying family. A chapter writer may use an earlier output as a theorem only after its status is certified in the proof ledger; otherwise write an explicitly conditional result. Each chapter should open with a half-page inventory of imported objects and definitions. Remind the reader of any construction-specific notation after 3–4 pages without use. Omit formal-code package names from prose. Introduce any useful auxiliary notion by its mathematical content, not by translating its metaphor literally.

C1. The free Maxwell comparison theory (20 pages)

Purpose. Supply a concrete reference space and finite detecting correlations before using them to control ranks of interacting fields. This chapter is independent of construction of the interacting limit.

Sections. (1) Gaussian curvature covariance and Wick polynomials (4 pp). Fix the Green function Gamma(x)=1/(4 pi^2 |x|^2) and derive the curvature covariance from the declared sign convention for derivatives in its two arguments. Define on-shell Taylor jets by dF=delta F=0; Taylor coefficients include factorials. Curvature has scaling dimension 2 and each derivative adds 1. (2) Polynomial quotient and reflection positivity (5 pp). Explain why covariance null relations require quotienting by Maxwell equations; distinguish gauge-invariant jet polynomials from all polynomials. Use Gaussian/Wick theory by reference. Retain the particular Kelvin-transform calculation that makes the finite jet Gram form strictly positive. (3) Finite detecting families (4 pp). Turn nondegeneracy into a finite family of correlation functionals, specifying whether a row is a singleton or pair. Constants and the unit are included. (4) Degree-four tensors and their divergences (3 pp). Give the finite bases, the divergence matrix and its rank, rather than reproducing repeated component computations. Separate the scalar constant from centered tensors. (5) Stress flux and finite Ward tests (4 pp). Fix the exact normalization using a unit-mass mollifier and explain how derivatives of probes are handled. State the bounded-degree external detector and internal-symmetry test needed in C3.

Inputs. Compact matrix group and the declared trace inner product; finite jet representation, conjugation and signed coordinate permutations. Only the free Gaussian covariance is imported analytically. No phase, interacting Ward identity or rotation theorem is assumed.

Outputs. C1.a: a Euclidean free correlation family S^0, field spaces F_d^0, units, injective inclusions, derivative maps and O(4) actions; strict reflected positivity for each nonzero on-shell polynomial. C1.b: for every finite degree d, finitely many separated test rows give an injective linear map from F_d^0 to a finite-dimensional coordinate space. C1.c: the centered real hypercubic degree-four symmetric tensors have the stated three-dimensional candidate space; divergence has rank two and a one-dimensional kernel generated by the Maxwell stress. C1.d: the normalized free stress has translation and rotation Ward fluxes with the stated common nonzero coefficient. Finite vector-map tests detect exactly scalar multiples of the derivative action; finite internal tests eliminate the bracket defect. Keep the exact domain/degree cutoffs from the source statements; do not enlarge them to arbitrary maps.
