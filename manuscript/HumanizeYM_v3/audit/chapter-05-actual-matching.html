Remaining exact conditions, not silently discharged

  • The selected incoming uncut classical path action must satisfy ch05:path-budget: fixed finite path arity, connected rooted supports, and absolute coefficient sum bounded after weighting by exp(sigma times total true path length). Products of path rows require the corresponding product budget. This is a condition on the actual selected coefficient family, not merely a bound on a flat kernel.

  • Its quadratic coefficient must equal the Kf used in the chosen Gaussian construction. In direct/path-square coordinates this means the actual linearized row factorization, including orientation normalization, gives the selected Hf (or agrees on all curls). Higher curvature-degree atoms have no quadratic coefficient; this fact does not establish the row-factorization identity for arbitrary supplied coefficients. A positive actual metric alone suffices for the stationary chart; identifying its reduced Hessian with the particular historical Kc additionally requires this coefficient match.

  • Common minimizing-branch equality is proved for the actual frame/predictor tags. It does not identify an arbitrarily modified local test action, a different Borel mask, or a different incoming coefficient family with this one. Cutoff recognition on shrinking plateaux and Gaussian exterior estimates remain Chapter6/9 issues.

  • Fixed-L supremum stationary estimates do not by themselves imply every ratio-uniform weighted inverse estimate required for one-loop nonlinear response. The actual smooth transport bridge is now supplied, but weighted Hessian/vertex estimates for the differentiated one-loop determinant remain separate inputs if their norm is stronger than the chapter's fixed-L counting/supremum norms.

  • Gauge changes of the determinant must retain Haar and gauge normalization contributions. The tangent identification is not a license to replace a full nonlinear background by its linear tangent in a second derivative of the one-loop action.

Fixed-scale convergence of the actual selected free rows

Added ch05:horizon-convergence and Proposition ch05:horizon-limit. Source packet: Martin existence/free-horizon-convergence.tex (all subsections), with the exact covariance recurrence and uniform alias/strip estimates already developed in Chapter5. This closes the previous qualitative quadratic cutoff-convergence interface for the fixed nested schedule.

The actual schedule is held fixed at every scale while its fine endpoint is extended. Its directional driving-filter squares decrease on real momenta; extending the history leaves old noise contributions unchanged and appends a nonnegative contribution. Uniform noise bounds and a summable high-alias majorant give convergence of each nonzero-momentum directional covariance. All rational metric and transport denominators have positive limits off zero. The common analytic strip then identifies unique infinite-cover coefficient limits, and convergence holds in every smaller exponential coefficient norm. Zero modes are treated by continuity of the regular cluster symbols, without assigning a finite massless covariance at zero.

The specified exterior lifts are the fixed coordinate divided-difference lifts, so they inherit convergence; arbitrary fresh solutions of an exterior identity would not. Metric amendments are fixed before cutoff selection. Positive square roots are the canonical positive roots and converge by positive-semidefinite uniqueness on all tori plus recovery of cover coefficients from unbounded periods. A direct-factor root of Htilde-cI requires the stated fixed strict gap margin. Finite products/inverses of these rows, including the usual retained factor blocks, inherit convergence. The actual predictor correction R and its cardinal extension are fixed finite-range rows at fixed scale, so the corrected tangent inherits the same conclusion.

Martin flat-step.tex and flat-estimate.tex concern a different quantitative cutoff-rate mechanism. The latter explicitly does not prove the former RG majorant hypothesis. Neither is needed for the qualitative free-row limit proved here, and no rate is claimed. Nonlinear activity convergence and marked fixed-input continuity are still separate obligations; this proof does not identify those with Gaussian convergence.
