Deliberate scope

No infinite-volume limit, collision extension, or all-phase reconstruction is claimed here. Full cutoff convergence at fixed p and volume-uniform separated estimates are distinct quantifiers. Position coordinate ties are permitted whenever complete points are distinct modulo the period. Difference stencils use physical lengths.
