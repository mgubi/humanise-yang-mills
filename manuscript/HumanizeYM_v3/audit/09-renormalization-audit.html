Checked identities and quantitative deductions

Gaussian normalization and supports

The active/dummy coordinate multiplier is (2pi)^(-(D-n_act q)/2) g^(n_act q)c_H^(n_act) =(2pi)^(-D/2)m^(N_free) exp(-(N_free-n_act)log m), m=c_H(2pi g²)^(q/2). This is an exact scalar equality. The source's b=sqrt(2pi) has been eliminated to avoid collision with the monograph's block penalty b.

The actual sector finite-range covariance is not asserted equal to the inverse of the affine precision. Its density correction remains among factors. Complete integration dependence regions include covariance-range halos, Haar variables and tag coordinates. Independence holds only for disjoint such regions. Exclusion supports Y used by exact covers are separate; their disjointness does not imply that all flag dependence is disjoint.

First connectivity integrates elementary products whose dependence regions overlap. Second connectivity is the logarithmic cluster graph on the resulting polymer labels, allowing repetitions. Model-specific same-law estimates enter before both sums. No product of separate L1 estimates is used.

Degree gain

Each elementary factor has a majorant at most bfrak^-7/16 after a subpower loss. Inflating each by h=bfrak^3/8 leaves the smallness parameter bfrak^-1/16. A cluster with total elementary degree⩾3 gains h^-3, while the inflated total rooted mass is P h bfrak^-7/16. Hence exponent -19/16, relaxed to -23/20 with reserve3/80. These are elementary degrees across polymer occurrences, not merely a count of cluster vertices. Checked with exact rational arithmetic.

The inherited regular term's principal local weight begins at5 after gauge removal and zero affine Hessian. Summation over L^4 fine anchors leaves L^-1. This uses actual radial-gauge interpolation derivative estimates; the draft expressly conditions the full estimate on these, rather than deriving a theorem from engineering dimensions alone. For d³⩾L, the replacement 1⩽L^-5(1+d)^15 is valid, so support tails can be controlled at the same rate.

Subtraction and path reset

The local split is h_Xf=f(1)+h_o Q_f-zeta_f+h_ZR_f, R_f=f-f(1)-Q_f, zeta_f=(1-h_Z)(f(1)+h_oQ_f-h_Xf). Checked separately on h_Z=1 and0. No mask derivatives.

Color invariance gives Df(1)=0; the second Ward identity then annihilates local gradients without additional gauge-orbit second derivative. On nonwrapping supports zero-curl affine potentials are gradients of quadratic site fields, allowing constant-curl projection. Schur's lemma identifies the color bilinear form. Hypercubic symmetry kills orientation off-diagonals and makes diagonal sums equal Delta. Projection bounds give t²|Delta|⩽C H_pre. This precedes any scalar reset.

Channel identity: (alpha+Delta)L_d+h_Y Delta a_d(Q_d-P_d) =alpha h_Y a_dP_d+Delta a_dh_oQ_d+J_d, where J_d is the displayed complement-of-small-field discrepancy. Regular costs involve H_pre and |alpha|t³. Logarithmic costs may involve |alpha|t, because the clipped energy on a large-field field need only be linear in t. Both are accounted for in ch09:reset-cost. The reset error bound H_pre/t² tends to zero as beta_hat^-1/10/Mcut².

Wilson start

For a small plaquette the residual energy is h k+(1-h)(1-cdir)W-chi_rel k, with0⩽k⩽(1-cdir)W. It is nonnegative for every allowed h,chi_rel, including nearby flagged configurations. The algebraic energy split was checked numerically for all four h,chi_rel choices and representative W,k; the symbolic cancellation is displayed in the chapter. A relaxed test can fail even when that plaquette and its vertices are small, because its neighborhood is larger. The draft retains the resulting residual k-chi_rel k instead of setting it to zero.

For a large plaquette the residual is W-clin t chord⩾chord²/4. Component counting uses radius2B overlap inventories, no assumption that every possible exact cover is a connected-component cover: only this particular initialization's indicators force that unique nonzero selection.

Truncation uniformity: corrected audit interpretation

An initial concern that Scott's compact-coupling truncation claimed too much has been withdrawn after reading D351. The selected low-order terms expressly contain no incoming activities; they depend only on the prescribed path, coupling and fixed labels. Hence weighted continuity on the compact coupling band does give a uniform truncation for all inputs with those frozen data. Terms depending on incoming activities were separated and estimated by inherited/marked-factor bounds. The chapter now states this stronger valid conclusion. Compactness along an arbitrary activity curve alone would give only a curve-specific truncation, but that is not the source's actual low-order construction. This audit does not claim a source gap here.
