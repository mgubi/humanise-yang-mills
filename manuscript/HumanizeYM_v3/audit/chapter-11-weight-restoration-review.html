Checks

  • The norm's cover really is connected (Chapter 4), so the dispersal argument can charge disjoint initial connected paths around separated cell neighborhoods. Its distance applies between cells, not between two marks inside one cell.

  • With at most n=2 marks per cell, changing marker weight costs at most H^(Un) for a one-cell activity. Multicell activities pay through the spare connected-support exponent. The stated condition q>=kappa+Un+2 is sufficient, uniformly in the number of cells.

  • The failed-cutoff expansion is exact in the square-zero marker algebra. A component with new failed factors must retain an old selected inventory. At most 2^n new factors per cell can be charged to an old activity intersecting that cell neighborhood. Thus the auxiliary e^g and H^(2^n t(X)) accounting is consistent and does not presume independence of flags.

  • For the upper numerical step, l=max(1,H^-3/(alpha z+H^-q)) gives the stated nonprincipal bound in both cases l=1 and l>1. The choice alpha>=8T gives the incidence margin, eventually. Choosing the reported G exponent small relative to the actual owner exponent can pay m=G^-2 while retaining positive inventory decay. This must be a pre-history reserve, as the text now says.

  • The lower incidence estimate is valid when its stated incoming attenuation is retained. Paying G/a_low<=H^-q is available for the actual polynomial attenuation and reserved exponential decay, not for an arbitrary function a_low merely satisfying 0<=a_low<=1.

Explicit quantitative premise requested

The argument uses R_e and R_(e+1) as support charges without yet bounding their growth in H. Require them, C_*, L^4 T_e and the finite incidence constants to be H^o(1) on the comparison windows (or fixed with a uniform finite-stage entry payment). Otherwise neither the new-factor auxiliary sum 2^n C_* R_(e+1) H^-7/8 <= H^-1/2/2 nor the principal remainder 2^n R_e H^-27/32 <= H^-3/4 follows. A cover bound for the actual prescribed enlarged supports, followed by the finite-stage delay, supplies the relevant R bounds; a bare diameter or number of marks is insufficient. Sent this request to the author and parent.
