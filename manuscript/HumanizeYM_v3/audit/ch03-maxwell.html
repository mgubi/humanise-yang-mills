Added and checked in the second draft

  • Proposition ch03:jet-positive: mixed-order strict positivity via Kelvin inversion. Compared Martin all-grade-free-detectors.tex:13–260 with Scott eq:G-kelvin/eq:G-pivots. Fixed the probe normalization explicitly: sum over ordered antisymmetric indices and derivative tuples, divided by j!. The harmonic derivative identity gives the scalar c_j, the two curls give (j+1)(j+2)(-2)^j/pi²; the second-insertion derivative sign cancels its parity. Translation after inversion gives a lower-triangular transformation with identity diagonal, so positivity is simultaneous in all jet orders. No interacting hypothesis occurs.

  • Corollary ch03:detectors: cited Wick/Fock polynomial pairing gives positive monomial weights, including the unit. Finite basis columns detect every field in the on-shell image, identifying all-spectator null labels there. This is now available to UV rank comparisons, provided their maps and normalizations are separately established.

  • Proposition ch03:stress-line: signed-coordinate parity gives three candidate centered degree-four tensors. Independently evaluated their divergence against the two quadratic test fields by exact integer contractions of the kernel Hessian and third derivatives at e1; obtained (-256,0,128) and (0,-512,-128), agreeing with Scott. One off-diagonal correlation detects the third candidate. Known algebraic Maxwell conservation gives the one-dimensional kernel, without an additional surjectivity assertion.

Added and checked in the third draft

  • Vector response tests (ch03:vector-tests): physical-ward-free-classification.tex:220–470. Reproduces the three-quadratic Wick formula, mixed panel rank-one factorization, reciprocity, signed-parity coefficient comparison, normalized dilation commutator, and inversion parity argument.   • Internal rotation defect (ch03:internal-tests): free-internal-ward-erasure.tex:45–235. The Householder covariance comparison is asserted only at weight four.
