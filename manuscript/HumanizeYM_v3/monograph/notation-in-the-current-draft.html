Notation in the current draft

This index lists the main symbols used in the written chapters. Auxiliary symbols are defined where used. The chapter and equation references give the precise conventions; in particular, lattice distances and physical distances must not be interchanged.

Symbol Meaning and definition
\(G, \mathfrak{g}, n_G\) Compact matrix gauge group, its real Lie algebra and matrix size; Section 1.1.
\(\| \cdot \|_G\) Normalized matrix norm, \(\|A\|_G^2 = n_G^{- 1} \text{tr} (A^{\ast} A)\); (1.1).
\(C_{\mathfrak{g}}, \kappa_G\) Adjoint Casimir and inverse-coupling logarithmic coefficient; (1.3)–(1.4).
\(\Lambda_s, E_s\) Periodic lattice with \(s\) sites per direction and its positive links; Section 1.2.
\(U_e, U_{y ; \mu \nu}\) Link variable and oriented plaquette holonomy; (1.6).
\(E_s^{\text{W}}\) Wilson energy, the sum of \(\tfrac{1}{2} \|U_{y ; \mu \nu} - 1\|_G^2\) over plaquettes; (1.7).
\(Z_{s, \beta}, \mu_{s, \beta}\) Partition function and Wilson probability measure; Definition 1.1.
\(R_p, L_m\) Physical period and odd block ratio, respectively; Definition 1.3.
\(D_m, a_m, \tau_m\) Cumulative lattice denominator, spacing \(D_m^{- 1}\) and logarithmic scale \(\log D_m\); (1.11).
\(N, m, p\) Ultraviolet cutoff, renormalization level and physical-volume index; Section 1.3.
\(\text{Conf}_n\) Configurations of \(n\) distinct points in \(\mathbb{R}^4\); Definition 1.5.
\(V_d\) Invariant radial-jet polynomials of scaling dimension at most \(d\); (2.2).
\(N_{\text{fac}}\) Polynomial factor-count operator, distinct from scaling dimension; (2.3).
\(P_d^{\text{loc}}\) Finite-stencil representative of a local polynomial; Theorem 2.4.
\(S_n^{\text{Max}}\) Wick correlations in the free Maxwell comparison theory; Section 3.2.
\(\mathcal{S}_j, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle_j\) Homogeneous degree-\(j\) Maxwell solution jets (Section 3.3) and their coefficient inner product (Section 3.5).
\(\omega_d\) Restriction from radial-potential labels to free solution jets; Section 3.3.
\(b\) Positive penalty in the soft block constraint, distinct from inverse coupling \(\beta\); Section 4.2.
\(\mathcal{B}^{(L)}_{\beta}\) Exact block transformation from \(\Lambda_{Ls}\) to \(\Lambda_s\) at ratio \(L\); (4.9).
\(\hat{\beta}_m, \mathfrak{B}_m\) Reference inverse coupling and the permitted interval around it; Section 4.4.
\(M_m^{\text{cut}}, t_m, B_m\) Cutoff amplitude, plaquette threshold and radius used in support weights; (4.16).
\(E_i, X_i, o_i\) Regular activity, its support and anchor; Section 4.6.
\(K_i, Y_i, I_i\) Large-field activity, exclusion support and prescribed set of large-field sites; Section 4.6. The full link-dependence region can be larger than \(Y_i\).
\(\mathcal{E}_m, \mathcal{K}_m\) Weighted regular and large-field activity sums; (4.24).
\(\Omega^r, d_r\) Lattice \(r\)-forms and forward exterior derivative; Section 5.1.
\(H, K\) Curvature metric and kinetic operator \(d_1^{\ast} Hd_1\); (5.1). These are operators, not polymer activities.
\(Q, A^{- 1}, W\) Linear map from fine to coarse fields, covariance of the normalized fine fluctuations, and map from a coarse field to its minimizing fine field; (5.3).
\(T\) Two-form transport associated with \(W\); (5.17).
\(\mathcal{Q}, W_a, W_F\) Linearized gauge-covariant block average and two representatives of the minimizing fine field, respectively in the smooth gauge and in the forest coordinates; Section 5.9.3.
\(\mathscr{Z}_{\text{blk}}\) Exact blocked density before its local reorganization; Section 9.1.
\(J (m), \mathscr{L}_j\) Stage at level \(m\) and odd block ratio selected for stage \(j\); Section 10.2.
\(\bar{\beta}, \beta_{N, p, m}\) Prescribed coarse coupling and actual coupling along the finite history; Theorem 10.1.
\(z_i, \mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{I}}\) Commuting nilpotent source variables and their finite coefficient algebra; Chapter 11.
\(q_m\) Density with coefficients indexed by subsets of insertions in Chapter 11; (11.2). In Chapter 10, \(q_m\) instead denotes the local scalar coupling correction.
\(M^{\text{UV}}_{p, v}\) Smooth fixed-volume correlation after the full ultraviolet limit; Theorem 12.5.
\(S_n (v ; x)\) Infinite-volume separated correlation along the selected volume subsequence; (13.1).
\(N_d (S), F_d\) Polynomials that vanish in every separated correlation, and the quotient by this null space; (13.5).
\(\Pi_d\) Limiting corrected coefficient projection; Proposition 13.1.
\(\iota_{d, e}, \partial_{\mu}\) Inclusions of filtered field spaces and derivatives with respect to an insertion point; Section 13.4.
\(\theta w, w^{\dagger}\) Antilinearly reflected word and its translated reflected word; Sections 13.5 and 13.6.
\(\mathcal{H}_{\mu}^{\text{OS}}, E_{\mu} (t), P_{\mu}^0\) Coordinate reflection space, heat semigroup and zero-energy projection; Section 14.1.
\(B_h (w)\) Bounded insertion of a word in a slab of width \(h\); Proposition 14.1.
\(J, T\) Conserved tensor and its normalization by the Ward identities; (14.11) and Proposition 14.6.
\(M (w), \mathfrak{A}\) Insertion operator for a word, compressed to the ground space, and the commutative algebra generated as the word varies; Section 15.2.
\(\Xi, \nu, S^{\xi}\) Character space, full-support representing measure and phase correlations; Section 15.3.
\(\xi_{\ast}\) Retained phase with a one-dimensional ground space; Section 15.6.
\(\mathcal{H}_{\xi}, \mathcal{D}_{\xi}, \Phi_{\xi}\) Reconstructed Hilbert space, common word domain and smeared fields; Section 16.4.
\(\textsf{F}_n^{\xi}, \mathcal{W}_n^{\xi}, W_n^{\xi}\) Ordered-time holomorphic function, full tube function and joint boundary distribution; (16.2), (16.7) and Proposition 16.1.
\(\Theta, G_{\Theta}, \rho_{\Theta}\) Centered trace, its Euclidean radial two-point function and positive spectral measure in the retained phase; Section 17.1.
\(E_j, Q_j, O_{j, a}\) Complement of the fields of lower scaling dimension in \(F_j\), projection onto this complement, and its chosen basis for the operator product expansion; (18.2).
\(c_{j, a} (w ; o)\) Coefficient for a cluster word \(w\) about center \(o\) in the fixed prescription; (18.3).
\(\mathfrak{D}_{\boldsymbol{A}}^{\xi}\) Distributional contact extension with specified normal Taylor subtraction; (18.10).
