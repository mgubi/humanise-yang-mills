\noindent\textbf{Status.} This is an incomplete draft of the existence argument.
Local results below have proofs from their displayed hypotheses. The construction
estimates and the quantitative inputs to the phase, symmetry and ultraviolet
arguments still require proofs before the final existence statement can be
asserted on the basis of this article. The conditional reconstruction is
developed below, including locality and the spectral condition. Outstanding
inputs are stated explicitly. All prose remains a draft pending independent
human mathematical and source-correspondence review.
