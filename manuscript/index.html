Mathematical manuscript

Read the manuscript (HTML version, converted from the LaTeX source)

LaTeX is the authoritative mathematical text from the first draft onward. Write sections here as soon as their local statements and input interfaces are precise. A completed blueprint and fully proved prerequisites are not required before writing. State outstanding inputs exactly and track their status in the linked blueprint unit; never conceal a missing argument.

main.tex assembles section files with \input. preamble.tex defines shared notation and theorem environments; references.bib holds conventional references. The running-coefficient pilot is the first section, owned by YM-000022. Its blueprint contains correspondence and review evidence, not a second version of the proof.

Build and inspect

From the repository root, with a TeX distribution providing latexmk and BibTeX: 

mkdir -p manuscript/build
cd manuscript
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error -outdir=build main.tex

The result is manuscript/build/main.pdf. Build products are ignored by Git. Inspect the rendered pages after changes; resolve missing references and layout warnings before submission. CI builds the manuscript as well as checking metadata.

Writing and review

Use ordinary mathematical language, conventional references and the shared notation contract. Give complete arguments for substantive steps not supplied by checked references. Check every cited result's hypotheses. Keep Lean identifiers, audit logs, and source crosswalks in the blueprint.

Create a section from templates/proof-unit/section.tex; place its unit.json and correspondence.md in the canonical blueprint directory. List the section and all local mathematical support files in unit.json's manuscript array, including nested inputs, macros and bibliography. Shared support-file edits invalidate every listed dependent unit's review fingerprint. Claim shared-file edits explicitly; adding a section also needs a bounded edit to main.tex.

Draft status is visible in the assembled manuscript; inclusion does not imply acceptance. Independent mathematical and Lean-correspondence reviews remain necessary. Integration checks notation, interfaces and the full chain of inputs; it improves the existing LaTeX rather than translating a separate Markdown proof.
