Literature provenance review: separate evidence pin

Scott Armstrong's later provenance review at external commit 7cebbf05447136e0a61ac9c0417a181f4df06ebf compares mechanisms in the same imported Lean revision, 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464, with Bałaban's gauge/RG papers, OpenAI's 2026 nonlinear-sigma-model preprints, and other literature. Scott describes the analysis as AI-written, limited to antecedents among the sources read, and unreviewed by the authors of the works discussed. Its classifications and novelty suggestions are therefore literature leads, not accepted theorem matches, proof verification, or priority findings here. This later review is pinned independently of the earlier source-cluster map above; the cluster assignments have not been repinned or re-audited by adding this link.

Promote a lead to a reusable result only through a focused background note that checks the original statement, hypotheses, definitions, and conventions against fully qualified declarations at the pinned Lean revision. The proposed Balaban gauge-averaging task covers the patch-frame interface in III.1; the existing Osterwalder–Schrader literature task covers reconstruction in V.2. Any other candidate requires its own scoped task and review before a proof unit cites it as an applicable result. Credit Scott's review for identifying or mapping a lead; the monograph should cite the original mathematical source for a result it uses.

Gaps before assigning canonical units

  • III.4: Which exact theorem passes from the Gaussian-sector change and determinant normalization to the window/jet estimates consumed by III.10? Check the covariance/Hessian identification flagged in the summary before fixing a split.

  • III.10: Which norm, parameter order and reset conditions are output by the nonlinear step, and which are re-established by III.9 or III.2? Determine whether PU-08–PU-10 can own disjoint mathematical statements.

  • III.11: Where does the determinant budget end and the free-field rank/stress comparison begin? Identify the handoff to VI.1 and avoid double ownership in PU-11/PU-12/PU-20.

  • III.13 versus III.14: The former constructs an alternative family for habitat; the latter selects the public run. Do not use III.13's common-family properties for YangMills.constructed without an explicit bridge. The old PU-14 proposal has been folded into PU-15 for the public family.

  • VII.1/VII.3: The source allocation puts free Maxwell detection and reflected Gram positivity here. The interacting comparison and nonfree conclusion cross VI.2, VII.2 and VIII.1; assign those mathematical interfaces rather than repeating their declarations.

  • VII.5/VII.6: Distinguish the bare/effective running-coupling law used in the public regularity clause from the separate comparison conclusion; place the scale-tuning Sky argument on the main spine, while independently checking its hypotheses and remaining outline limits.

  • Release-root coverage: CP-01, CP-02 and CP-04 target additional or alternative constructions in the broader source allocation. Their exact relation to YangMills.jointExistence remains a separate statement audit.
