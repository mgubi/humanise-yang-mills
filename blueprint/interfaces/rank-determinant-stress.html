Summary of findings

  1. The determinant budget is a stable, separate result that precedes the rank comparison. YM.Next.Rank.determinant and YM.Next.Rank.Station.affordable reference none of the other candidate theorems of this cluster, nor the nonlinear step or the Gaussian sector [G]; the rank comparison reaches both of them [G]. PU-12 can be written first and cited by PU-11.

  2. Two different "determinants" occur, with different consumers. The budget of item 1 is a ratio of Haar integrals on a torus. The relative matrix determinant of #12's third package (Dyed.cardinal_spin, Port.book_price, RelativeLogdet.Priced.chains) is not referenced by Rank.determinant, by Station.affordable or by the rank theorem MergeProof.Accent.rated [G]. It enters only upstream of one hypothesis of the rank theorem, through InteractingRank.Soft.accepts_stock → Soft.soluble → Soft.shapers [G]. #12's open question "is there a proof edge from cardinal_spin or book_price to Rank.determinant" has the answer no in either direction [G].

  3. The rank comparison is one theorem with three carried hypotheses [E]: MergeProof.Accent.rated. Its conclusion is an extraction record, a Spec.Seed and Maxwell.Ranks. It does not reference the nonlinear step (Rain.glazing, Roost.Factor.worksNow) or Next.SectorChange.committed [G]; the RG history enters through the hypotheses.

  4. The stress theorems do not reference the rank theorem [G]. The handoff from rank to stress is a statement, not a proof dependency: Acme.emanate takes a Spec.Seed and uses only its field corrected : Cells.RankWidths, through Next.UVStress.cap_given : RankWidths → DimAbove, plus the seed's extraction fields [S]. Maxwell.Ranks itself is not consumed by the stress construction.

  5. Maxwell.Ranks is produced twice. Once by the rank theorem, and again from the stress output by YM.Climb.comparison : ActLaw → Maxwell.Ranks [E]. Which of the two the later chapters use is not settled here; it decides whether PU-11 must prove the equivariant isomorphism at all or only the dimension count.

  6. The handoff to VI.1 is the record YM.Org.UV [E]: a seed, the orientation law, the Ward record Climb.Pre, the action law Climb.ActLaw and the grid condition Org.Grill. Its direct VI.1 consumer is Org.Commission.picks [E]. No consumer of the stress statements in V.3 was found; see obstruction 4.

  7. III.11 as delimited by the external outline is not three packages but at least five. Besides the three in the issue title there are the free covariance estimates (III.11.2, III.11.3) and the production of trial systems from frequent good scales (III.11.4), each with its own statement. A split is proposed below.

Contract A — determinant budget (candidate PU-12)

Statements [E].

For a representation R : RG.Rep G n with R.Lawful σ A Λ δ w, a Haar budget O, 0 ≤ R.eval pointwise, 1 < n, 0 < g ≤ R.p.threshold, R.beta * g^2 = 1, a Wilson.prior-integrable F : Config G n → ℂ, 0 ≤ z, and the pointwise bound z * ‖F U‖ ≤ exp (R.exponent U + card(Site n) * X):

  • YM.Next.Rank.Station.affordable, YM/Next/RANK/StationMeter.lean:119. For a Haar budget O, levels A : RG.Levels G and simple G: eventually in the level m, for every period index p, every representation P on A.S.side m p, all g Λ δ X and k : ℕ with Station.Meets A m p P g Λ δ and X ≤ 1 + k, 

    protection O P (A.params m).paths Λ δ X g
    - 4 * G.lieDim * log g / card(Site (A.S.side m p))

Order of quantifiers. In determinant nothing is chosen: it is a bound for each fixed torus, representation and g. In affordable the level threshold depends on O and A only and is uniform in p, P, g, Λ, δ, X, k.

It reaches none of: SectorChange.committed, SectorAffine.assembly, Rain.glazing, Roost.Factor.worksNow, Fluctuations.grants, Target.issue, Dyed.cardinal_spin. affordable (87 theorems) likewise.

Consumers [G]. determinant has one: InteractingRank.Soft.denture (the same inequality under a Soft.Tissue hypothesis [E]), which is reached by the rank theorem along Accent.rated → Soft.wheeled → Soft.v_obtain_purchases → Soft.coupon → Soft.s_postage → Soft.denture. affordable has one: Rank.Ship.banking, used by v_obtain_purchases.

Recommendation: separate unit, before PU-11. One coherent argument (tree gauge, a triangular choice of plaquettes for the numerator, Haar shells for the vacuum, cancellation of powers of g), a short statement and a single consumer. The mathematical description of the proof is taken from the external outline and was not checked against the proof bodies.
