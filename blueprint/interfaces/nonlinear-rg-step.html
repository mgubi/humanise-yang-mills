  3. The proposed PU-08/PU-09/PU-10 split survives, with the dependency order of PU-08 and PU-09 reversed relative to unit-plan.md.   4. PU-09 is not one theorem. It is four separately stated estimates with different quantifier orders [S]; a two-unit split is recommended below.

  5. Part of III.10 is not in the step-program closure at all: the integrated molecule estimate of III.10.3, the schedule statements of III.10.5 and the marker statements I01, I03, I04 have other consumers [G].

  6. The closure is conditional on a purchased schedule. The hypothesis ThroughLaunch.PaidView of Roof.timberT and Roost.commerce already contains the scheduled energy bound Pressure.Testing.Ringing, the Gaussian run hypothesis Next.SectorChange.Cosmos, Stock.Priced and the start-up prices [E].   7. The hypothesis bundle is the one-step estimates evaluated along the schedule. Every analytic field of Roof.Sheltered and Roof.WorksIn that was unfolded (seven rows of the field table were not) has the form "for each admissible stage pair and every coupling above the entry coupling, a one-step statement holds" [E]. These are not all consequences of the four Contract 1 statements: the fields pipes, peak, jewels and most of WorksIn are produced from further Cash-conditional one-step theorems that Contract 1 does not list [S]; see the producer table in obstruction 1.
