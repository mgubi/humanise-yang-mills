Proposed boundary

Recommend three disjoint candidate proof units, subject to independent correspondence review, following mathematical handoffs rather than file size:

  1. Sector change: the exact Haar-to-Gaussian identity for arbitrary permitted positive covariance and measurable shift; this owns the change of variables, all masks and normalisation in YM.Next.SectorChange.authorized_change.

  2. Window and jet input: selected Gram, windowed covariance and shift; uniform invertibility, approximation and finite-order field-jet estimates. It supplies a permitted pair and quantitative bounds to the first and third packages. It does not own the exact change-of-measure proof.

  3. Local relative determinant: Priced.chains and the finite-sector normalization handoff through Dyed.cardinal_spin and Port.book_price, conditional on their stated certificates. This owns the logdet series and sector identity, but not part 1’s Gaussian integral or part 2’s window bounds. The subsequent per-site budget and rank comparison belong to #14; the polymer activity construction belongs to #13 and PU-04. The precise border at book_price is a proposed handoff requiring agreement with #14.

This ordering describes proposed mathematical use, not an assertion that Lean imports or all implementation dependencies are acyclic. The integrated committed step combines 1 and 2 and should be owned as an application, rather than copied as a second proof. The jet theorem is bundled with the window estimates because both are contracts for the same selected matrices and its proof invokes the window positivity result. The local determinant and later determinant budget are separated at the identity for the actual sector; #14 must confirm that this handoff is sufficient. No proof-unit paths or declarations are reserved here.

Exact sector identity

In YM/SectorNext/GAUSSIAN_SECTOR_CHANGE/Shell.lean, YM.Next.SectorChange.chill freezes a free forest link if a parent endpoint lies in the store's 10*radiusB x dome; Frozen and Active partition free links. Standing requires, on nonzero allocation bill and eligible likelihood support, that each active link is in the specified exponential chart with norm at most d. Frozen variables are in LinkArray G (Frozen A x F), integrated against product Haar measure; active coordinates are Prices G (Active A x F), indexed by active links and the gauge colour index. The source fixes the sector, forest, field and tag.

In Change.lean, YM.Next.SectorChange.Permitted requires C.PosDef, β>0, a measurable frozen-field-dependent shift m, Standing, LocalRatio J, d>0 and 4*d < J.radius. YM.Precision.LocalRatio (YM/PCalc/Loop.lean) further specifies positive scale and radius, injectivity of the Lie exponential on the radius ball, positive analytic Jacobian on that ball with value one at zero, global measurability, openness of the chart image, and the exact push-forward of (J.scale * J.J) da restricted to the ball to probability Haar restricted to its image. YM.RG.Rep.Lawful (YM/RG/History.lean) consists of path bounds and activity validity; YM.Pressure.Teller.Funds (YM/Pressure/Seeds.lean) requires matching kernel and stage, the branch condition, the deep metric bound, matching clips, two path bounds, and attached compatible towers. Their nested mathematical hypotheses are source-defined inputs to this conditional theorem; this audit does not purport to prove or fully unfold them. In addition YM.Next.SectorChange.authorized_change requires R.Lawful σ n Λ δ l and P.Funds S T. For every such permitted pair, Licenses asserts integrability of the original sector integrand on the forest slice, every transformed section, and the joint transformed integrand, as well as

\(\displaystyle \int_{F. \text{Slice}} \Phi \hspace{0.17em} d (F. \text{order} \hspace{0.17em} G) = \text{prefactor} \hspace{0.17em} J. \text{scale}^{| \text{Active} |} \text{chartCost} (C, g) \int d \text{Haar}^{\text{Frozen}} (Y) \int \text{dyed} (Y, z) \hspace{0.17em} d \text{cloud} (C) (z), \quad g = (\sqrt{R. \beta})^{- 1} .\)

Here chartCost in Counting.lean is (sqrt (2*pi*g^2))^card(Active × G.ColorIndex) * sqrt(det C); cloud C is the push-forward of the library's multivariateGaussian 0 C from Euclidean coordinates to the finite coordinate-function space. For positive C, YM.Next.SectorChange.Gaussian.cloud_center, .cloud_cov, and .cloud_formula (YM/SectorNext/GAUSSIAN_SECTOR_CHANGE/Gaussian.lean) give zero mean, covariance C, and Lebesgue density (2π)^(-N/2) (det C)^(-1/2) exp(-zᵀC⁻¹z/2), with N the number of active colour coordinates. dyed retains chart Jacobians, cutoff doors, allocation bill, likelihood, exposed density, and the exponential of the actual residual and correction, evaluated at a=m(Y)+g z. It is not an identity after discarding those factors. recolored identifies the whole transformed multiplier with the regularized original integrand times the inverse Gaussian density factor. The general theorem does not require C to be the inverse Gram or the shift to be a minimizer.

YM.Next.SectorChange.committed (Handoff.lean) applies this to a directed run: under Ringing, Cosmos, m<N, Lives, and the representation's beta-band hypothesis it supplies positive chart parameters, the sector licences and the sum of sector integrals for the pushed density. For matching flags it additionally asserts positivity of the selected covariance and Gram; wrong flags are handled separately by displaced_wrong, not by asserting positivity for them. Exact expansion of the run and funding structures is still required for an independent review of this applied interface.

Window and field-jet contract

YM.Next.SectorChange.Rows.gram (Remainder.lean) is moveᵀ * move on Active × G.ColorIndex; Rows.guide is half the square of move*a + source, and Rows.blame = moveᵀ * source. In Chosen.lean, fog is Schur.clouded panels gram, while meanLog = -(per panels gram)ᵀ * blame. The columnwise per uses the inverse of each principal window and zero extension; clouded is half of per + perᵀ. The panels use both endpoints with radius radiusB x / 20. The source explicitly warns that a different midpoint window prescription is not automatically equal to this one.

YM.Next.SectorChange.Schur.windows (Windows.lean) states: for σ,η>0, K≥0 and nonnegative B, there are positive v,C,g such that for all sufficiently large natural radii r, all finite coordinate sets mapped to a finite torus, all symmetric matrices Q with gap η, weighted row-and-column Schur bound K at rate σ, the Revenue uniform local counting bound B, and windows Bars containing the radius-r balls, the symmetrized windowed inverse Cw has

\(\displaystyle \|C_w - Q^{- 1} \|_{\text{Schur}} \leqslant Ce^{- vr}, \qquad C_w \geqslant gI, \qquad C_w \text{ positive definite} .\)

The statement is uniform in the torus and finite coordinate set, not valid at arbitrary window sizes. YM.Next.SectorChange.opaqueCost (Chosen.lean) applies this to the selected Gram: fix gauge, patch and teller with branch condition; for sufficiently large coarse parameter x, a step with the same patch, 100*(1+radiusB x) ≤ c.val, a funded tube, arbitrary store, forest, tag and coarse configuration with matching flags, has uniformly decaying and gapped Gram and a positive fog, with unweighted Schur error bounded by E * exp(-b * (radiusB x / 60)). Constants precede the choice of volume and sector.

YM.AnalyticBridge.SectorAffine.assembly (YM/AnalyticBridge/SECTOR_AFFINE_JETS/Covariance.lean) fixes gauge, patch, branching teller and arbitrary finite jet order n, then chooses positive constants and a sufficiently large x. Under the same size, funding and matching-flag conditions, for each fixed store, forest, coarse field, tag and perturbation B with ‖B‖≤r, it supplies KernelJets. This record includes analytic move, weighted row/column Polar bounds for move, Gram and covariance, positive gaps, and principal, global and windowed resolvent jets for all u≥0. Its deficit and window-minus-full-resolvent jet bounds have factors M exp(-v radiusB x)/(1+u) and M exp(-v radiusB x)/(1+u)^2, respectively. For every list of coordinate directions of length at most n, Polar requires the corresponding iterated derivative to satisfy both weighted row and column Bill bounds; the row bound multiplies the weighted absolute row sum by the product of derivative-position marks, and the column bound applies the same construction to the transpose. ResolventJets uses the same bills with cap M/(1+u) at derivative order zero and M/(1+u)^2 at positive orders. The position metric is coarse-torus separation; exact coordinate conventions are the definitions Polar, Bundle, Mark.Bill, marks, and bolt in the source. For a coordinate direction t, coordRoot t is its coarse site, bolt t is its coordinate basis direction, and its mark at site a is exp(d * separation(a, coordRoot t)). Thus for a list l, the row weight is exp(d * sum_{t in l} separation(a(i),coordRoot t)) times the weighted row sum with kernel factor exp(d * separation(a(i),a(j))); the column bound is the corresponding bound for the transpose. These are iterated Fréchet coordinate derivatives in the left chart, not Lie derivatives. Store, flags, forest, tags, scale and tube are held fixed while the unmasked field-dependent functions vary; this does not assert differentiability of masks or sector decisions.

Hessian caution. The selected fog is generally a symmetrized windowed inverse of the Gram, not definitionally the full inverse. YM.AnalyticBridge. GaussianFieldAlignment.Agreements.exact (YM/AnalyticBridge/GAUSSIAN_FIELD_ALIGNMENT/Agreements.lean) takes Q = Rows.gram T x V k (shots S x) A F and requires all three hypotheses: Q.PosDef, 120 ≤ radiusB x, and, for every i,j, Q⁻¹ i j = 0 whenever i is outside the intersection panels A F j ∩ mids A F x j. It then concludes all three identities fog A F T x V k = Q⁻¹, middle A F x T V k = Q⁻¹, and meanLog A F T x V k = middleLog A F x T V k. These are sufficient conditions; the source does not assert they hold generally. The inspected Rows.guide identifies the Gram as the quadratic coefficient of the selected affine-square guide. It does not establish that this is the Hessian of the full effective action at its background. The latter identification remains open; the external summary also flags it as such.

Determinants and consumer handoffs

YM.AnalyticBridge.RelativeLogdet.Priced (YM/AnalyticBridge/RELATIVE_LOGDET_TRACE/Walls.lean) is a full hypothesis bundle for a finite index set, cost D, position map p, window family W, matrix Q, weighted rate v, bound C, and error q. It requires each index to lie in its own window, Q.PosDef, 1 ≤ C, v ≥ 0, q ≥ 0, and the weighted row-and-column D.Schur bounds: Q at cost C; for every u ≥ 0, the full resolvent (Q+uI)⁻¹ and the columnwise windowed inverse at cost C/(1+u), and their defect (Q+uI)*pie(W,Q,u)-I at cost q/(1+u); and S = Q*Cw-I at cost q, where Cw=Schur.clouded W Q. YM.AnalyticBridge.RelativeLogdet.Priced.chains (Series.lean) additionally requires q<1 and Cw.PosDef. It states separately, with the source's local terms letterBase, r and Round.ripple,

\(\displaystyle \begin{aligned} \log \det Q & = \sum_i \left( \text{letterBase}_i + \sum_{n \geqslant 0} r_{i, n} \right),\\ \log \det C_w & = - \log \det Q + \sum_i \sum_{n \geqslant 0} \text{ripple} (S)_{n, i, i},\\ \log \det (I + S) & = \sum_i \sum_{n \geqslant 0} \text{ripple} (S)_{n, i, i} . \end{aligned}\)

The theorem also asserts summability of both series for each i; by definition I+S=Q*Cw, which need not be symmetric. This is a relative/localisation result, distinct from chartCost. The exact handoff from these hypotheses to the selected sector, including all normalisation signs and PU-11 overlap, is not yet checked.

No blanket classification of uninspected helpers is made; any further substantive content remains open.

Literature and remaining obligations

No literature theorem has been verified as a replacement for any complete output. A bounded search inspected Demko, Moss and Smith, “Decay Rates for Inverses of Band Matrices,” Mathematics of Computation 43 (1984), 491–499, Theorem 2.4. It gives inverse-entry decay for positive definite band matrices; its one-dimensional band hypotheses do not directly supply the torus-metric, exponentially decaying matrix, uniform counting, window error, positivity and jet contract above. The finite-dimensional Gaussian density is a natural literature lead for part 1, but a precise source and its coordinate, Haar chart and integrability hypotheses have not been verified.
