This ledger audits the 17 advertised theorem declarations of the imported OpenAI-YM source at revision 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464. It is a statement and interpretation audit, not a proof of their mathematical content. The corresponding source-level signatures and check evidence are in source/audit.md. All source paths below are relative to the repository root. The public theorem is YangMills.jointExistence; the other release headlines have distinct scopes and must not be read as extra conjuncts of that theorem.

Conventions and evidence

YangMills.GaugeGroup is a closed matrix subgroup in a specified finite unitary representation. Its IsSimple predicate refers to the associated real Lie algebra: it is nonabelian and has no proper Lie ideals. The internal YM.Gauge/Family notation is related by a bridge, but a theorem formulated in that notation does not thereby become a public Wilson-family theorem. All spacetime coordinates in the public challenge are four-dimensional; the time coordinate is distinguished. These are definitions of this formal source, not additional assumptions about every gauge theory.

The accepted issue #2 axiom evidence reports propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound for every root, with no nonstandard endpoint axiom. It used a checksum-verified compiled artifact and the pinned Lean 4.32.0 kernel; it was not a clean build from source on this host. Those facts concern proof terms, not the physical interpretation of a statement.

Assumption layers. The theorem hypotheses are the displayed simplicity, positivity, geometry, filter, construction, and lower-grade conditions in the entries below. Existential witnesses such as a family, hierarchy, refinement, table, reader, or scaling are constructed data, not hypotheses silently imposed on all gauge theories. The formal proof environment uses the three reported Lean axioms and pinned Mathlib; neither the public theorem nor this ledger adds an external physical axiom such as a mass gap. No literature result is used to replace inspection of these formal definitions.

Public and assembly roots

YangMills.jointExistence

Source: Lean/Solution.lean:4, with the statement JointExistence in Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean:186–188. Input: every g : GaugeGroup with g.IsSimple. Conclusion: there is one C : WilsonFamily g satisfying WilsonRegularity C. For every strictly increasing sequence p : ℕ → ℕ of volumes, there is a strictly increasing q : ℕ → ℕ and a T : Hierarchy C such that T.Properties (p ∘ q) holds. The order of these quantifiers permits the hierarchy and subsequence to depend on p, while the Wilson family is chosen before p.

The meanings of WilsonRegularity and Hierarchy.Properties are fixed by Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean and the public Wilson and theory definitions. The property record comprises Wilson convergence, a selected reconstructed theory, an all-orders separated operator-product expansion, and a short-distance nonfreeness test for a centered grade-four field. The reconstruction's spectral condition is positive energy and uniqueness of the invariant vacuum. Neither a positive mass gap nor uniqueness of the continuum limit appears as a conclusion. This is the principal public statement; the detailed construction below supplies its witnesses.

More precisely, regularity supplies one positive running slope such that the ratio of the bare action coefficient to log(mesh⁻¹) approaches it uniformly over the volume index; continuity and gauge invariance of every insertion; and, for each insertion token, one finite lattice-offset stencil that controls its values at every spacing, volume and placement (ChallengeSpec.lean:11–22). C.Converges first takes mesh limits of smeared finite Wilson moments on suitable volume rows, then a volume limit to the unselected hierarchy correlation (PublicWilson.lean:75–90). The four fields of Hierarchy.Properties are exactly wilson, reconstruction, ope, and nonfree (ChallengeSpec.lean:177–182); contacts are not a fifth field.

The reconstruction field includes faithful tempered fields on a common dense cyclic domain, nontriviality and label representation, a probability measure with full support on characters, permutation symmetry and smoothness of separated correlations, continuity in the character, and a phase-mixture identity. Its selected Wightman theory satisfies normalized vacuum, positive-energy spectrum in the closed forward cone, uniqueness of the translation-invariant vacuum up to scalar, Poincaré covariance, and spacelike locality. Analytic continuation on ordered complex times has the stated Euclidean root and Wightman boundary values on product Schwartz tests (ChallengeSpec.lean:117–143; PublicTheory.lean:252–312). These conditions do not require a spectral interval above the vacuum to be empty.

The OPE has one phase-independent coefficient system. For fixed labels, geometry constants, truncation degree D, and spectator separation, the remainder is bounded by r^(D+1-weight(a)-ε) at all sufficiently small positive r, uniformly over admissible positions and characters. Coefficient bounds are stated separately for each degree and channel. Within requires cluster points within Mr of the center and separated by at least ηr; Clear requires spectators separated from the center and from each other by at least c (ChallengeSpec.lean:53–80). Coefficient-bound thresholds may depend on degree and channel; a fixed-truncation remainder threshold is uniform over the channels in its finite sum but may depend on that truncation and the labels. No threshold uniform over all degrees, truncations, or label choices is asserted.

The trace test centers a grade-four field and gives the selected two-point function along the time axis the positive limit r⁸ log²(r⁻¹) traceTwo(r) → 3 dimℝ(g.lieAlgebra)/π⁴ as r ↓ 0. It excludes eventual equality near zero with every finite sum of the specified mass-shell Wick profiles (ChallengeSpec.lean:145–175). The README's physical trace identification and Maxwell Fock countertests refer to the detailed construction statement, beyond this four-field public property record.

For every internal extraction order p, a refinement q gives a table S, final object F, all-phase reconstruction L, OPE reader Q, contact devices M, and scaling X, simultaneously satisfying the projected public hierarchy properties, ordered extraction, full-volume position conditions, calibration, the internal independent core, reconstruction, separated and nested OPE, trace exclusions, contact/basis conditions, and the first extension and four position-meter tests.

Lean/WilsonBridge.lean:10–17 turns the public matrix group into the internal gauge structure. The extra contact and position conditions belong to this detailed result and its selected witnesses. They are not additional conjuncts in YangMills.jointExistence. The statement gives an extension under the constructed contact prescription; it does not claim uniqueness across prescriptions or raw cutoff convergence at collisions.

YM.Independent.Core.point

Source: Lean/YM/Independent/Core.lean:49–63. Inputs: an internal Core for a final object, almanac, and OPE reader; finite sets of clustered and spectator labels, nonnegative cluster-size constant M, positive room, separation, and loss constants η,c,ε, and degree D. Conclusion: for all sufficiently small positive radii r, uniformly over clustered positions x in WithinRoom r M η about a center o, spectator positions y in ClearOf o y c, and viewers s, the norm of the whole correlation minus the finite OPE sum over degrees j ≤ D and reader channels is at most r^(D+1-weight(d)-ε). The sum uses the same table's spectator correlation with one field inserted at o. Lean/YM/OPE/Orbits/Profile.lean defines the geometry and exponent; Lean/YM/OPE/Orbits/Words.lean defines the label words. The theorem extracts a consequence of an assumed Core, rather than constructing a public hierarchy. A pinned constant-closure query finds this declaration reachable from jointExistence's type or proof; its pointwise estimate is still not a separate conjunct of the public Properties record.

YM.Contacts.Solstice.celestial

Source: Lean/YM/Contacts/Solstice/Result.lean:78–79. Input: an internal Gauge G with G.Simple. Conclusion: there exist an internal family, extraction order, table, final object, and contact devices satisfying MultipleLight, with a nonempty transport system basis and nonempty threshold package. MultipleLight is a record of services, a rotation identity, and conditional slope, moment, and distributional estimates under its own finite-dimensional Euclidean and contact-geometry hypotheses (Result.lean:52–63). These conditions are not a claim of uniqueness of contact extensions, reflection positivity on overlapping supports, arbitrary contact Ward identities, or real-time time-ordered products. This is a separately advertised existence statement; the issue #3 dependency audit does not identify it as a premise of jointExistence.

The conditional clauses quantify over finite-dimensional real inner-product test spaces equipped with the specified Haar/Lebesgue measure structure. The slope field requires a rooted normal-group route, a nonempty group, Run, Given, route authority, and an eligible backdrop, and concludes the named Sunshine estimate. The moment field concludes Resolution for a route and eligible backdrop under Given and authority. The distribution field concludes a Charge bound for the major kernel when the contact plane lies in the test-space range, the configuration is collapsed, and 0 < B ≤ 1, B is below the device clearance, and 0 < s with 2s ≤ B. These are the immediate formal conditions of MultipleLight; they should not be replaced with an unrestricted contact-distribution claim.

Sunshine supplies power-law Claim estimates for the backdrop, each root coefficient, and a remainder; for configurations with more than one point it also supplies conditional annulus estimates (Result.lean:33–40). Resolution packages an Achieves bound, a finite-part/series formula for the route integral, and integrability, absolute summability and HasSum statements for the selected and centered contact expansions (Result.lean:23–31). Charge is a compact-local test-order bound for the major-kernel distribution: on each compact test support, a constant controls the distribution by the prescribed jet order (Solstice/Venue.lean:30–31). Thus the existential root has contact estimates for this constructed prescription under the displayed geometry and eligibility conditions, not an arbitrary contact theory.

YM.SharpGram.habitat

Source: Lean/YM/SharpGram/Measured.lean:11. Inputs: internal G.Simple, a positive penalty b, and an extraction order f. Conclusion: there exist an internal family, refinement, table, final object, every-phase almanac, and prescription with the stipulated construction, calibration, penalty, UV, order, margin, facility, logarithmic, completion, all-shapes, and dense-coverage conditions. Its final quantified clause runs over every ultrafilter below the fused extraction filter and every horizon D: a weak portfolio exists, and each such portfolio has priced spin-change data whose basis readings converge grade by grade to the model. The full conjunction is transcribed in the source audit. This is a fixed-field and weak portfolio habitat; the statement does not assert a Gram matrix is positive definite in every physical regime or establish a mass gap.

Here Floral is a structured ultraviolet extension/cellar package with analytic arc, spring, geometric and eventual realization clauses (UVExtension/Oasis/Flora.lean:55–72); its many nested predicates are not collapsed to a standard continuum-limit theorem. Facilities combines a same-table OPE service package with a logarithmic package (OPE/ElementaryLog.lean:12–13); AllShapes adds coherent one-family, nested-tree, and discounted two-family subpower estimates (OPE/Subpower.lean:37–45). Dense.Coverage chooses a common predecessor schedule and panels with eventual Poised data at every horizon (SharpGram/Dense/Passes.lean:16–24). A weak Portfolio carries binding, budget, chart and panel data, a late predecessor, matrix/field sequences and subfilter limits (SharpGram/Weak/Reading.lean:35–49); its nonemptiness is not the existence of a standalone probability measure.

YM.SharpGram.inletFirst

Source: Lean/YM/SharpGram/Clauses.lean:126. Inputs: an internal simple gauge group, a family and final object, a filter φ on ℕ × ℕ below the extraction order's fused filter, and a label x of grade d ≤ 3, and the remaining section data. Conclusion: the projected field S.project d x satisfies Supplies C φ d. Precisely, an exponent p can be chosen so that, for every sufficiently large k, there are a label y projecting to that field and a label net q tending to y along φ, whose quota is eventually bounded by hatBeta(k)^p (Clauses.lean:18–21). The statement concerns supply for grades through three under these hypotheses; it makes no higher-grade or public-hierarchy claim.

YM.SharpGram.outletFirst

Source: Lean/YM/SharpGram/Clauses.lean:120. Inputs: the same internal simple-group, family, final/table/ticket and filter context; a complex-linear functional L on grade d; a lower grade w ≤ d with w ≤ 3; and Kills on all grades below w. Conclusion: Releases C φ d w L. An exponent p exists such that, for every sufficiently large k, every nonbottom subfilter u ≤ φ, convergent label net q → x along u, and eventual quota bound z, the value of L on the projected field is bounded by hatBeta(k)^p z / a(k)^(d-w) (Clauses.lean:23–29). The order of the eventual and universal quantifiers matters. It is an estimate conditional on annihilation of lower grades, not a general regularity theorem.

Interpretation limits and public prose

The upstream Lean/README.md describes Wilson origin, reconstructed Wightman properties, separated OPE, and a nonfreeness test for the public challenge. Those descriptions must be read with the actual predicates in ChallengeSpec.lean, PublicWilson.lean, and PublicTheory.lean; they do not add hypotheses or conclusions to JointExistence. The README explicitly excludes a positive mass gap, general exclusion of continuous-mass generalized free fields, and all-orders MS-bar matching. It also says the constructed contact prescription is an extension, without uniqueness over other prescriptions or raw cutoff convergence on collision diagonals.

The release-headline list alone does not say which focused results occur in the proof term of YangMills.jointExistence. The direct source chain inspected here is continuum_with_position_tests → existence → constructed → jointExistence; continuum is a projection of the first, and su2Theory specializes the last. A pinned type/proof constant-closure query additionally finds YM.Independent.Core.point and YM.Pert.Position.finite_leading reachable from jointExistence; the other focused headline names are not in that closure. Reachability records a formal dependency, not a public conjunct or a physical interpretation. The contact, parity, asymptotics and Sharp-Gram headlines remain separately advertised statements.
