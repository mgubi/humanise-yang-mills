Findings and evidence

I checked every displayed step of the chapter. In order:

  1. Free normalisation (lines 1–96).

    • The three evaluation rows: \((0, 2, 0)\) for the jet with \(F_{12} = 1\); \((1, 2, 0)\) for \(F_{01} = 1\), \(\partial_0 F_{01} = 1\), \(\partial_2 F_{21} = - 1\), where \(J^{(3)}_0 = \partial_0 F_{01} + \partial_2 F_{21} = 0\); \((1, 2, 1)\) for the last jet. The determinant is \(- 2 \neq 0\).

    • The Bianchi check of the first jet: \(1 - \tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2} = 0\). The other two jets have only repeated-index derivative entries, so the cyclic identity holds trivially.

    • The radial-gauge potential and \(\partial_c (\partial_a A_b - \partial_b A_a) (0) = \tfrac{1}{3} (2 K_{cab} - K_{abc} - K_{bca}) = K_{cab}\).

    • The divergence computation, including \(B = J^{(2)} - B\) via the Bianchi identity and the exchange \(a \leftrightarrow i\), giving coordinates \(2 (w_0 - w_2), \hspace{0.17em} 2 w_1 + \tfrac{1}{2} w_2, \hspace{0.17em} w_2\).

    • Trace \((w_0 + 4 w_1) \mathcal{I}\), hence \(\rho = - 4 (w_0 + 4 w_1)\), \(J^{(3)} \mapsto (1, - \tfrac{1}{4}, 1)\) and \(\rho (x) = 0\).

    All correct.

  2. Clock ratio (lines 97–138). The minimal-overshoot bound \(0 \leqslant \ell_{q (k)} - \ell_k \leqslant 2 g\) uses \(\hat{\beta}_m \leqslant \chi + \gamma g \leqslant 2 \chi\) and \(\log L_m \leqslant L_m \leqslant \log \hat{\beta}_m \leqslant \log 2 \chi \leqslant g\). From it follow \(\hat{\beta}_{q (k)} / \hat{\beta}_k \to 1\) and, with \(|b_j - \hat{\beta}_j | \leqslant \varepsilon \ell_j \leqslant \varepsilon \hat{\beta}_j / \gamma\), the limit \(b_{k_i} / b_{n_i} \to 1\). Correct.

  3. Chart, right inverse and parity (lines 139–220). \(s_i u_i = \text{id}\) follows from \(\text{core} M_i Y_i h = M_i Y_i h\) and \(PY_i = X_i\). The parity argument for the vanishing unit coefficient (total inversion acts by \(- 1\) on lifted vector components and fixes \(e_0\)) is correct. So is the construction of \(U\): the \(\Gamma\)-averaged Hermitian form makes the signed action unitary and conjugation antiunitary, and \(K\) is real and stable, so the orthogonal-complement section intertwines both.

  4. Tested map and centring (lines 255–288). Injectivity of \(\textsf{T}\) from the first bound. Recentring with \(v^{\circ} = v - (\textsf{T} v)_0 \boldsymbol{1}\) gives \(\textsf{T} v^{\circ} = \text{core} \textsf{T} v\), \(\text{core} \textsf{A} v^{\circ} = \text{core} \textsf{A} v\), \(| \textsf{A} v^{\circ} |_1 \leqslant 2 | \textsf{T} v^{\circ} |_1\), and hence eq:trace-sensor-centered-price with constant \(2 \eta_i\). Correct (see R3 for one wording point).

  5. Calibration (lines 290–339). Every nonunit coordinate of an odd two-factor label has position degree 5 and factor degree 2, because jet factors have weight \(r + 2\) and sign \((- 1)^{r + 2}\). Then \(\textsf{T} \lambda_i = \text{Dil}_{\xi^{- 1}} \text{Pos}_{\sigma_i} p = \xi^{- 2} \sigma_i^5 p\) and \(\xi^{- 2} = \beta_i \vartheta_{k_i}^2\). Multiplying by \(\vartheta_{k_i}^{- 2} d_{k_i}^5\) gives exactly eq:trace-sensor-finite-price, using \(r_i = d_{k_i} \sigma_i\). Correct. The vanishing of one-factor invariants is proved in trace-free-rank.tex, but the chapter refers to it only as "above" (R2).

  6. Transport price (lines 341–466).

    • Backward induction: \(|z_j - z_{n_i} |_1 \leqslant a (n_i - j) |z_{n_i} |_1\) with \(a = \delta_i / N_i\) and \(aN_i \leqslant 1\).

    • Grade scaling: Block factor 1 and dilation factor \((\vartheta_j / \vartheta_{j + 1})^2\) on the nonunit coordinates. Applying the core and dividing gives \(|z_j - z_{j + 1} |_1 \leqslant 4 g_j |z_{j + 1} |_1\).

    • Budget comparison: \(N_i \leqslant 2 (\log \chi)^{48}\), \(C_{j + 1} \leqslant 2 \log \chi\), \(A_{1 / 2} (j + 1) \geqslant A_{1 / 2} (k_i)\), and \(32 (\log \chi)^{48} (1 + 2 \log \chi) \leqslant A_{1 / 10} (k_i)\) eventually.

    All correct. The quantifier handling is also correct: finite intersection over \(k_i \leqslant j < n_i\) for fixed \(i\), then the ultrafilter limit, then \(i \to \infty\).

  7. \(u_i \to U\) (lines 456–475). I checked the identity \((u_i h)_{\alpha} = (b_{k_i} / b_{n_i}) \textsf{R}_i\) from \(c_i = b_{n_i} r_i^5\) and \(D_i = b_{k_i} r_i^5\). Required changes and remaining uncertainty

  • R1 (required, presentation). The proposition's hypotheses are given by reference to prose: "the tested-map, selected-lift and finite-volume normalization inputs described around (…)–(…)" and "the stated oddness, joint convergence and column-budget inputs". In the text, the inputs are introduced by "Assume" or "Suppose" inside paragraphs that also derive consequences. A reader cannot extract the exact hypothesis list, with quantifiers, without re-reading the whole chapter. For a research monograph, state the inputs as a numbered list immediately before the proposition (for example (S1) clock and block-size inputs; (S2) chart formulas and identities with their tails; (S3) symmetry intertwining; (S4) joint transport and lift convergence along the ultrafilter; (S5) tested-map bounds and calibration; (S6) one-step residual and threshold ratio, with the quantifier "for each fixed \(j\), eventually in \(\nu\), uniformly in \(N > j\) and the label"; (S7) column budget and oddness preservation). Make the derived intermediate results labelled lemmas: the arch ratio, the initial price, the transport price, and \(u_i \to U\). The proposition can then cite them. No mathematical change is needed.

  • R2 (nit; corrected 13 November 2026). Line 306: "A one-factor invariant vanishes by the invariant-linear-form argument in the low-grade label calculation above". The argument is in a support file of this unit: the "Low-grade invariant labels" subsection of trace-free-rank.tex (sec:ex-lattice-trace-free-rank), which proves that an \(\text{Ad}\)-invariant real linear functional on the simple algebra vanishes. The earlier version of this report wrongly said it was missing. The only request is to replace "above" by a reference to that section.

  • R3 (minor). Lines 266–280: \(\textsf{A}, \textsf{T}\) are called "complex-linear maps on the finite complex coefficient space", and then "the two finite-dimensional coefficient spaces have the same dimension" is used for invertibility. Say which spaces they are. If both maps are endomorphisms of one space, injectivity suffices and the sentence can go. If not, justify the equality of dimensions or make it part of the input.

  • R4 (nit). Lines 233–234: "Fix one ultrafilter on these pairs containing both coordinate tails." In the source the ultrafilter satisfies \(\mathcal{L} \leqslant F. \text{fuse}\) (hb in springs). Say that the inputs are taken along the selected ultrafilter, which refines the fused order, and that only its coordinate tails are used in the argument.

  • R5 (record). The wrapper evidence/wrapper.tex now builds to 19 pages with two undefined references (prop:ex-lattice-charge-bounds, prop:ex-trace-mission-price-transfer). Both were introduced into the support chapters by the C15 pass. They are not in this unit's chapter. Update the wrapper or its validation note before integration.
