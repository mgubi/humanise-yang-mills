Re-verification of the revised mathematics

I rechecked each derivation in the changed text.

  1. Lemma lem:trace-sensor-arch-ratio. Let \(\chi = \hat{\beta}_k\), \(g = (\log \chi)^{48}\), \(q (k) = m + 1\). Minimality gives \(\ell_m - \ell_k \leqslant g\). For large \(k\), \(\gamma g \leqslant \chi\), so \(\hat{\beta}_m \leqslant 2 \chi\). Then \(\log L_m \leqslant L_m \leqslant \log \hat{\beta}_m \leqslant \log 2 \chi \leqslant g\). So \(0 \leqslant \ell_{q (k)} - \ell_k \leqslant 2 g\), and \(\hat{\beta}_{q (k)} / \hat{\beta}_k \to 1\). With \(\hat{\beta}_j \geqslant \gamma \ell_j\), eq:trace-sensor-clock-input gives \(b_j / \hat{\beta}_j \to 1\), hence the claim. Correct.

  2. Lemma lem:trace-sensor-initial-price.

    • Centring: with \(c = (\textsf{T} v)_0\) and \(v^{\circ} = v - c\boldsymbol{1}\), \(\textsf{T} v^{\circ} = \textsf{T} v - ce_0 = \text{core} \textsf{T} v\) and \(\text{core} \textsf{A} v^{\circ} = \text{core} \textsf{A} v\). From \(| \textsf{A} v^{\circ} |_1 \leqslant \tfrac{1}{2} | \textsf{A} v^{\circ} |_1 + | \textsf{T} v^{\circ} |_1\) we get \(| \textsf{A} v^{\circ} |_1 \leqslant 2 | \text{core} \textsf{T} v|_1\). The second bound applied at \(v^{\circ}\), then \(\text{core}\), gives eq:trace-sensor-centered-price. Correct (O1).

    • Calibration: \(\text{Dil}_{\xi} \textsf{T} \lambda_i = \text{Pos}_{\sigma_i} p = \sigma_i^5 p\) because the nonzero nonunit coordinates of the odd \(p\) have position degree 5. \(\text{Dil}_{\xi^{- 1}}\) multiplies factor-degree-2 coordinates by \(\xi^{- 2} = \beta_i \vartheta_{k_i}^2\). So \(\text{core} \textsf{T} \lambda_i = \beta_i \vartheta_{k_i}^2 \sigma_i^5 p\). Multiplying the centred price by \(\vartheta_{k_i}^{- 2} d_{k_i}^5\) and using \(r_i = d_{k_i} \sigma_i\) gives \(|Z_i - \beta_i r_i^5 p|_1 \leqslant 2 \eta_i \beta_i r_i^5 |p|_1\), which is eq:trace-sensor-finite-price. Correct.

    • The degree classification (311–322) is the same argument as before, now with the R2 reference. Correct.

    • Limits: \(Z_i (\omega) \to \vartheta_{k_i}^{- 2} \text{core} M (k_i) y_i = W_{k_i} y_i\) by (S3) applied to \(v (\omega) = \lambda_i (\omega) \to y_i\); \(B_i (\omega) \to b_{k_i} r_i^5 = D_i\). The non-strict inequality passes to the ultrafilter limit. Correct, with the volume limit at fixed \(i\).

  3. Lemma lem:trace-sensor-transport-price.

    • Telescoping with \(a = \delta_i / N_i \leqslant 1 / N_i\): from \(|z_{j + 1} |_1 \leqslant 2 |z_{n_i} |_1\), the step bound \(\tfrac{\delta_i}{2 N_i} |z_{j + 1} |_1 \leqslant a |z_{n_i} |_1\). Correct.

    • Grade scaling eq:trace-sensor-grade-scaling: block factor \(L_j^0 = 1\), dilation factor \((\vartheta_j / \vartheta_{j + 1})^2\) on the nonunit coordinates of the odd \(\tilde{z}_{j + 1}\). Correct.

    • Budget: \(\hat{\beta}_{n_i} \leqslant 2 \chi\); \(\log L_j \geqslant \log 3 \geqslant 1\) gives \(N_i \leqslant 2 (\log \chi)^{48}\); \(C_{j + 1} \leqslant \log \hat{\beta}_{j + 1} \leqslant 2 \log \chi\); monotonicity gives \(A_{1 / 2} (j + 1) \geqslant A_{1 / 2} (k_i)\). Then \(2 N_i \cdot 4 g_j \leqslant 32 (\log \chi)^{48} (1 + 2 \log \chi) / A_{1 / 2} (k_i) \leqslant A_{- 2 / 5} (k_i) = \delta_i\), because a fixed power of \(\log \chi\) is eventually below \(A_{1 / 10} (k_i)\). Correct.

    • Quantifiers: the finite intersection over \(k_i \leqslant j < n_i\) with \(N \geqslant n_i\) is taken for fixed \(i\) before the ultrafilter limit. This matches (S5).

  4. Lemma lem:trace-sensor-lift-limit. \((u_i h)_{\alpha} = c_i^{- 1} W_{n_i} y_i = (b_{k_i} / b_{n_i}) \textsf{R}_i\). The bound \(| \textsf{R}_i - p|_1 \leqslant (2 \eta_i + \delta_i) (1 - \delta_i)^{- 1} |p|_1\) follows: \(\delta (1 + 2 \eta) / (1 - \delta) + 2 \eta = (2 \eta + \delta) / (1 - \delta)\). With lem:trace-sensor-arch-ratio, \(u_i h \to Uh\). Finite dimension gives operator-norm convergence. Correct.

  5. Proposition. The proof is unchanged in substance from the version reviewed in #126 (item 8 there), which I re-read. \(T_i = u_i f + A \otimes v_i \to \text{id}\), \(s_i T_i = f + A \otimes s_i (v_i)\), \(\|s_i - f\| \leqslant (\|A\|\|s_i (v_i)\|+\|f\| \epsilon_i) / (1 - \epsilon_i)\), then lem:ex-lattice-trace-weighted-price with \((s_i, u_i, v_i, \mathfrak{a}_i)\). The statement now has "for all sufficiently large \(i\), \(| \mu_i (h) | \leqslant C \|h\|\)" and one tail per \(\varepsilon\), uniform in \(y\). Correct.

Existing results. The tools remain the Neumann series (cited in correspondence.md to Winklmeier, Thm 2.10) and the rank-one receiver lemma proved in trace-weighted-estimates.tex. I know of no literature result that would shorten the chart and calibration bookkeeping.

Optional suggestions (not required)

  • O1 (clarity), lines 295–299. "Applying core to the second bound" should say "applied to \(v^{\circ}\)". At \(v\) itself the bound has \(| \textsf{A} v|_1\) on the right, which need not be at most \(2 | \text{core} \textsf{T} v|_1\).

  • O2 (presentation). The lemmas cite "(S1)–(S6) below" before the list appears (line 229). Two proofs also open with "Suppose …" for statements they derive later (248–256, 372–381). Moving the list before Lemma lem:trace-sensor-initial-price, and stating the finite-volume price and the one-step price as claims to be proved, would read more directly. The proposition statement also contains a proof sentence ("By Lemmas …, the stated chart has an eventual right inverse …"), which could move to the proof.

  • O3 (scaffolding), line 340. "On the selected tail, where \(i \geqslant 5\)" comes from the Lean indexing of the lift (heightV, with 5 < i+1; see correspondence.md line 33). In the monograph, "on the selected tail" suffices.

  • O4 (wording), lines 243–245. "Write \(\mathcal{F}_{\text{fuse}}\) for its fused-order filter" — "its" refers to the order, not the ultrafilter. Suggest: "Write \(\mathcal{F}_{\text{fuse}}\) for the fused-order filter on pairs; the selected ultrafilter satisfies \(\mathcal{L} \leqslant \mathcal{F}_{\text{fuse}}\), so it contains both coordinate tails."
