## Input contract and notation

The selected-chart input `eq:trace-sensor-chart` spells out the componentwise terminal identity and odd-vector core identity used in `YM.Trace.Terrace.lensP`, alongside its limiting section. The manuscript derives the array-level real covariance and the eventual right-inverse identity from intertwining of the selected lift and arch maps and the same odd-core cancellation as `lensP`. It also derives pointwise transported-lift convergence from the initial price and the derived transport estimate. Both are derived from separate finite-volume receipts and their ultrafilter limits; the arch-scale ratio is derived from the clock inputs. These primitive receipts are upstream inputs, not sensor conclusions. The limiting section is the componentwise grade-five `ladder`; the source's `lensP` supplies its right-inverse identity and the eventual common-tail chart properties. The source supplies this chart under the parameters in `springs`: `hZ : steep 𝓛 Z`, `H : C.Makes`, `hb : (𝓛 : Filter (ℕ × ℕ)) ≤ F.fuse`, `hr : Scrim C W u ψ φ δ ζ r D`, `hq : Gondola F a X 𝓛 V Y`, `K : Sceptre C W`, `θ : PostT C.path.levels`, `hG : G.Simple`, and `hp : Spun Z X V a s`. These are structured assumptions, not asserted to follow just from the abstract identities in the manuscript. `Gondola.growth` is the selected-level divergence; `Gondola.terminal` supplies eventual grade-five lift convergence along the fixed ultrafilter and `X.U i 5 z = S.project 5 (Y 5 i z)` for every field `z`. `Spun.starts` identifies the sensor on a common tail, component by component, with the normalized Vault map. `YM.Trace.Terrace.vspin` and `cruise` make each `Y 5 i h_α` odd under total inversion; `gateW` transports oddness through `Z.M`, and `nights` proves the core fixes that odd vector. The source's finite-volume/scale chart production has not been independently audited in this unit; the above is the precise interface consumed.

The second input `eq:trace-sensor-marked` is **exactly** the three extra arguments of `springs`, for an arbitrary sequence `v`: convergence to `Stem5`, `hueK(A)(Stern V i) • s i (v i) → 0`, and `hueK(A)(Stern V i) * Lease (U#) hG (v i) → 0`. There is no requirement that `v` be the medium or that the resulting functionals converge in the statement of `Prosper`. The special medium is one later supplier, not a restriction on `springs`. Pointwise inequalities in `Prosper.vote` have one eventual index uniform over vectors; the weighted bound has a tail that may depend on the positive epsilon. Both are preserved.

## Literature and editorial decisions

Searched `blueprint/background/` and relevant `Bots/notes/` for trace, receiver, finite-dimensional perturbation and Ward results; no accepted matching external receiver theorem was located. Checked M. Winklmeier, *Functional Analysis*, Theorem 2.10, p. 29, https://math.uniandes.edu.co/~mwinklme/teaching/FuncAna/FA-lecture_notes.pdf : on a Banach space `||T||<1` makes `id−T` invertible by the Neumann series with inverse norm bounded by `(1−||T||)⁻¹`.
