Notation and inputs

The proof uses the same selected table, standard table, tickets, ultrafilter and chart across the three branches. The separation ultrafilter is used at each fixed original scale before the original scale tends to infinity. The chart sequence is indexed by i, with k_i = Stern V i, and tends to infinity by Gondola.growth and Sstar. The coefficient-limit sequence is composed with k_i; no diagonal selection of new histories is made.
