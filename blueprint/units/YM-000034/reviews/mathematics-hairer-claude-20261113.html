Findings and evidence

Checked step by step:

  1. Material tensor (lines 61–120). Injectivity of \(w \mapsto \mathcal{S} (w)\) on the jets \(F_{01} = v\), giving the \((2, 2)\) and \((0, 0)\) entries \(2 w_2 |v|^2\) and \((w_1 + 2 w_2) |v|^2\), and \(F_{02} = F_{12} = v\), giving \((0, 1)\) entry \(w_3 |v|^2\). Symmetry and invariance under the signed and real average. \(\text{tr} M (w) = 4 (w_1 + 4 w_2) \mathcal{O}\). The traces \(0, 0, 4\mathcal{O}\) for \(w^0 = (- 1, \tfrac{1}{4}, - 1)\), \(w^- = (0, 0, 1)\), \(w^+ = (0, \tfrac{1}{4}, 0)\). The coefficients \(w^{\pm}\) agree with the displayed correction fields \(J^{\pm}\).

  2. Chart identity (lines 122–160). \(V_{k, c} B_k x = c \hspace{0.17em} \pi A_k^{- 1} KA_k x\). The difference from \(c \hspace{0.17em} \pi x\) is a multiple of \(\pi A_k^{- 1} e_0 = a_k^{- 4} \boldsymbol{1}_4\), which centring removes.

  3. Coefficient limit (lines 170–230). \(\|z - \vartheta_k^{- 2} K \Gamma v\|_{\infty} \leqslant \vartheta_k^{- 2} \| \xi - \Gamma v\|_{\text{c}} \leqslant \delta_k\), using \(z = \vartheta_k^{- 2} K \xi\). Then the two-set bound \(\| \textsf{R} \| \delta_k + \|T\| \varepsilon\), preserved by the fixed-\(k\) ultrafilter limit, and the order \(k \to \infty\) before \(\varepsilon \downarrow 0\). The principal-witness argument is sound as a deduction from its stated local inputs:

    • the invariant linear functional on a simple algebra vanishes;

    • the Hessian kills infinitesimal gauge directions because the first variation vanishes;

    • the elementary-direction bound \(16 (1 + \max (r_i, r_j))^4\);

    • the compactness-by-contradiction upgrade, using injectivity of \(T\).

  4. Slow trace (lines 338–354). \(\| \text{tr} W^b_k \| \leqslant 4 \| \textsf{R} \| \delta_k\) for \(b \in \{0, -\}\). The remark that \(\hat{\beta}_k^p \delta_k \to \infty\) is correct: \(\tfrac{1}{4} (\log y)^2 \leqslant 4 \sqrt{y}\).

  5. Moment-map convergence (lines 355–615).

    • Injectivity of \(\pi_0\) at grade four via the constant-curvature affine Maxwell solutions, and \(\mathcal{O} \neq 0\) (value \(\tfrac{1}{2} |v|^2\)).

    • The recentring bound \(\|K (\mathcal{N}v -\mathcal{T}v)\|_{\text{c}} \leqslant 2 \alpha \|K\mathcal{T}v\|_{\text{c}}\), and the lift scaling \(\vartheta^{- 2} a^4\) with \(\beta (a_m h)^4\).

    • The one-step factor \(4 g\) and the backward induction.

    • The summit bound \(0 \leqslant g_j \leqslant 2 \text{Gate} (\hat{\beta}_j)\), so \(\hat{\beta}_{\text{summit}} / \hat{\beta} \to 1\), and the scheduled budget \(32 (1 + 2 \log x) (\log x)^{48} \leqslant e^{(\log \log x)^2 / 10}\) eventually.

    • The \(Z_i / X_i\) combination, and \(\textsf{M}_i \mathcal{A}_i x = \pi_0 x - \langle U_i \pi_0 x \rangle \boldsymbol{1}^0_4\). The latter uses \(\pi Y_i = U_i\) and the eventual transport of the unit, which is correctly flagged as an input.

    • The Neumann inverse with \(\|\mathcal{A}_i^{- 1} - 1\| \leqslant 2 \|\mathcal{A}_i - 1\|\).

  6. prop:ex-branch-moments. The chart-array identity is the chart identity applied to \((\textsf{R} \text{sym} x)_{ac}\) after commuting \(B_k\) with the average. The limit is \(\text{sym} (\mathfrak{c}_0 \pi_0 \mathcal{S}(w^b)) = M (w^b)\). Correct.

  7. Recording machinery (lines 660–920).

    • Grade-three fields are multiples of the unit (one factor at most; the invariant linear part vanishes).

    • Detection inequality eq:ex-branch-moving-detector.

    • The radius bound \(R (x) = O (x^{122 / 32}) = o (x^4)\).

    • The geometric tail \(\text{tail}_{k + 1} \leqslant \tfrac{2}{3} \text{tail}_k\), using \(a_{k + 1} \leqslant a_k / 3\) and \(J_{k + 1} \leqslant J_k + 1\).

    • The rooted price is fast for every \(\eta < 1 / 8\).

    All correct as deductions from the stated purchase and spectator inputs.

  8. prop:ex-branch-raw-fast. The averaged functional \(\Lambda^{\sigma}_k\) over \(n_k = \lceil e^{(1 - \tau) s_k} \rceil\) displacements telescopes the difference identity to \(| \Lambda^{\sigma}_k w_{d, k} | \leqslant D^{\sigma}_{d, k} + 2 E^{\sigma}_{d, k} / (a_k n_k)\). The endpoint values agree by the grade-three unit property. Only a finite intersection of large sets is used at each fixed \(k\). The false branch is exactly zero; the principal trace is exactly \(\sum_d \nabla_d Q_d\). Correct. The explicit rate \((1 + s)^5 e^{- s / 8}\) with \(n_k = \lceil e^{s_k} / (1 + s_k) \rceil\) is also correct.

  9. prop:ex-branch-washout. \(\text{tr} t^b_k = |\mathcal{G}|^{- 1} \sum_g \mathfrak{c} \rho_g (y^b_k)\) is fast and centred. With \(\pi r^b_k = q^b_k\), the chart identity gives \(\hat{\beta}^p \text{tr} (\textsf{P}_i t^b) = \textsf{M}_i (\hat{\beta}^p B_{k_i} r^b_{k_i}) \to 0\), because \(\|B_k \| \leqslant C \hat{\beta}_k e^{\ell_k}\) is subexponential. The explicit rate exponent \(p + 6\) and the true-branch divergence (limit trace \(4\mathcal{O} \neq 0\)) are correct.
