Mathematical content and status

The manuscript reorganizes the local proof of YM.RG08.Flat.toward as two mathematical arguments: a neutral weighted-kernel convolution estimate and an exact quadratic representative identity. It states the finite-label weighted row/column and first-difference costs explicitly, including the powers L^{-2}, L^2, and L^{-3}. The scalar bound used in centering is proved in the text. The product bound is obtained by the triangle inequality for cover expense, Tonelli/Fubini on nonnegative absolute majorants, and multiplication of the row/column constants. Its coefficient is m^2 q (2/tau)p/L, where m is the common finite face-label cardinality.

The zero-sum replacement of the outgoing kernel by its centered difference is proved from neutrality. The separate neutrality and simultaneous-translation invariance conclusions use the source's travel and covariance assumptions; their proofs are given at the level of cover sums and changes of variables. The matrix identity is derived in the manuscript: it proves the mixed Schur identity by matrix multiplication from the two primal/dual input relations, and proves each inherited carrier identity from the floor equalities and diagonal tower identity.

This write-up is newly reorganized prose; it is not a literal translation of Lean helper structure. The independent reviewer has been asked to check the cover-path estimate, label sums, and finite-period reindexing. No external literature theorem is invoked: the arguments are elementary weighted-sum and finite-dimensional matrix calculations, and the local source proof was directly inspected.

Constants and normalization cross-check

For order two, the source Atlas bounds are row cost q/L^2, column cost q L^4/L^2=qL^2, and first-difference row cost p/L^3. The manuscript keeps each fixed input/output type in these inequalities; each finite-type sum is counted once explicitly. The centered row coefficient per fixed output label is (2/tau)p/L^3; summing over output labels gives m(2/tau)p/L^3. The root column per fixed input label is qL^2; summing over input labels gives mqL^2. Their product is m^2q(2/tau)p/L, which equals YM.RG08.Packets.neutralCoin with both finite label sets equal to the face labels. The pullback matrix orientation is M^T A N; in toward, the root kernel is matched by the Y carrier and the outgoing kernel by the H carrier. The inherited identities consequently produce N_A on the left of the fine representative and M_A on its right, matching the orientation of YM.RG08.Flat.poised.
