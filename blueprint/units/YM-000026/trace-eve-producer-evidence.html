Exact construction audit

bairn fixes the finite frame family \(F = (\text{frames} \hspace{0.17em} G) . \text{level} \hspace{0.17em} d\), its test list, \(e = \text{title} (P_1)\), \(a = \text{title} (P_0)\), credibility \(h\), door rate \(\nu\), and

\(\displaystyle c = \text{orbitCost} (F) \hspace{0.17em} \text{errorPrice} (\text{hol} \hspace{0.17em} G, F) \hspace{0.17em} \text{corn} (G, d) \hspace{0.17em} a.\)

It sets \(Z = \text{testPrice} (F) \geqslant 1\), \(T = \text{readPrice} (F) \geqslant 1\), and \(\kappa = \min ((\nu / 2) / (cZ), \zeta)\). For each probe \(p\), the charged scalar is

\(\displaystyle g_p (s) = \text{partPrice} (\text{hol} \hspace{0.17em} G, p) \hspace{0.17em} \text{gaugeMeter} (d) L^d \hspace{0.17em} 2 (es + s) + s.\)

Each \(g_p\) is continuous at zero with \(g_p (0) = 0\). V.eventually_all over the finite test family gives a common open ball radius \(b > 0\) on which all the finitely many inequalities \(g_p (s) \leqslant \kappa\) hold. The source sets \(Q = b / 2\).

The proof then combines the following exact source outputs on one eventual tail:

  1. Mercury.tuned supplies \(u\) with strict-power rate \(- 1 / 2\) and PermitY. If there is no branch patch, \(u = 0\) and the permit statement is vacuous. Otherwise it applies trove to that actual branch patch and replaces its response \(f\) by \(2 |f|\); the rate is unchanged and the absolute value preserves the purchased permit bound.

  2. highstar at tolerance \(Q\) gives the local probe-to-run error, after multiplication by \(L^d\).

  3. scales_m is called with price \(Q / e\) and its linear-in-\(t\) bound is evaluated at \(t = e\), giving the cap \(\leqslant 1\) and clock/moment bound \(Q / \chi\).

  4. Blush.fruition, with coefficient \(L^d\) and budget \(S = (\nu / 2) / (cTh)\), gives the cloud mass bound.

  5. Rebuild.geometry gives the two threshold ratios required for the factor-two blend.

The source has \(\chi \in \mathcal{R}_0\) and \(u \in \mathcal{R}_{- 1 / 2}\), so the product-rate rule gives \(\chi u \in \mathcal{R}_{- 1 / 2}\) and hence \(\chi |u| \to 0\) by the strict-power definition. The manuscript then carries out the remaining algebra: cap and path bounds are already \(Q / \chi\); the law follows from \(g_p (Q) \leqslant \kappa\); and the combined cloud/probe charge is at most \(c (Z \kappa + TSh) \leqslant \nu\).
