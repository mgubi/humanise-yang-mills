Reorganized proofs and exact conventions

  • Injectivity of E_t is proved by repeated halving and strong continuity, a valid shorter variant of the source's analytic identity-theorem proof. The imaginary unitary group is still constructed on the dense range of E_1. Spectral zero is never assigned an imaginary power by continuous calculus.

  • Complex duration is -i z - p on the initial vector leg and -i(z_j-z_i)-2p between insertions. Real-time translation has sign +i in its spectral exponent.

  • The independent-height Gram path is (-n s,…,-s,t,…,m t). The first real position tuple is reversed but NOT geometrically reflected. The first labels and tests are reversed and starred/conjugated.

  • Joint tempered boundary uses the exact finite Taylor formula on weakly ordered heights. It is stronger than fixed-profile convergence and applies to arbitrary ratios of the two independent heat parameters.

  • Fourier convention: test transform integrates exp(-i X.p), with inverse factor (2 pi)^(-4n); the distribution transform is W composed with that same hat. The halfspace result concerns absolute-coordinate momenta and all suffix sums.

  • The field common domain is the finite span of all word vectors. Its null space is invariant because of the star-concatenation pairing identity, not because positivity alone makes arbitrary concatenation bounded. No field boundedness or essential self-adjointness is claimed.
