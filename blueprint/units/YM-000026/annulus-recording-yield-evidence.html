Expanded proof and exact correspondence

  1. Full mesh, no extraction. Firm.tryout applies Vane.issues to a strip-bounded periodic entry at width parameter sigma/4. The resulting Width bound is SumBudget(exp((sigma/4)*size(n))*|c_n|, A*(2*(1+sigma/4)/(sigma/4))^4). On a side-ell grid, character orthogonality gives shop as the sum of precisely the Laurent modes with frequency in ell*Z^4; stock is the zero mode. Every nonzero such frequency has size(n)>=ell, so the weighted mass bounds the entire alias tail by royalty(sigma,A)*exp(-sigma*ell/4). This proves the estimate for every mesh size, not just a selected subsequence. The manuscript spells out the 4D contour shift, the one-coordinate geometric sum, and character orthogonality.

  2. Exact local coefficient identity. PoleTest is fineMag(input…), where fineMag=(1/2) log det(rankAux)-log det(grenade). The ticket for the combined response gives exact Taylor identities for the rank and reference matrices through order two. Their determinants at zero are nonzero by rank_positive and grenade_safe, which use the regulator, floor, path bounds, attachment and swornness. The ordinary first and second derivative formulas for log det produce the direct quadratic jet and one ordered product of first jets for the specified ordered pair. ring_award, followed by gemF, yields the source's Awards identity: nonconserving two-leg entries vanish, and a conserving entry is ell^4 * shop of the response entry. profitable is half of the response zero mode, so its mixed second derivative has factor 2! and equals stock; no extra volume factor appears. These are finite-volume identities and precede the Fourier limit.

  3. Exchange and reality. At each finite side the pole test is a real scalar function of real site/color variables, so its Hessian is symmetric and real. Fourier transformation gives exchange of the two external legs and conjugation at negative momentum on every allowed grid point. The Fourier-tail estimate gives uniform convergence of the normalized grid pictures to the analytic zero-mode picture. The union of a cofinal full sequence of side grids is dense, so continuity extends both identities to all torus momenta. This is why the half-scaled zero mode is a genuine real symmetric Hessian picture, not merely a formal factorial normalization.

  4. Analytic zero-mode picture. Averaging the second momentum over normalized Haar measure preserves the bound and holomorphy on any smaller strip. The output picture is half the internal zero mode, which is Firm(sigma/2,A). The coefficient tail is applied at fixed real external momentum to the internal momentum variable. This distinction is important: it gives both the output strip and the source-accurate error ell^4*royalty(sigma,A)*exp(-sigma*ell/4).

  5. All-period assembly. In pouring, the baseline H comes from vintageWilson; it has fees (k,E) and exact Wilson matches. For fixed target parameters, saunter produces uniform (v,M) and, for every growing side sequence plus admissible (B,p,R,Lambda), a family T with soft order 3 and binding intended-Wilson at each mesh. Set w=min(k,v) and add the two families, preserving the exact ticket and fee (w,E+M). At each requested odd side L, put y=1/L and s=min(sigma,w,y/128). The positivity/floor data give HouseWord; its massifTabs field uses all the tickets, floors at reference and growing sides, attachment, metric and swornness. HouseWord.done uses the regulated interacting and free matrices and a common strip reduction to produce the analytic response bounds. profit then gives the all-mesh recording estimate and e_j -> 0 for this fixed L.

  6. Drift chosen before L. astronomy selects constants a,b before L; its quantifier is over every Orion datum, including the House–Word constructed at y=1/L. The same response then satisfies Waves1(a,y) and Waves2(b,y) for the full centered box and shell. absoluteGeode splits the box into the inner ball, annulus and exterior. The respective errors are bounded by a*pi^2/2, 3*pi^2*b, and a*volume(boundsEarth), with the exact pole integral on the annulus. Hence D0=cartWeight*(a*(pi^2/2+volume(boundsEarth))+3*pi^2*b) is independent of L and of mesh sequence. The source pouring sets the minimum odd side to one.

Remaining producer boundary

This unit proves the Fourier alias-tail estimate and the downstream finite-recording/drift assembly under the explicitly stated baseline, response-service, House–Word, and all-record two-wave inputs. It does not prove the local producer of the two-wave estimates. In particular, astronomy applies getReals to Hstar and restricted Orion populations, then calls Rally.register at scale |x|+mu and Rally.shell at scale |x| only when mu<=|x|. Rally.register requires both upper bounds from Fleet.tadControl; Rally.shell also requires the whole-lower-face bounds from Fleet.bubbleControl and the lower-minus-halo remainders from Fleet.haloControl. These local Hstar/Orion to Fleet face estimates remain the local supplier task. The companion Fleet-face chapter now derives the same-bed floor/free identities and vacuum first-variation formula, but still keeps scale-weighted matrix prices and shell-separated halo differences as explicit quantitative inputs. The present theorem does not infer those prices from generic compactness or from the annulus conversion.
