Scope and local notation

Exact estimate boundary and remaining obligations

The first local extraction theorem, YM.Extraction.first_surface, does not establish its own estimates. It gives limits only along a mesh subsequence; the full-mesh upgrade at the public endpoint is a separate input, not an output of this compactness argument. Its exact inputs are TransferLip C f and Bound C f. The inspected producers reduce those to CenteredBook C f and C.Makes. Thus the first finite-cutoff estimate to independently establish for an application is either the displayed CenteredBook budget (including the fixed-period and expanding quantifiers) together with C.Makes, or a direct alternative yielding both clauses of TransferLip and both clauses of Bound. In CenterPort.book, the cells component is also required; no weaker “eventually in one diagonal” estimate suffices. The manuscript keeps this boundary explicit and does not claim that the abstract compactness step proves these inputs.

first_surface only returns generic-continuous RawLimits. It does not prove smoothness at coincident configurations. The hierarchy passage Harvest.assembles requires S.Smooth and S.Capped, so these remain separate dependencies. A second caveat is quantifier scope: the source selects a common subsequence over the countable signature index, then uses compact equicontinuity to extend from dense sets. This establishes limits for every signature in that countable index; it is not a raw diagonal choice over uncountably many arbitrary test functions.

No literature note in blueprint/background/README.md supplies this exact Wilson-lattice estimate package or the source-specific limit passage. The compactness and positivity arguments in the draft are given directly; the standard countable-product compactness and dominated-convergence facts should be checked by reviewers against the stated spaces and hypotheses. No external source is cited as replacing the model-specific estimate obligations.
