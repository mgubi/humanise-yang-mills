Exact source map

All names below lie in YM.Maxwell.Free unless qualified otherwise.

  • rad, Clears, Pole, green_pole, Pole.step, Pole.taylor, kernel_pole, Lean/YM/Next/MAXWELL/Pole.lean: degree -r, harmonicity, and globally smooth radial numerator after multiplying by |z|^(2(r-1)). The prose derives the numerator differentiation formula and all kernel orders from the canonical covariance, rather than treating harmonicity as a new input. It only uses smoothness, a consequence of the source's ContDiff … omega.

  • lap_valued, clears_faint, Faint.lean: Laplacian coefficient 4(s+1)(s+2-r) in four dimensions, clearing descent, and homogeneity plus continuity at zero. The zero coefficient at the genuine pole clearing order is not divided by; descent only uses exponents strictly below r-1.

  • Silt, Echoes, Deep, Silt.twice, private sum_peel, Deep.clear, Silt.lean: product poles homogeneous of degree -q with radial clearing q-2; finite highest-degree peeling separates them from a harmonic residue. Prose writes natural truncation max(q-2,0) explicitly. No false plain limit of the full colliding moment is asserted.

  • Rack, shelf, Shelves.lean: jet cutoff j, not weighted cap d. eat, Recurse.lean; singleBite, all_bite, upper_shelf, circulate, circulate_eval, Morsels.lean: finite coordinate contraction derivation and same-colour quadratic circulation. Polynomial derivations do not act on z.

  • path_wield, chorus, Deep.weaker, duet_path, Frenzy.lean: continuous distant contractions, highest jet residue, lower-order suppression, and exact double-hit expansion into product poles. Orders are 4+k+s and 8+k+l+s+b; M is strictly greater than 8+k+l+2j.

  • front_egress, private cluster_in, vertex, cluster_clear, Rumbles.lean: four contraction placements, reconstruction of the surviving single-hit sum by the one-leg Wick identity, eventual separated configuration along every fixed nonzero direction, and gauge-invariant colour sum. Local vanishing uses the original null capacity because the added quadratic test has grade k+l+4<=d. The source requires l<k; it does not impose l<j here. That latter inequality belongs to the separate weighted-degree drop in circulates_lower and must be checked by the classifier consumer.

The chapter's top theorem is exactly the same-capacity null conclusion of cluster_clear for P in shelf j. It does not prove the generic invertibility of the colour-probe matrix, the uniform external detector, or the classification of quiet charges. These remain in the separate classifier chapter.
