Exact mathematical scope

The outer radius is (B=\operatorname{radiusB}(x)=\lceil(\log x)^2\rceil). The reader panel radius is the source's natural-number quotient

\(\displaystyle p = \text{boxCharge} (x) = \lfloor (B + 80) / 20 \rfloor,\)

not the real quotient. The article proves the geometric inequalities for (B\ge200):

\(\displaystyle 30 p < 11 B + 1 - p, \quad 9 p \leqslant B, \quad B / 4 + p + B / 50 \leqslant B / 2, \quad B / 4 + 2 p + B / 25 \leqslant B, \quad p + B / 50 + B / 4 + B / 2 \leqslant B.\)

Here Clears at outer B excludes the seed dome of radius 10B+B+1. Translate supplies an onto bijection of actual and cleared panel labels, using clearance to prove range/liveness. It does not equate the curved and identity row Gram entries. Instead the manuscript derives the source change from the rowwise frozen cancellation, chart estimates, and a cutoff-plus-tail estimate; derives the actual mean via the normal residual identity and its local inverse/tail split; and derives the inverse-column bound by an entrywise Gram perturbation followed by the finite resolvent identity. The two distinct positive Gram gaps are retained.

The stated outputs are the actual mean error (x^{-7/8}) and the actual-to-cleared panel inverse-column error (x^{-1/4}), for a fixed payment multiplier R and source parameter 0<=t<=R*clearanceFare(x), with coarse period c>=100(1+B), the stored-flag equality, and the same pose/history across all queried centers. The mean coordinate is active and within B/4 of the target; the inverse-column indices lie in its panel. The exact outer-scale price, clearance and path receipts. The later cut-head split to the full physical covariance is expressly excluded from the theorem.
