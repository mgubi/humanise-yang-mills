Proof audit

Store_l packages the outputs of july and year_spec with the already selected scaling, hierarchy, and same-history chart. The first theorem is not a new selection: it obtains ChoiceK from reveals. reveals uses the three source-provided paid tensor families (principal, false correction, true correction), obtains the same-history response, and calls formulate to solve the two-direction cancellation. formulate proves its matrix eventually bijective by convergence to the invertible reference two-direction map, obtains a bounded solution by finite-dimensional stable inversion, and uses transferX/mere to calibrate the second coefficient. The prose gives the exact finite-dimensional inversion and coefficient extraction. Its calibration uses the displayed principal-response inputs, the weighted response comparison gapC, the Ward comparison diadem, and the paid sensor estimate Prosper.vote; the sensor estimate is an incoming producer boundary for this unit. Likewise the branch calculation reduces the field and trace limits to the operator convergence of Moment4, the finite branch coefficient limits, the exponent-one muteP bounds, and the true-branch trace identity sum_delta. The latter branch limits are the other incoming producer boundary, not consequences of unweighted field convergence alone. serene checks conservation from the exact cancellation equation.

For year_spec, the physical tensor and the correction sequence belong to the same one-dimensional conserved kernel. awake_o finds a nonzero reference radial derivative and an observer test. pay_jet and comparison_jets give convergence of the identical shell derivative for both sequences. If the physical tensor were zero, its measured sequence would vanish, contradicting the nonzero reference limit. Rank one therefore gives a scalar ratio; evaluation by a linear functional nonzero on the reference limit proves that ratio tends to one. The field and flux limits then transfer by homogeneity. This is why the shell test is essential: the rank-one statement alone leaves the scalar undetermined.

july uses center_plus to write the comparison trace as the trace of the recentered physical stress plus a scalar multiple of the unit. year_spec handles the first term; shift_unit makes the second term vanish at the zoomed scale. trace_Year identifies the zoomed trace with the diagonal trace of the normalized physical stress. leaderB applies a left inverse of the scalar embedding and takes the two coordinates, yielding the exact eventual decomposition and its two limits. Store_l then fills the Scribe fields directly; gang uses pays_sky and gravity to obtain a selected scaling and chart for the arbitrary-null-sequence clause before invoking the same Store_l at that same history.

The prose reuses the reviewed units sec:ex-lattice-trace-producer, sec:ex-lattice-trace-weighted-estimates, sec:ex-lattice-trace-crown-buffer, and sec:ex-lattice-trace-eve-producer for the numerical source prices and scale comparisons. It does not reproduce their kernel-rank, response-price, or finite-probe calculations. The physical stress transfer above is the additional same-history bridge required by the scale/tested-chart argument in sec:ex-lattice-firesky-gallery.
