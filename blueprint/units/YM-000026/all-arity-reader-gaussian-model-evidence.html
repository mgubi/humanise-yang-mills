Exact route and mathematical correspondence

In Horns.lean, the consumer iterates over the finite streak of the lantern. For each streak element it calls e_xpose with that element's wood (forest) and tag and retains its nonnegative share; the shares sum to one. Thus the article's common-law statement is applied separately at each forest/tag atom. It does not identify different tag covariances as one law. Within each atom, however, all reader patches use the same Mural probability space and the same random vector.

RoomView is exactly the product of Haar variables on frozen links and a Gaussian vector on In x A F, the active fine-link Lie coordinates. viewer equips this space with product Haar times the centered cloud of the selected channel covariance. gazed applies one frozen-link-dependent channel drift and the same scaled Gaussian vector to the coarse configuration by the finite physical update. These are the article's (1) and (2). The common sample convention is source-exact: the integration variable is not resampled when the reader label changes.

For the covariance, Compass.projected first makes the single signed transport of the frozen links/forest and applies the pose's fixed gauge rotation to the Gaussian vector. The matrix rowView is exactly (dKeys A F x).submatrix toPAC id; the incidence-curvature vector is formed only at this row map, not at the Gaussian input stage. fanMap proves that the transported second coordinate has exactly the covariance of the transported channel with its transported tag. The turned channel law then gives the covariance at the pose-aligned field. rowView is the submatrix of dKeys selecting the incidence coordinates, and covView is defined by R * Q * R.transpose. Therefore the centered reader array has full joint law cloud (R Q Rᵀ), not merely matching diagonal variances. The exact measure-preserving statement is in preserves_counts; needle composes it with the full Gaussian integration-by-parts law and stores the resulting VMeasure in Mural.initial.

faithfulSight proves the comparison with the short covariance for every pair of incidence coordinates in sp_okes. It inserts the tracked finite-volume covariance, applies the paid Dawn.strong.sight bound on the two entries, and then applies the common-history telescope. The manuscript displays this exact two-term split. The reader supports enter through sp_okes; the room and pose margins provide the separation hypotheses for both entries. The estimate is not just a diagonal covariance statement.

For the coordinate comparison, e_xpose defines a separate scalar mask Γ a for each patch reader a, while retaining the same room law, sample, forest, tag, and centered vector. shade_auth supplies the Guarded receipt; by the definition of Guarded, it has values in [0,1] and ∫(1-Γ a)≤ε². This implies μ{Γ a=0}≤ε²; it does not imply μ{Γ a<1}≤ε². If that reader's mask is positive, paid_veil and the local room estimate put the sample in the region where screens applies. screens first returns health and then, for every chamber coordinate in that patch's reader set, a single gauge element (depending on sample and patch, but not on the coordinate) with the field-coordinate error at most ε. The complex fine read is exactly rigid (coloring q (basisFt …)); the center is (colorFt …).re using the one fixed parent manner T.2.val and coarse configuration U, where colorFt applies Normal.reads (R i).H to that rigid coarse basis. The fine patch manner a.val enters only the fine basis, while J_a restricts the common parent forecast coordinates. fine_read identifies the fine basis value with the transported local core, and the change-of-level identity identifies the coarse reference value with this real normal read. These are precisely equation eq:ex-lattice-reader-coordinate and its per-reader quantifiers.

The guard estimate is per patch. This chapter makes no union-bound claim that all patch masks are simultaneously positive; such a claim would require an additional cost depending on the number of patches. What is shared across the family is the underlying law and the full Gaussian vector, so cross-reader covariances are preserved.

Relation to existing proof units

The section does not duplicate the density-jet comparison in sec:principal-gaussian-comparison: it identifies the finite-dimensional Gaussian reader vector and the fixed masked field coordinates that the comparison requires. That canonical comparison still needs its separate mean, covariance-jet, cutoff, compact-ball, and tail inputs. It does not differentiate a reader insertion.

The section does not duplicate sec:gaussian-reassembly, which proves the absolutely convergent activity sum restores the original block integral and factors by its assigned supports. The present auxiliary Mural law alone does not prove that reassembly identity.

The handoff to sec:ex-lattice-trace-eve-producer is the same fixed masked insertion, common reader coordinates, and uniformity over its allowed reader directions. The response-rate and cloud-price estimates are not reproved here. The finite tag average outside this construction has nonnegative weights summing to one, so estimates uniform in the tag atom pass through that average without changing the common-law argument within each atom.
