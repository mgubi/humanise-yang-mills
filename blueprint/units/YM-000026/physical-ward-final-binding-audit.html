Finding

I found no type, grade, or same-object mismatch in the public finite-grade binding. The public route supplies the data consumed by YM.Bid.station from one selected Run; station binds A01–A08 to the same hierarchy, response, Maxwell reference, and interaction. At each grade it chooses one scaling sequence after taking a finite maximum of the requests. The scale sequence may depend on the grade and finite test family. The resulting proportionality constant does not: YM.Climb.extends_form compares the gradewise constants by injective grade inclusion, first above and then below its reference grade. The later stress normalization is a separate, explicit identification of the spin response with the same positional scalar and of the reference stress with the canonical free stress. It is not a consequence of the free classifier alone.

The finite-test construction does not assert one uniform cutoff over all grades or all possible tests. At each fixed grade, the tests are finite; the source first takes the maximum of their finitely many scale thresholds. Grade uniformity needed for the scalar identity comes from the structural extends_form argument, not from a diagonal choice of one scale working for all grades. The global configuration statement is then obtained at each fixed finite word/configuration by the analytic propagation theorem.

Exact source chain

  1. YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Run.extractible17 in Lean/YM/RG17/Joint.lean starts from the purchased Run R, calls R.bought, and constructs the single table S, final field package, and scaling supplier X. This is the actual public caller, rather than a separately selected Bourse or comparison family.

  2. YM.Bid.station in Lean/YM/Bid/Town.lean receives that same M,E,I,H and p.uv. Its witnessing step combines A01, A02, A03, and YM.Outer.miles; the resulting Shapes, ShellCom, Hyper, and transverse conditions refer to I.p and I.Y on the selected table.

  3. In YM.Bid.pressured, each grade d uses the selector W.lit.cuts for M.T.W and its reference spin structure. For that grade, A04, A05, and A06 provide Watches. buysBoth takes the maximum of the finite request bounds in these three records, and selects uses ActLaw.connects to choose one scaling object X satisfying their conjunction. It is the same X for the fitted derivative row, both A05 observer limits, and all rows in this grade's A06 selector.

  4. YM.Work.A06.test_aisle gives the exact identity X.test k s (X.aisle k d q) = X.r k ^ s.m * scaledScene.lens q; moves_limit obtains convergence of the finite row map on fixed free vectors. transverse_zero says the moved rows annihilate the actual derivative and I.p exactly at every index. This is a common-kernel transfer, not an injectivity claim for the free scene family.

  5. YM.Climb.extends_form in Lean/YM/Climb/Peaks.lean combines each gradewise concentrated statement with nonvanishing of the derivative and response. fixed_kernel gives a scalar at that grade. For any two grades above the reference threshold, over_peak compares their scalars after raising the lower grade; injectivity of the inclusion and nonvanishing force equality. A grade below the threshold is compared by raising it to the fixed reference grade. Thus one nonzero real c satisfies leaves I.p d = c • leaves V.deriv d for every grade. The scale witnesses themselves need not be common across grades.

  6. YM.Bid.formed turns that identity into Crystal: it normalizes the same I.Y by c^{-1} and obtains the commutation and collinearity hypotheses. YM.Climb.descends calls YM.Ward.global on precisely this normalized spin row. Its hypotheses are DiffPlan.Derivatives, Crystal.ready.1 (commutation), and Crystal.ready.2 (collinearity); the conclusion is Still at every finite arity and every separated configuration.

  7. A07 is applied to this same (V,M.T,J,H.spin,M.hT.spin). Its free input is the sibling Maxwell-data field M.frames : erases M.hT.spin M.T.W, supplied by the free internal-map theorem. Its separate transfer input is the actual ActLaw H.weak (in the public caller, p.uv.weak), aligned to this same M.T and actual table. YM.Work.A07.transfer works at each fixed grade on the finite dimensional real subspace Winds, selects finitely many separating rows, and uses fixed-vector row convergence to get eventual injectivity. The threshold may depend on the grade. A08 only needs such a threshold: YM.Work.A08.answer kills the Lie defect above it and uses injective grade inclusion to descend the bracket to every grade.

  8. YM.Bid.station then packages the bracket result into Commission; YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Run.extractible17 obtains the station result and passes it into YM.Pert.withFinal. Its private attached proof identifies the same reference stress: from hM, hK, and hJ it proves (M.T.κ)^{-1} • M.T.J = freeStress. The final YM.RG17.supplies application therefore retains the one selected run and the same table for both the finite-grade station and the perturbative extension.

Remaining qualification / risk

This audit validates the object and quantifier joins, not the entirety of the large theorem. Its conclusion is conditional on the selected Run and MaxwellData producer hypotheses already discharged in their own units, including the Maxwell-data field M.frames, finite-window jets, the A04–A06 observer receipts, the free classifier and free erasure. The prose must preserve their finite request quantifiers: for any fixed finite list of grades, arities, rows, and windows, take the maximum requested scale threshold before selecting the scale. It must not claim a threshold uniform over all grades or all configurations. The scalar's grade-uniformity is instead the exact extends_form consequence described above.

No mismatch was found between A01--A08, YM.Bid.station, and the Run.extractible17 caller. The remaining proof status is whatever the independent review records for those supplier units and the public Run construction; this audit does not upgrade their status or assert that the full Yang–Mills proof has been independently accepted.
