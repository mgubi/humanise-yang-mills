Quantitative proof notes and limits

  1. Palette.lucent is a selected source estimate, not a consequence of the fee formula alone. Palette.sizes derives summability by the shared Charity.everyoneN counting result with the fragrance library and its separation hypotheses. The manuscript must not infer global summability by summing a pointwise root inequality over the infinite lattice.

  2. Many.usable doubles lace and lucent; this gives exact output allowance 2*pledgeA/(1-sailUp). Its payment exponent is the sum of the earned exponent and the selected allowance. The rooted support set is the full bookFoot used by the source. Charity.everyoneN invokes YM.Push.AbsoluteSums.Product.everyone_ne, which turns the pointwise rooted hit into a total fee sum with explicit factor |Site(c)|=c^4; no infinite-lattice sum over roots is taken.

  3. The two activity rows are distinct. Stage.usable uses Signed.crowd, whose plants receipt is Mallow.cools (softNeed) with an ordinary allowance at most betaF^(-1/8). Paprika.crowd2 instead uses Pungent and the raised Spicy.june receipt (opens at the base budget/profile) for its second rate clause. Its first rate clause separately uses the raised precursor allowance at most exp(coverTicket)*betaF^(-1/8); this is not the final opens price. Keep their different rate clauses and allowance hypotheses.

  4. The widening proof is the zero extension along the injective activity-label map: old coefficients are retained on its image and all other coefficients are zero. Physical supports at retained labels are unchanged, so the same rooted-hit receipt is transported. The widening retains the exponent allowance; harvesting consumes the reserved exponent unit. Motion.harvest then uses the hard/soft fusion price and the rooted forest budget. Its local algebra is expanded in the manuscript, while remaining analytic receipts are identified as source inputs.

  5. The singleton jet identity used by Photo.shade is discharged in sibling unit rg-menu-jets.tex, equation eq:ex-lattice-menu-jet-proof. It follows from the actual empty/singleton interpolation identities and differentiability of the one-menu final path. The menu fee equals the tuning coefficient in the selected bidding step. The paid score majorants and door-volume receipt remain explicit quantitative inputs to the countable coefficient and hinge sums.

  6. The soft/hard fused gas calculation is cited from sibling draft unit manuscript/existence/soft-log-fusion.tex, section sec:soft-log-fusion, equations eq:fusion-gas-identity and eq:fusion-site-output. It gives the exact flag-compatible component bijection and the site-price estimate used by Motion.harvest; this chapter does not reproduce the component bijection.

  7. The final field polynomial is indexed by boxes (P_p,Q_p) with full support P_p and assigned flag Q_p⊆P_p. Families require disjoint full supports and have flag union equal to the selected support mode. Nonzero compatible activity terms cannot share a barcode because its full support is nonempty; grouping by barcode therefore preserves the support-disjoint family, and absolute convergence justifies the countable regrouping.
