Reorganized arguments and open obligations

The comparison AgeK(a)/b≤2β̂^{a−1}, the decomposition of the medium's limit and weighted price, and the transfer from the β̂ field weight to AgeK(1/5) are ordinary scalar arguments newly written in the manuscript. The source lemmas aspiring, legacy, and rungs are not cited as proof in place of those calculations.

Substantive upstream estimates remain. In particular, the local charge and finite-volume mileage price estimates are relied on through the source proofs of penumbra and ear_price; crestship relies on the funded cover estimate. The selected chart hierarchy must provide the derivative/orientation and probe identities used in small_means. Those producers have not been independently reconstructed in this file. The article therefore proves the scalar receiver bridge and records how source estimates feed it, but it does not claim an independent proof of those analytic suppliers or of the full trace-gallery construction.
