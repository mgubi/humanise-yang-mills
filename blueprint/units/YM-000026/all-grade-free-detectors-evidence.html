Argument correspondence

The off-origin scalar identity eq:ex-lattice-shell-green is the explicit induction corresponding to YM.Extra.FD.shell_green in Lean/YM/Extra/FREE_DETECTOR_ALGEBRA/Harmonic.lean: applyDiff_succ decomposes the normalized Taylor coefficient into derivatives of frozen first-slot tensors; the trace sum vanishes by freeze_trace, and their coordinate contraction recovers the original tensor by freeze_diagonal. The trace vanishing is derivative-slot harmonicity: differentiating the Bianchi identity and summing gives Delta F_im=0 after the divergence terms vanish; the coefficient/freeze relation then gives sum_a partial_a(A_a)=0. It is not inferred from the Maxwell divergence equation alone. The response formula in eq:ex-lattice-kelvin-response then groups radial Green differentiation, divergence reduction, and the second curl in reply_radial and curl_kelvin. The manuscript supplies this radial calculation explicitly: the divergence-free equation removes derivatives of the field strength, the radial-curl identity gives the factor j+2, and the derivative of |x|^{-2(j+2)} gives the reflected radial term. Its constant is strength j = 4(j+1)(j+2)(-2)^j normal, with normal=1/(4*pi^2).

The translated Taylor maps in the manuscript are towards j k (-I(x)) with the source normalization strength(j)/strength(k). Their finite sum is field_expands. Applying the Kelvin response termwise gives the signal of Shift.signal_kelvin. invert_line, line_eq, and signal_diag justify the line-jet statement: the first nonzero normalized Taylor coefficient is at order j and equals strength(j) rr(x)^(j+2) solO(face x) P. The one-leg test formula replyTest supplies the second-side sign (-1)^k.

The reflection in scan/legKernel is solO(face x), which acts on both the field indices and every derivative slot. This is the full action (s dot Q)(v)=s^(2)Q(sv), not merely a two-index matrix multiplication. The manuscript's lower-triangular transform on the finite direct sum of solution grades is the source shiftAll. newLeg_below makes its off-diagonal blocks vanish after using the symmetry of towerB; newLeg_diag and slide_top give the diagonal factor (-1)^j strength(j) rr(x)^(j+2) dotF(P,Q). Since this factor is positive and dotF is positive definite, towerB_pos proves positivity on the full tower simultaneously. The manuscript additionally explains invertibility from the identity diagonal and strict grade lowering.

For a finite orthogonal basis of this tower, aligned_step is the exact removal identity. The manuscript solves its recurrence by induction on monomial degree, obtaining the diagonal weight prod_i alpha_i! d_i^(alpha_i). from_large represents every fixed Wick polynomial in a sufficiently large finite tower. The manuscript explains why: every restricted linear jet coordinate is represented by a vector in the finite-dimensional solution space via its Frobenius Riesz vector, so a polynomial in finitely many jet coordinates is a finite polynomial in tower coordinates. Thus reflected_positive is strict on every nonzero Wick polynomial.

shellSector is the image of the polynomial observation map, so quotienting by Maxwell's equations and Bianchi relations is not an informal deletion of coordinates. no_residual uses point_probe and strict positivity to show that no further on-shell vector is invisible to every probe; free_null identifies the free field quotient with the kernel of the observation map. writes_inj embeds that quotient into the polynomial algebra. Finally, faceTest_eq identifies the actual scanner columns with the reflected pairing, applying the geometric reflection to every tensor index and ordinary coefficient conjugation separately. Strict positivity then gives scanner_inj.
