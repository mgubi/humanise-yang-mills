Proof content and scope

The manuscript expands the terminal recentering, the cancellation in the terminal density and coordinate, the finite downward induction, and the room/bank cone arithmetic. It identifies the exact local response fields needed at every step and derives the marched-weight recurrence used in the root-test conversion. The public application fixes one family, one selected history, one period, one terminal volume, and one Music throughout; no volume-dependent reselection occurs in this argument.

The chapter is conditional on the actual selected-run inputs. The canonical heading-history handoff proves local three-card algebra when its same-history Heading/Stampede and card premises are present; it is not the all-arity PayW compiler. Inns.chaletrail is the exact assembly for the Scrim consumed by the final caller. Its immediate producers Marco.squad, Bandage.nectar, and Bandage.ravine expose the precise upstream fields and hypotheses. The remaining obligation is to derive their common Nest, climbing doors, Freight, delay/payment data, and fixed-family making property from the selected funded history, uniformly in degree, period and volume, while retaining the same history through terminal Cream attachment. The three-card proof and the separate mixed compiler do not establish that selected-history attachment. The terminal and history attachment must be the original strong selected history, with no substitution by a restarted run. The one-step algebra, eaves, terminal toast, finite baking lemma, and G09 cone inequality do not by themselves close these suppliers or the whole existence proof.
