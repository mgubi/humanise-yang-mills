More fundamentally, the offered lanes already assume raw one-step estimates, and the terminal birth argument assumes thickness. Forward.step is an optional conditional route to the raw estimate; its upstream component and integration hypotheses are not established from the gauge setup here. The next producer unit must trace actual callers of Offer, Unbilled.Raw, and Thick back from the final supplier, prove the available upstream estimates, and mark any still-open analytic premises explicitly.
