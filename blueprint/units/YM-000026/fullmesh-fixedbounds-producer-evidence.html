Correspondence and mathematical organization

  • eq:ex-cap-rank is the finite-dimensional rank/readout estimate furnished by the selected detector ticket in YM.FullVolume.cap.

  • eq:ex-cap-tested is the bounded tested coefficient obtained there by subtracting the detector value of the funded difference trial and using the owed residual whose budget tends to zero.

  • eq:ex-cap-remainder is the scaled remainder estimate. In the source it is obtained by setting z=(1+||display(x)||)^{-1}(x-display(x)). The row has bounded coefficient cost because the scalar is at most one and the projected row has norm at most one. The sweep estimate applies since its projected component vanishes. The detector limit is then made at most 1/(2Z).

  • The abstract cap proposition now states the source Coin-type membership for the original row, closure under bounded scalar multiplication/subtraction, and inclusion of uniformly bounded row families. The paid trial contributes assay values in the detector codomain; the separate equation residual compares the detector of the source row with those assays. The sweep premise is stated for normalized-cost rows with zero projected component. This avoids applying a detector defined on coefficient rows to a trial object.

  • The proof of lem:ex-fullmesh-cap uses the exact absorption calculation: if X=||display(x)||, then X <= Zc + Z||L(x-display(x))|| and the scaled remainder estimate gives Z||L(x-display(x))|| <= (1+X)/2; rearrangement yields X <= 2Zc+1.

  • The paragraph after prop:ex-fullmesh-cap-source records the decomposition in YM.FullVolume.improved: write x=display(x)+(x-display(x)), sweep the kernel term to a vanishing owed budget, use the original equation residual as a second vanishing owed budget, and close the paid root-test bound under addition/subtraction. This is the source's argument turning the finite projected-row bound into a paid difference trial.

  • The finite-list induction corresponds to YM.FullVolume.funding: its helper list starts with single-slot insertion trials; the exact update identity is YM.FullVolume.actuate, and each slot's successive differences are paid by YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Run.financed.

  • The two-scale tiling argument corresponds to YM.FullVolume.cohort: choose a fixed tiling span below the requested physical separation, then a finer cell span with 6n a_k<a_ell; fits_together places all points with three-cell-span margin; extent bounds same-cell periodic separation by the tiling side T.side (not the smaller recentering margin t), so strict physical separation makes cell assignment injective. Pairwise separation is required for distinct slot indices even when insertions coincide; YM.Cells.Tiling.planted gives the product root-test estimate under the single common Wilson law. The cutoffs are eventually deep enough for the fixed scales for every period, which is the full-mesh quantifier in FixedBounds.

  • The final estimate eq:ex-fullmesh-bound is the mathematical content of YM.FullVolume.FixedBounds. The positional construction is downstream: YM.FullVolume.positioned requires both this bound and the independently produced same-history Cauchy input YM.FullVolume.Purchased.

Assumptions and remaining inputs

The cap proof uses the actual source's finite selected detector/rank ticket, primary paid trial, normalized coefficient-cost stability, and uniform same-run sweep estimate. This unit proves the nonlinear absorption step and the finite-volume assembly from those receipts; it does not reprove their RG production. The public route still depends on the separate Purchased producer for full-mesh Cauchy convergence. No claim is made here that FixedBounds alone implies convergence, or that the finite volume threshold is uniform over unbounded field/test families.
