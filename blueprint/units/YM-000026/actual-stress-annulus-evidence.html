Proof correspondence and limitations

The numeric gates used in bricks were read literally. Put lambda_i=scale(i)=log(a_i^{-1}); the diverging exponential is in this logarithmic scale, not in the vanishing physical spacing. With Z_i=ventola(C.path.levels,j_i,i+1,δ,φ,η,δ_{i+1},A_i), X_i=exp(sqrt(hatBeta_{i+1})), e_i=32(1+columns_i) a_{i+1}^D X_i/Z_i, and c_i=exp(lambda_i/32), pricey supplies eventually Z_i>0, march(c_i,η,k,i)≤δ_i Z_i/(16 X_i), and 32(1+columns_i)c_i ||alto(k,D)||(e_i/gainChart(i,k))≤fly(D,k)/2. Its logarithmic condition is ((d+12)(1-t)>-1/32). At each fixed opened reference level p_0 and level k, the remaining stake is exp((a_{p_0}/a_k)^4 treasury_k+5), hence constant as i→∞ while c_i→∞. The separate lightness output is eventually 32 fuga(i+1)≤a_{p_0} and a_{i+1}(B_{i+1}^2+1+2*1024*B_{i+1}^2)≤a_{p_0}. bricks uses the first gate for collar room and the second to fit the finite stencil into the coarse chamber. This exact arithmetic is in the manuscript; it is not a proof of the sublemmas feeding these receipts.

The manuscript gives the measure-theoretic shrinking argument explicitly, conditional on the shell-node input and the displayed separated-point majorants from Peak.beneath. At each fixed stencil point the bounded counterterm sequence is extracted once in the common fused filter, using finite-dimensional compactness; that one limit is used for every watch in the finite family. The cost-limit identities then approximate all tester values on the same filter refinement, so their weighted sum cancels. No watch-dependent subsequences are used.

For the bump with unit-scale profile capB, the source support of its derivative lies between radii 1/2 and 1; after dilation by r_m, it lies inside the source annulus r_m/4<|x|<2r_m. The four-dimensional Jacobian, one derivative of the bump, and homogeneity degree p give the factor r_m^(3+p). The grade relation in the two source propositions implies h=3+p, so the shell estimate has exactly the required scaling. The componentwise integral has an integrable compactly supported weight; uniform annulus decay therefore makes it tend to zero. Conservation/window-change identifies it with a fixed flux combination. This proves the exact annihilator statement, not merely asymptotic flux decay.

The finite-dimensional range argument is expanded in ordinary linear algebra: every functional annihilating the tester range also annihilates the flux vector, hence the flux vector belongs to that range. The finite faithful tester set ensures that the representative is independent of the added watch. The continuity extension is the source's frequently_eq argument using grid density and continuous flux/disc maps.

The shell economics are recorded at the level of their actual output and composition. The manuscript does not prove the shell-node output from those inputs. In particular, it does not rederive the primitive analytic estimates feeding pricey, lightness, the paid local-cost receipt, the cofinal run/throw receipt, or the two-channel construction. In particular, YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.pricey imports YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.quilled, YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.mintN, YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.fuchsia, and YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.seeing; lightness imports fuchsia and briar. The proof of the paid local-cost and run/throw records is not rederived here. Those are genuine upstream quantitative proof obligations if the monograph is to discharge the whole source chain here. This draft does not claim their names prove the estimates. The listed declarations were read, but their transitive producer proofs were not exhaustively expanded in this bounded unit. The annular annihilator, finite-dimensional range argument, and root-centered translation/rotation conversion are proved conditional on the explicit shell-node statement; the public endpoint chain is a correspondence, not a completed proof that this statement is available.
