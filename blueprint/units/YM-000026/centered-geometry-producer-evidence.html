Scope

The manuscript is manuscript/existence/centered-geometry-producer.tex. It proves the opened-copy construction of the migration, occupancy, and separated-origin inputs needed by the finite-volume centered moment route. It does not produce marched weights, paid response estimates, the terminal service receipt, or vacuum prices.

The selected root-test consumer Accent.tentF builds the site family by centering a single root cell and taking its copy names. The source calls the geometric interface with opening parameter one, derives spacing by I03, uses injectivity for occupancy, and passes migration/occupancy/Drake into Accent.occursT. The downstream multiscale propagation of those geometric facts under Brine weights is separately proved in the centered-construction chapter. The remaining independent inputs to that consumer are the selected weight/paid-size, service, and vacuum-price data.
