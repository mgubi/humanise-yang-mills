## Exact construction audit

`bairn` fixes the finite frame family $F=(\mathrm{frames}\,G).\mathrm{level}\,d$, its test list, $e=\mathrm{title}(P_1)$, $a=\mathrm{title}(P_0)$, credibility $h$, door rate $\nu$, and
\[
c=\mathrm{orbitCost}(F)\,\mathrm{errorPrice}(\mathrm{hol}\,G,F)\,\mathrm{corn}(G,d)\,a.
\]
It sets $Z=\mathrm{testPrice}(F)\ge1$, $T=\mathrm{readPrice}(F)\ge1$, and $\kappa=\min((\nu/2)/(cZ),\zeta)$. For each probe $p$, the charged scalar is
\[
g_p(s)=\mathrm{partPrice}(\mathrm{hol}\,G,p)\,\mathrm{gaugeMeter}(d)L^d\,2(es+s)+s.
\]
Each $g_p$ is continuous at zero with $g_p(0)=0$. `V.eventually_all` over the finite test family gives a common open ball radius $b>0$ on which all the finitely many inequalities $g_p(s)\le\kappa$ hold. The source sets $Q=b/2$.

The proof then combines the following exact source outputs on one eventual tail:

1. `Mercury.tuned` supplies $u$ with strict-power rate $-1/2$ and `PermitY`. If there is no branch patch, $u=0$ and the permit statement is vacuous. Otherwise it applies `trove` to that actual branch patch and replaces its response $f$ by $2|f|$; the rate is unchanged and the absolute value preserves the purchased permit bound.
2. `highstar` at tolerance $Q$ gives the local probe-to-run error, after multiplication by $L^d$.
3. `scales_m` is called with price $Q/e$ and its linear-in-$t$ bound is evaluated at $t=e$, giving the cap $\le1$ and clock/moment bound $Q/\chi$.
4. `Blush.fruition`, with coefficient $L^d$ and budget $S=(\nu/2)/(cTh)$, gives the cloud mass bound.
5. `Rebuild.geometry` gives the two threshold ratios required for the factor-two blend.

The source has $\chi\in\mathcal R_0$ and $u\in\mathcal R_{-1/2}$, so the product-rate rule gives $\chi u\in\mathcal R_{-1/2}$ and hence $\chi|u|\to0$ by the strict-power definition. The manuscript then carries out the remaining algebra: cap and path bounds are already $Q/\chi$; the law follows from $g_p(Q)\le\kappa$; and the combined cloud/probe charge is at most $c(Z\kappa+TS h)\le\nu$.
