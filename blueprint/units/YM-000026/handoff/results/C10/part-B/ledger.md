- The annulus recording and the radial drift use one response `F=Q+W`, one strip `s=min(σ,w,μ/128)`, one bed, clip, path and side sequence; the local record of the radial bound is `(s,μ,c,B,p,Q)` (`pouring`: the `Orion` literal `⟨k,y,i,B,p,T,ha',hs',hK⟩`).

- In the one-step construction the fine pole picture of the annulus estimate is taken for the fine bed, the fine clip, the incoming path and the fine sides `n_i=Lm_i`; the rank picture is recorded at the coarse sides `m_i`; `pole_reduced` equates their coefficients.
