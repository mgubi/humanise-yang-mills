### M2. The nearest-point projection of the group is used in three chapters without a proof or a reference (A-I-2 and D-I-1, both labelled GAP). Status: CONFIRMED; manuscript, pre-existing.

Location: `predictor-branch.tex` lines 13–19 and 86–89; `seed-geometry.tex` lines 112–119; `seed-energy.tex` lines 37–47.

Passages: "The analytic nearest-point projection \(\pi\) near \(1\) has \(D\pi(1)=\Pi\)."; "The group has a uniform nearest-point tube, obtained from the local tube by left translation."; "Shrinking the ball also ensures that every nearest point is this local projection."; "The local tubular projection in the group data is analytic, equals the identity on the group near \(1\), and has derivative \(\Pi\) at \(1\)."

Verified reasoning. Four facts about a closed subgroup \(G\subset U(N)\) with the real Frobenius metric are used: (a) there is an analytic map \(\pi\) from an ambient neighbourhood of \(1\) to \(G\), equal to the identity on \(G\), with \(D\pi(1)=\Pi\); (b) for \(Z\) near \(1\), every point of \(G\) nearest to \(Z\) equals \(\pi(Z)\); (c) by left translation the neighbourhood is a uniform tube around \(G\); (d) the second derivative of \(\pi\) is bounded near \(1\). Fact (b) is what identifies the minimizing frame entries \(h_i\) with \(\pi(m_i)\) (`predictor-branch.tex` line 45, `seed-geometry.tex` line 133) and makes the predictor minimum unique (line 88–90); it does not follow from the existence of a local analytic retraction. A search of `manuscript/` for "tubular", "nearest-point" and "nearest point" finds assertions only, no construction and no citation (`validation.md`). `seed-energy.tex` calls the projection part of "the group data"; the group data of the source are a closed subgroup only, and the projection is a derived result there (`lean.md`, L2). Given (a)–(d), I checked the arguments that use them and they are valid: the contraction \(v\le v\varepsilon+\tfrac14v\) of `predictor-branch.tex` lines 47–64 (each step re-derived, including \(\|m-1\|\le2\varepsilon\), \(\|m_i-1\|\le3\varepsilon<\delta\), \(\Pi(m_h-1)=0\) and the use of `eq:branch-projection` on the convex ball); the first-power estimate `eq:geometry-cluster` with \(C_G=\max\{2,2/a,2/\sigma\}\); and `eq:seed-curvature-bound` with \(K=C^2D^2\). The statements are true (they are the tubular-neighbourhood theorem for a compact embedded analytic submanifold, and the source proves them), so this is a missing justification, not a false step. The group may be disconnected or finite; the reference chosen must cover that case.

Required: one background lemma, proved or cited with a precise locator, stating (a)–(d), and a reference to it from the three chapters. The sketch in report A (the chart \((X,N)\mapsto\exp(X)(1+N)\), with the normality condition \(u^*Z-1\in\mathfrak g^\perp\) for a nearest point \(u\)) is the source's construction.
