Author commit `00d5d5a` incorrectly replaced the local window with the global one; the subsequent correction supersedes it and explicitly distinguishes both. For `R = floor(radiusB(x)/20) ≥ 2`, the parent anchors themselves are endpoints and all endpoints lie within distance one of their anchors by `meetings`. Thus the panel contains the anchor ball of radius `R−2` and is contained in the anchor ball of radius `R`. These inclusions justify the generic `B,B+2` bounds with `B=R−2`. The separately used conservative radius `floor(radiusB/60)` follows from `boards` on the `radiusB≥120` tail. The generic panel-inverse argument needs ball inclusion, not an existential endpoint condition. Added the distinction and both radius arguments; independent correspondence and mathematical review is required.
