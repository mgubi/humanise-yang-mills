## Findings that need action

1. **The packet repair is incomplete in its own chapter (inaccuracy; part 3, M1, M2; framing from PR #96).** `packet-estimates.tex` 275–296: the derivative bound `eq:packet-jet-price` and the sentence before it still name the physical packet \(\mathcal P_A\); what is differentiated is the stock extension (source: `Clutch.chimney` bounds `Polar.stock`). Lines 136–141 and 209–252 say the \(500B\) health condition is needed only to identify the extension with the physical packet; in the source the value bound of the extension itself uses it (`Clutch.dinner`), and the jet is taken at the base point (`Clutch.atV`). The closing sentence "applies separately at every point of the allowed variation" therefore holds only at points where health and manifestness hold; health is a closed condition and manifestness is not preserved along the variation.
2. **The same repair is missing in a sibling chapter (inaccuracy; part 3, M3).** `inherited-activity-prices.tex` 218–229: "There is no moving coarse kept-row indicator in this term" is true of the unmasked extension (`Polar.nest`) only. The actual term carries the kept test, and the two are identified only under the room and health conditions (`Polar.albedo`). The records do not list this as open.
3. **A conclusion that a consumer needs is not in the statement (inaccuracy; part 1).** `actual-global-row-prices.tex`: the bound \(\lVert Q\rVert_{\sigma/2}\le L_0^2\) appears only inside the proof (line 115); the C13 chapter `actual-local-compiler-handoff.tex` (67–80) uses it. In the same chapter (15–16, 34–37, 91–93, 254) the selected rows are asserted to be clear of flags without derivation, "active" and the stencil radius are not defined, and \(R=\lfloor B/100000\rfloor\) is defined and never used; the missing argument is short (source `small_span`, `guarded`). This is the one gap of part 1.
4. **Hypothesis and citation in the new logarithm (inaccuracy; part 2, F2, F3; F3 introduced by PR #96).** `compensated-variation.tex` 27–29 omits that \(g\) is a group element; with the new logarithm the identity is false otherwise (source `Canary.right` quantifies over group elements). No later step is affected. Lines 17–19 "write the element as \(\exp(u)\)" uses that every group element near the identity is the exponential of a small Lie algebra element, which is the closed-subgroup theorem (source `YM.Gauge.isolates`), not an elementary fact; cite it as `predictor-branch` does.
5. **A sketched estimate that another chapter relies on (gap, minor; part 2, F1).** `gaussian-differentiation.tex` 79–100 only sketches the base-point relative-density estimate that `eq:moment-ratio-jets` in `gaussian-moment-prices.tex` uses. The reviewer checked that it is true; the dependence on \(F_I\), the \(\ell^1/\ell^\infty\) pairing for the quadratic form and the absorption of constants into the scale are not written out.
6. **Smaller statement issues (parts 1, 3).** `actual-palette-warning.tex` 138–139 (introduced by PR #96) attaches the condition "local width strictly positive" to the move-row price, but only the source bound needs it; its display `cab-wedge` (341–351) uses \(C\) and \(L\) undefined. `packet-estimates.tex` 111–119 describes the two frame-tag branches the wrong way round relative to `RG.Patch.frameMass` (the bound is unaffected). `packet-annulus.tex` 15–20 calls \(100(1+B)\le c\) an eventual inequality; it is a hypothesis on the period (`Works.fits`). `sector-gram.tex` 5–6 and 249–251 still calls the transported-flat matrix "actual".
7. **Notation across consecutive chapters (part 2, F4).** `haar-chart.tex` 85 attributes \(t=P/\sqrt h\) to `eq:warning-scales`, where that quantity is called \(\tau\) and \(t\) is another threshold; \(d\) is both the fixed chart radius and the growing scale \(1+B+(\log h)^2\); \(h\) is both the Haar measure and the coupling.
8. **Records (parts 1, 3).** `ledger.md` line 59 omits several premises of `chimney` and places the zero-germ case split in `dinner`; the records repeat the health framing of finding 1; `ledger.md` line 43 is stale after the C13 author pass; the canonical evidence file of the global row prices still says the sector-Gram chapter supplies the coercivity, and that of the palette chapter says the global unit "passed independent review" and gives wrong paths; the author snapshot `35173bd` is not in the repository and cannot be checked. One cross-package note for C13: `actual-local-compiler-handoff.tex` 26–33 credits `sec:warning-safety` with inequalities that are the palette chapter's `cab-wedge` (they do imply the warning-safety conditions; the reference is wrong, the step is not).

Nits are in the part files. The Jaffard reference in `local-covariance.tex` is bibliographically correct; its locator ("Prop. 2, p. 464") was not verified against the paper.
