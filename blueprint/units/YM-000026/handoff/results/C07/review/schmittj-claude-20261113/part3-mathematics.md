### M2. `packet-estimates.tex` lines 136-141, 209-252 and 295-296: the healthy-neighbourhood hypothesis is presented as needed only for identifying the extension with the physical packet; it is also used in the extension's own value bound, and "every point of the allowed variation" needs that hypothesis at each point. INACCURACY (manuscript; framing introduced by PR #96, line 295-296 pre-existing).

Quoted: lines 138-141 "The latter is the eligibility rule of the locally smooth stock extension used in the local jet argument; its equality with the physical packet also uses the funded room and manifest-sector conditions and a healthy neighbourhood through radius at least $500B$"; lines 249-251 "The last inequality in \eqref{eq:packet-local-jet-inputs} is the closed support version just explained"; lines 295-296 "This argument applies separately at every point of the allowed variation, with the same constants."

Reasoning (checked against the Lean text): the last line of \eqref{eq:packet-local-jet-inputs}, $e^{\phi(y)+\ell n}\le e^{(Cd^5-g)n}$, is a bound on the extension's exponent at $y$. The extension's exponent uses the unmasked rows (`Polar.plumeLog`, built from `rootface`), whereas the energy bound \eqref{eq:packet-assigned-energy} and the value argument of lines 133-167 concern the clipped rows of $\Phi_A$ (`Coat.steamLog`, built from `quoteNear`). In `Clutch.dinner` (`TowerPower.lean`) the value bound for the extension is obtained only after rewriting `plumeLog` as `steamLog` by `Polar.sectorPass`, which needs `Room`, `X.manifest`, $l\ge500$ and `Healthy (temp X.x) (A.dome (l*X.B)) X.V`; the same health hypothesis also gives the absence of actual flags on the $300B$ dome used for the profile masks (`mistB`). Moreover `Clutch.atV : U x = X.V` places the jet at the base point of the variation. Health is a closed, not an open, condition (`RG.flags` uses a strict inequality `t < chord`, so `Healthy` asks all touching chords to be at most `t`), and manifestness `X.seeds.flags = flags (temp X.x) X.V` is not preserved when only the coarse field is varied (`vary` keeps the seeds). Hence lines 295-296 are correct only at those points of the variation at which health, manifestness and the other hypotheses hold, the estimate being re-based there. `activity-jet-assembly.tex` line 140-141 does state that the packet's full enlarged support must be healthy "to apply its packet jet estimate", so no downstream statement is false; the defect is that `packet-estimates.tex` itself does not list this hypothesis for the jet bound.

Requested change: in lines 209-212 state that the jet estimate is at a base field for which the packet's $500B$ neighbourhood is healthy and the funded-room and manifest-sector conditions hold, and that these are used both for the value bound of the extension's exponent (identification of its unmasked rows with the clipped rows of $\Phi_A$) and for the identification with $\mathcal P_A$. Replace "at every point of the allowed variation" by "at every point of the variation at which these hypotheses hold".
