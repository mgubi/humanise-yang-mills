# C13 common-tuple audit (author draft)

Source revision: `3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464`.
This is a guide to the checked dependencies, not an independent review or a
claim that the history-level matching has been proved.

The proposed common data are the gauge, patch and its funded tube, branch
and Haar-chart choices, teller, purse and positive reserves, and then the
actual short-course step and its fine representation. The path and clip
used by the tube must be those of that representation. The station coupling
interval, threshold, palette, owner, Tissue and remainder receipts have to
refer to that same step. Constants and the eventual station threshold are
chosen uniformly before the period, field, forest, tags and marked family,
as specified by the individual suppliers. In particular a certificate for
one posed reader is not the all-matched-pilot palette receipt.

## Cell and weak-comparison quantifiers

`YM.Cells.CellHyp.centered` in `Lean/YM/Cells/Packing.lean` first fixes a
level d, positive epsilon and a positive norm radius R; it then chooses one
m₀ and one nonnegative A. The same A works for every m at least m₀. After
m and an arbitrary positive slack eta are fixed, the conclusion holds
`f.late` in the ordered extraction, uniformly for all grade-d labels in the
R-ball and all lattice sites, including the interior and root-test bound.
The late cutoff may depend on m and eta; it is not asserted uniform in them.
`YM.Spec.Seed` in `Lean/YM/Spec/Closed.lean` carries that hypothesis with
`Makes`, ordered and point limits, hierarchy and rank widths on its one
family, extraction and table. Refinement preserves the cell hypothesis via
`YM.Cells.recur`, but that fact alone does not identify an independently
chosen table or calibration.

The C13 three-panel estimate is uniform in the limiting insertion tuple and
in smooth tests supported in fixed K_R with fixed sup bound, conditional on
its three panel receipts and common readout. The chapter's compactness
argument for the cutoff-dependent insertions is performed for each fixed
comparison depth and each fixed test; it does not establish a single late
cutoff uniform over all tests. This distinction must be respected when
matching C14's uniform-test supplier and its normalization, rather than
silently exchanging a late cutoff with a test quantifier.

For the filtration edge, `YM.OPE.Columns.limit_lower` is a precise
conditional bridge: fix `e < d`, a nontrivial filter b finer than the same
`f.fuse`, and a cutoff-label family converging to x along b whose coordinates
lie eventually in the low-e subspace. Under its `TableTickets`, simplicity,
same-table `Base` and derivative-plan inclusions, the projected limit
`S.project d x` belongs to the range of the derivative inclusion from
grade e. The proof replaces the family outside its eventual low and bounded
set by zero before invoking `belongs`; it does not claim the original family
has those properties uniformly on the whole fused filter. This bridge is not
by itself the full C05 filtration theorem, which also needs its projection
and test-kernel correspondence on that same data.

The finite cumulant comparison also has a literal common-coordinate
requirement. `YM.Next.Rank.Particle.acuity`
(`Lean/YM/MergeProof/West/Teddy.lean`) takes equalities identifying the
original covariance and mean with `Normal.scope (perSlot M) Q` and
`Normal.reads (perSlot M) μ`. Its nearby-to-free hypothesis applies to the
physical block of that same Q and μ, while `Sorts` bounds the full enlarged
pair. `TollsX.mark` separately supplies the exact low-grade identity of
each substituted polynomial, and `TollsX.upper` pays its weighted tail.
Thus separately chosen per-slot Gaussian laws or entrywise row errors without
an absolute row-sum bound do not instantiate this theorem.
`Extraction.Order.late` (`Lean/YM/Extraction/Orders.lean`) makes its
ordering explicit: an eventual period-index threshold comes first, followed
by an eventual mesh-index threshold which may depend on that fixed period.
`Order.upper` adds an arbitrary positive slack outside `late`. Consequently
“eventually in the ordered filter” should not be read as a uniform cutoff
across every period or every test.

At the local-course entry, `Opacity.tabloid` is literally constructed from
P and `tunnel B N i p`, the canonical chi, chart ratio J, stage dressing,
`Paint.inbox` and a choice of the step's reference tag
(`Opacity/Forestage.lean`). `fitPost` uses that record with the actual
binding, `Makes`, `ShowHours`, `Marquee`, `Corridor`, `Bush`, and interval
inequalities. Its proof reads the binding's adjacent paths, floors, crossings,
stack and sworn data; it also uses the input Bush's exact path and clip.
The internal reference-tag choice is a field of this step record, not a
license to replace the family or select another run. The history-level
producer still has to identify these inputs on the selected course.

For C14's trace supplier, `eq:ex-lattice-trace-test-error` in draft PR #98
requires at comparison index k every arity at most k and every label tuple
of grades at most k, with error `(k+1)⁻¹ M` times the product of label norms.
For each fixed test, its finite-cutoff `upper` receipt must hold on a late
set belonging to the same subordinate ultrafilter; the mesh tail itself need
not be simultaneous over tests. C13's fixed-word integral inequality supplies
the needed sup-norm form only after its readouts, scaling radius and table
are identified with that lift. The source's `pay_word`/`supply_wheel` use
finite-basis expansion and finite intersections to obtain a common station
for bounded arity and grade with the product-of-label-norms factor; the
manuscript-level exact-index and normalization match remains to be shown.

The merged C07 statement can be compared to C13 without changing its input
scale: substitute C07's x by C13's recording parameter y = r(x), so both
use B = ceil((log y)^2). The candidate match uses the identical funded tube,
patch/branch, period, store with flags at `temp(y)`, forest and tag. C07's
actual derivative Gram Q then supplies the active block; adding identity on
inactive coordinates gives the C13 completed precision with gap at least
`min(η, 1)`. The C07 proposition's ordered window and its symmetric
covariance still have to be matched to C13's window convention and rates,
and all eventual thresholds must be intersected on the same half-interval.
This is a conditional index crosswalk from a remote, merged but not
independently reviewed producer; the combined tree has not been checked.
