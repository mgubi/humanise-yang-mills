### F1. INACCURACY (moderate) — `actual-local-compiler-handoff.tex` lines 22–24 and 261–265 — manuscript — pre-existing (not touched by PR #103)

Quoted: "Here $a_{\rm coast}$ is the incoming coefficient in the small-remainder budget." and "The correction construction adds this genuine remainder to the alphabet and pays it using $2a_{\rm coast}\leq K/4$."

Verified reasoning: In the source, `a_coast` is the field `a` of `YM.Next.Rank.Demand` (`Lean/YM/Next/RANK/Bird.lean`), and the large-field allowance is `gas c u = exp(-c.a * hot u ^ 2)` (same file, line 35). This is the forced large-field gas allowance $G(u)$ of `actual-mixed-marked-compiler.tex` lines 251, 266, 273, not the small additive remainder. The inequality `2*i.era.coast.a ≤ K/4` (hypothesis `hl` of `Soft.accepts_stock`, `Soft/French.lean` line 41) is passed to `Soft.weathered` (`Soft/Diamond.lean` line 21, hypothesis `hv : 2*coast.a ≤ α/4` with `α := K`), which uses it only through `Wax.flint` (`Blazing.lean` line 23): `2a ≤ t` implies `max(acres x y, Line) ≤ exp(t·hot(β_F)^2)`, where `acres = max (gas x ^2)⁻¹ (gas y)⁻¹` (`Posts.lean` line 24) is the forced multiplier. Thus the inequality pays the forced inflation $G(x)^{-2}, G(y)^{-1}$ against the owner decay $K\,\mathrm{hot}^2 s_i$. The genuine additive remainder $E$ is paid instead by the `Tissue` price: `Soft.affixed` (`Soft/Hem.lean`) gives auxiliary price $e+4d$, and `Escalates.reserves` (`Soft/Nuance.lean` line 50) gives $g_0\le$ `weather`, `8·weather ≤ 1` and `Line·(exp(8·weather)−1) ≤ sailboat`; the last line of `accepts_stock` checks `g0 + 4·weather ≤ 6·weather`. `allarity-planted-compiler.tex` lines 195–208 states this correctly ("the separate purchase $2a_{\rm coast}\le\alpha/4$ pays their forced inflation"; the remainder is paid through $e+4d\le5\omega\le6\omega$ and $L_0(e^{8\omega}-1)\le\delta_{\rm step}$). The handoff therefore contradicts the planted chapter and the source on which inequality pays what. No proof step depends on the misdescription, so I classify it as INACCURACY rather than ERROR.

Status: open. Requested change: replace line 23 by "Here $a_{\rm coast}$ is the coefficient in the forced large-field allowance $G(u)=\exp(-a_{\rm coast}\mathrm{hot}(u)^2)$" and lines 264–265 by "The correction construction adds this genuine remainder to the alphabet; its price is the bound $e+4d\le6\omega$ of Section~\ref{sec:allarity-planted-compiler}, while $2a_{\rm coast}\le K/4$ pays the forced inflation against the owner decay."

### F4. INACCURACY (missing sentence) — `actual-mixed-marked-compiler.tex` lines 143–147 — manuscript — pre-existing

Quoted: "If the body set is empty, compatibility forces the background set to be empty as well. A background packet has a nonempty fine inventory, whereas compatibility identifies the union of those inventories with the union supplied by the body letters."

Verified reasoning: The inventory identity is a property of the whole compatible collection, while the claim is about one connected component $C$. To conclude that a component with no body letter has no background packet, one needs that the body letter whose inventory contains a site of a background inventory lies in the same component, i.e. that the connection relation links letters whose inventories (or supports containing them) meet. The forced case in lines 138–139 uses the same per-component reading. The statement is very likely true in the source's connection relation (the evidence file cites `Display.refund` in `Dockets.lean` for "absence of background packets without body packets"), but the manuscript does not say why. Status: open. Requested change: add one sentence stating the connection rule and why inventory overlap implies adjacency.
