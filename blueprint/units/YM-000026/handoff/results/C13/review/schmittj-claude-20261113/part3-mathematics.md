### M1. GAP — forced realignment cites a regrouping estimate whose stated form does not give the displayed budget

- File: `manuscript/existence/allarity-marked-propagation.tex`, lines 112–146 (`eq:allarity-realign-forced-input`, `eq:allarity-realign-forced-output`). Pre-existing; not touched by PR #103. Manuscript.
- Passage: "Apply the exact regular/forced regrouping of Section~\ref{sec:soft-log-fusion} to the enlarged supports. … To apply the canonical regrouping estimate, the old forced pieces contribute $e^g s$ and the newly moved pieces contribute at most $n Z R_{e+1}$ … These are the two terms of its local inflation budget."
- Verified reasoning: `sec:soft-log-fusion` (`soft-log-fusion.tex` lines 7–60, 123–166) is stated for real-valued hard activities $K_i$ and real logarithms $\ell_j$, with factors $r_j=e^{\ell_j}-1$, the hypothesis $|\ell_j|\le v_j\le1$, and inflated budget $\Gamma=e^\kappa b+2w$. Applied literally with $\kappa=g$, $b=s$, the moved complementary pieces would enter either as hard labels (price $e^g nZR_{e+1}$) or as logarithmic labels (price $2nZR_{e+1}$, and only under $v_j\le1$). Neither gives $e^gs+nZR_{e+1}$. The displayed budget is nevertheless the one of the pinned source: `YM.Next.Rank.Tapes.West.resFt` (`Lean/YM/MergeProof/West/Prince.lean`) has the hypotheses `Real.exp g * s + m*Z*rentQ q (c+1) ≤ δ` and `((B+B+1))^4*δ ≤ 1` with output `Real.exp (-g)*(Real.exp δ-1)`. The missing factor $2$ is explained by the marker algebra: a moved piece is $z_Ih$ with $z_I^2=0$, so $\exp_*(z_Ih)-1=z_Ih$ exactly and the bound $|e^x-1|\le2|x|$ (with its $v_j\le1$ hypothesis) is not needed. That marker-valued variant, and the identification of the complementary pieces as logarithmic labels satisfying the vanishing hypothesis `eq:fusion-vanishing` (they are supported where the enlarged support meets a flag), are not stated. The conclusion is true, but the cited result does not supply it as written.
- Requested change: state the marker-valued version of the regrouping estimate used (nilpotent markers, factor $r_j=z_Ih_j$, budget $e^\kappa b+w$ at exponent $e+1$, output $e^{-\kappa}(e^\Gamma-1)$ under $(2B+1)^4\Gamma\le1$), either here or as a remark in `sec:soft-log-fusion`, and explain why the moved pieces satisfy its vanishing hypothesis.

### M3. INACCURACY — closedness of the actual mean and kernel is used but not stated in the final hypothesis

- File: `manuscript/existence/allarity-coordinate-calibration.tex`, lines 168–176. Pre-existing. Manuscript.
- Passage: "Let $\Gamma_{ij}$ be the actual symmetric cochain kernel and $\mu_i$ its actual mean. Suppose the means obey the two bounds of the previous subsection with constants $P,E$, and $\Gamma_{ij}-K^0_{ij}$ obeys their two-endpoint versions."
- Verified reasoning: the residual bound (lines 111–113) uses that the origin differences of $h=f-\Pi_df$ below order $q$ vanish, and this holds only for closed $f$: for a nonclosed cochain $\Pi_df$ need not reproduce its origin differences. The source includes them: `YM.Next.Rank.Beamer.Gather` and `YM.Next.Rank.Beamer.Vane` (`Lean/YM/Next/RANK/ArcEstimate.lean`) contain `shutter` for the mean, and for every row and column of the kernel. The hypothesis should still be explicit, because the chapter opens by stressing that only symmetry of the covariance is used (lines 7–8).
- Requested change: add "closed" for $\mu_i$, and "with closed rows and columns" for $\Gamma_{ij}$, in lines 168–171.

### M4. INACCURACY — parameters $d,b,a$ and the role of the purchase $6n+3\le a/(3+2n)$ in the one-cell argument

- File: `manuscript/existence/allarity-marked-propagation.tex`, lines 13–51. Lines 13–16 changed by PR #103 (correct; see below); the rest is pre-existing. Manuscript.
- Passage: "Assume distinct replica cells have reader separation strictly greater than $dB$, and $(3+2n)\rho\le dB\le bB$. … take the integer slice radius supplied by the local expansion, bounded by $aB/(3+2n)$ … The purchase $6n+3\le a/(3+2n)$ is the separate local room condition needed to produce these elementary slices. Once they are available, the argument above uses $(3+2n)\rho\le aB$ and the strict separation of the replica cells."
- Verified reasoning: the argument itself is valid. Two readers on one piece are at distance at most $\rho$ (this needs the inclusion added by PR #103). Readers on adjacent pieces are at distance at most $2\rho$. A cycle-erased label path has at most $n-1$ edges, so readers are at distance at most $2(n-1)\rho$ and every support point is within $(2n-1)\rho\le(2n+1)\rho\le dB$ of every reader. Remarks: (i) only separation $>2\rho$ is used, not $>dB$; (ii) $b$ is never used or defined, and $d$ is silently replaced by $a$ in line 48; (iii) in the source the elementary slice radius is $3B_K$ (`e_gress`, from `roomTest`), and the purchase `hd : 6*n+3 ≤ a/(3+2*n)` in `YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.Display.s_ectors` (`Lean/YM/MergeProof/West/Cabaret.lean`) is used only in the final step, to bound $n\cdot6B_K+3B_K$ by $(a/(3+2n))B_K$. So the source conclusion is distance $\le(a/(3+2n))B_K$, stronger than the manuscript's $\le aB$. The manuscript does not say which "required enlarged-support bounds" the consumer needs, so it is undetermined whether the weaker conclusion suffices. The consumer `resFt` uses `Carport (a*P.p.B) (S i) (q*P.p.B)`, which suggests $aB$ is enough, but I did not open `Carport` to confirm this.
- Requested change: name the required bound and its consumer; drop the unused $b$; replace the hypothesis "$>dB$" by what is used ($>2\rho$), or say that it is stronger; and describe the purchase as giving the final distance bound, not as producing the slices.

### M6. INACCURACY — conversion between marker weight $r\le1$ and rank-root coefficient price $a=e^{2I_k}\ge1$ not stated

To decide: read the consumer that instantiates `rank-root-normalization` (e.g. `restarted-rank-history.tex`, reviewed by another C13 reader) and check that the replicated coefficient there is that of the unamplified label with weight $a=rH^{U}$.
