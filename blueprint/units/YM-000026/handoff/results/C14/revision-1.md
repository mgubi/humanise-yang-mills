For the free table, the correlation of a derivative label is the derivative of the correlation; hence \(\phi_i(D_0w)=\sum_u\partial_uS_w(ii';u0;e_0)\)." If \(z\cdot A=0\), then \(A_a(z)=\int_0^1t\,z_iF_{ia}(tz)\,dt\). Its degree-zero part is \(\tfrac12z_iF_{ia}(0)\) and its degree-one part is \(\tfrac13z_kz_r\partial_rF_{ka}(0)\).
