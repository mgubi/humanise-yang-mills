## Findings, ordered by severity

### F1. GAP (manuscript, `actual-stress-annulus.tex` lines 184-185, 376-377, 402-411; made visible by PR #98, quantifier problem partly pre-existing)

Passage (lemma, new text): "Fix an opened reference level $p_0$ and level $k$ as in the shell-node receipt, with $p_0$ at least the reference depth of every watch below." Passage (proposition): "Assume the hypotheses of Lemma~\ref{lem:ex-lattice-annulus-annihilation}, with the shell-node receipt available for every finite family of watches. … For any further watch $I$, apply the result to the union of $I$ and the faithful family."

Reasoning. The proposition proof applies the lemma to the family $\{I\}\cup\mathcal T$ for an arbitrary further watch $I$. The lemma (after PR #98, correctly) requires a fixed opened pair $(p_0,k)$ with $p_0$ dominating the reference depth of every watch in the family, because the centered-cell spectator bound is only available at depth at most the opening (Lean `YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.acorns`, hypothesis `I.t ≤ k`; `stencil_pay` hypothesis `∀ i, (I i).t ≤ q`). An arbitrary watch $I$ may have depth larger than any $p_0$ fixed in advance, and nothing in the lemma, the definition of the receipt or the proposition asserts that every reference level is opened at some level $k\ge\operatorname{ref}(D)$. The receipt definition only says "for every fixed opened reference level $p_0$ and level $k$ with $p_0$ open at $k$", i.e. it is vacuous unless openings exist. The Lean proof does not have this problem: `YM.MergeProof.Acme.zone` (West/Ozone.lean) takes `Q := sup_i (X i).t`, obtains `k₀` from `Stock.Tabs.mayOpen L 1 hv.opens Q` (the `opens : Stock.Tabs` field of `Aroma`), sets `k := max k₀ (C.ref D)`, and only then calls `stencil_pay` and `fees`; `annular_fieldA` carries `HA : Aroma C`, so the opening is chosen per family. The same per-family dependence affects the lemma's other family-specific hypotheses (the separated-point majorants with constants $C_a$, the cloud degrees $c_a$ and the choice of $q_*$): "assume the hypotheses of the lemma" must be read for every finite family, which the proposition does not say for these hypotheses (pre-existing).

Status: open. Requested change (smallest repair): in the lemma replace "Fix an opened reference level $p_0$ and level $k$" by "Assume every reference level is opened at some level (the stock receipt); for the given family choose $p_0$ at least the maximal watch depth and $k\ge\operatorname{ref}(D)$ with $p_0$ open at $k$"; in the proposition state that the stock receipt, the separated-point majorants and the receipt hold for every finite family of watches. Record the stock receipt as an input in the records (see R1).

### F2. INACCURACY bordering on GAP (interface; `actual-stress-annulus.tex` lines 163-167, 443-448, 453-455 introduced by PR #98; `actual-stress-shell-nodes.tex` lines 407-443 pre-existing)

Passage (annulus, new): "Proposition~\ref{prop:ex-lattice-actual-shell-node} supplies this shell-node input from the stated same-history menu, transfer, quota and price receipts. The coefficient bound in that proposition has exactly the birth-level dependence allowed here."

Reasoning. The second sentence is correct (see "What is correct" below). The first sentence is not literally true of the statements as written. Definition `def:ex-lattice-shell-receipt` demands (a) that the fragment lies in the prescribed chamber, (b) $p_0\le N$ on the late set, (c) the scale note $\log r_m/\lambda_m\to-1$, (d) the conclusion for every extraction order $\mathfrak f$ encoding any finite family of watches, with the scale sequence chosen before $p_0,k$, labels and $\mathfrak f$. The proposition's displayed conclusion contains only the two norm bounds; chamber membership is argued in the text at lines 368-370 but is not in the statement; $p_0\le N$ is not stated; the scale note is not mentioned at all (in Lean it is the separate theorem `YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.fugacious` and the separate field `Aroma.notes`, not part of `Economics`); and the proposition speaks of "the selected volume and period filters" and "the finite extraction test family" rather than an arbitrary extraction order. Moreover the proposition's quantifier order is muddled (pre-existing): "Assume … $m+1<j_m<N$ … $\mathcal O_{N,q}(z,0)$ … lie in the centered chamber … $32r_m\le a_{p_0}$ …" are stated as plain hypotheses about given $m,N,q$ before "There is $m_0$ such that for $m\ge m_0$ … for filter-almost every pair" quantifies $m$ and $(N,q)$. In the source these are not hypotheses of the final statement: `YM.MergeProof.Ribbons.laurelE` derives the lightness and price gates eventually in $m$, and `bricks` derives the chamber memberships and placement on a late set (`near_center`, `faraway`) before calling `sales`. The Lean producer `Economics.fees` therefore does deliver items (a), (b), (d) for every `F : Extraction.Order`; only (c) comes from elsewhere. So nothing false is used, but the manuscript statement that is cited does not match the definition it is said to supply.

Status: open; introduced as a claim by PR #98 (before the PR the annulus said the producer was absent). Requested change: restate the proposition's conclusion in exactly the form of the definition (chamber membership, $p_0\le N$, every extraction order), move the chamber/room/placement conditions into the proof as eventual consequences of the cited receipts (or state them as eventual receipts), and either remove the scale note from the definition or cite its producer next to the proposition.
