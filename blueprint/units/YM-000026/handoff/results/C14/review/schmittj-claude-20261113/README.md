## Findings that need action

1. **The shell-to-annulus join is not closed (gap; part 4, F1 and F2; made visible by PR #98).** `actual-stress-annulus.tex` 184–185 fixes one opened level \(p_0\) "at least the reference depth of every watch below", and the proposition at 376–411 applies the lemma to arbitrarily enlarged finite families of watches. Nothing in the text provides an opening at every depth; the source obtains one per family (`Aroma.opens`, `Stock.Tabs.mayOpen`, used in `zone`). The family-dependent separated-point bounds have the same dependence. Separately, the new sentence at line 163 says the shell-node proposition "supplies this shell-node input", but that proposition's statement omits chamber membership, \(p_0\le N\), arbitrary extraction orders and the scale note, and its per-\((N,q)\) hypotheses precede the choice of \(m_0\). State the opening receipt as an input per family, and bring the shell-node statement to the form consumed.
2. **Finite-level results sketched and not proved (gap; part 2, G1, G2).** `physical-ward-propagation.tex` 397–415 and 449–456 describe, without proof, the local response laws (the commutation relation, the unit and mirror rules) and the two readout limits \(\xi^{-1}rU^{-1}pU\to\kappa\partial^0\), \(\xi^{-1}U^{-1}YU\to\kappa C\). `physical-angular-actions.tex` 41–64 uses those limits as stated. At line 495, "Since \(p\ne0\)" has no justification in the text; in the source it follows from the readout limit with \(\kappa\ne0\). The propagation evidence file says the manuscript proves A01–A08, which it does not.
3. **Hypotheses of the source missing from statements (part 1, F1, F2, F6; part 3, items 2 and 4).** `trace-free-rank.tex` 292–318 needs derivative compatibility (source hypothesis `V.Derivatives`) and, at 70–82, injectivity of curl on radial potentials. `finite-rank-kernel-transport.tex` 25–34 needs \(U\) to be \(J\)-invariant. `actual-equation-kernel-relay.tex` 11–22 and 59–61 presents as supplied two inputs of `prop:ex-marked-solaris` that are open: the floor/delay schedule condition (recorded as open in C12) and the slow-reserve hypotheses. `actual-insertion-derivative-relay.tex` 95–98 calls \(J_N\le\hat\beta_N\) "bought"; in the source it is a hypothesis discharged by the caller, and no manuscript producer was found.
4. **A false auxiliary sentence (inaccuracy; part 3, item 3).** `actual-equation-kernel-relay.tex` 249–264: "two points in one cell have physical separation at most \(a_k<\delta\)". A four-dimensional cube of side \(a_k\) has Euclidean diameter \(2a_k\), and the step uses pairwise separation of all insertions, which is not stated. Take \(a_k<\delta/2\) (or state the norm) and state pairwise separation.
5. **A limit misdescribed (inaccuracy; part 3, item 1; also noted in mgubi's C15 review).** `trace-firesky-gallery.tex` 223–233 says the zoomed scalar observable converges "to the prescribed multiple of the unit". In the source (`YM.Trace.Over.Scribe.gallery`, `goal`) only the weighted coefficients converge, and the zoomed scalar tends to a multiple of the curvature-square field, not of the unit. The table hypotheses (`T.Hierarchy`, `T.respects S`) are also missing.
6. **A wrong attribution (inaccuracy; part 2).** `physical-ward-free-classification.tex` 115–123 obtains a strict inequality from reflection positivity, which gives only \(\ge0\); the strictness is the content of `thm:ex-lattice-all-grade-free-detector` and should be cited from there. In the same chapter, `thm:free-collinear-scenes` (426–431) merges two source results with different thresholds and omits a conclusion that the propagation chapter uses (the kernel on the spin subspace is \(\mathbb R\partial\)).
7. **Statements to complete (parts 1, 2, 4).** Unproved assertions on the table and reference packaging in `free-maxwell-ward-flux.tex` 281–310 and an unspecified drive in its Ward identity (205–224); the direction of the separation in `all-grade-free-detectors.tex` (the sign \((-1)^k\) is right only for one convention); several unstated packet bounds and sign hypotheses in the propagation chapter (the reviewers derived them); the factor 2 in quota \(\le2e_m\) in the shell-node chapter; "cofinal" where the source has "eventually", a mixed-space identity and drifting notation in `trace-calibration-producer.tex`, which bears on the instruction to keep one comparison curve; the trace theorem does not state the uniform test estimate that the normalisation chapter uses.
8. **Records (part 4, R1–R4; parts 1, 2).** The lists of annulus inputs omit the opening receipt, the scale note and `C.Makes`; "repaired" overstates the state of the shell-to-annulus join; the factor-degree change is called terminology but redefines the exponent; several evidence files cite names or paths that do not exist (`YM.Euclid.Comparator.shellSector` for `YM.Maxwell.shellSector`; `YM.Maxwell.Free.Hidden.exposed` for `YM.Maxwell.Free.exposed`; a nonexistent `Extract.lean`).
