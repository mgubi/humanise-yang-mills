## Findings, by severity

1. **Adequacy of the public statement: the lattice clause can be met by insertions that never read the configuration (`adequacy.md`).** Starting from the constructed witness, one obtains another witness of `JointExistence` whose lattice insertions are constants: a second phase with correlations \(2M-S\) compensates in the mixture, and two added grade-zero channels keep the common OPE. This works because the public `Hierarchy.correlation` is not required to be multilinear in the fields, non-selected phases carry no positivity requirement, and the Wilson measure enters only through `correlation none`. **Update: now formalised.** `formal/Adequacy.lean` proves the Proposition in Lean 4 from the public specification alone (no `sorry`, standard axioms only), and `formal/AdequacyFull.lean` proves in the full project that the body of `JointExistence` holds for every simple gauge group with a family whose insertions do not depend on the configuration (`YangMills.jointExistence_with_constant_insertions`); see `formal/README.md`. The remaining caveats concern interpretation, not correctness of the construction, and the note is still **not independently reviewed**. History of the checks before formalisation: the argument was an author-draft mathematical proof. On the author side it was proposed by one Codex pass, re-verified clause by clause by the author agent, and then given to two further agents (one Claude Opus, one Codex `gpt-6-astra`) instructed only to refute it; neither found a violated clause, and their three cosmetic corrections are incorporated. These are checks by the author's own tools, not independent review. The Lean proof that this sentence originally called for has since been carried out (`formal/`). The finding concerns the public statement as a specification only: it shows no defect in the Lean proof, and the detailed theorem `YangMills.constructed` does not admit this construction, since there correlations are multilinear, every phase carries positivity, and the insertions are specific lattice objects (`adequacy.md` (d)). The construction reuses the genuine selected theory, so it does not exhibit a different theory satisfying the statement. `statement.tex` reproduces the Lean statement faithfully, this weakness included. Identically zero insertions, by contrast, are excluded (`adequacy.md` (b)).
2. **No sentence of `statement.tex` claims more mathematics than `JointExistence`, and none was found to be wrong.** The quantifier order (group; one family with its regularity; then every strictly increasing period-index sequence; then a subsequence and one hierarchy), the running condition with its uniformity in the period, the ordered limit (full mesh limit at every suitable period, then the volume limit along the subsequence), the hypotheses on the group, and every item of Definition `ex-reconstructed` agree with `Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean`, `Lean/PublicWilson.lean` and `Lean/PublicTheory.lean`. Ledger A1–A8, Part B.
3. **No mass-gap claim.** The Lean statement's spectral content is `WightmanTheory.W0`: spectrum in the closed forward cone, and translation-invariant vectors form the line spanned by the vacuum. Neither `statement.tex`, `assembly.tex`, `lattice.tex` nor `trace-profiles.tex` asserts or suggests a gap, and a search of the existence manuscript for the relevant phrases found nothing. Ledger A8, end of Part B.
4. **The non-freeness clause is exactly a two-point short-distance asymptotic, and the manuscript describes it as such.** `Hierarchy.TraceTest.excludes` follows from `shortDistance` and positivity alone in the source (`YangMills.publicTrace`, `Lean/TraceBridge.lean` 35–44; `YM.TraceExtension.Wick.euclidean_excludes`). It excludes coincidence near \(r=0\) with finite combinations of the listed mass-shell profiles; it does not exclude continuous-mass generalised free fields and says nothing about higher truncated correlations. `assembly.tex` 42–58 and `trace-profiles.tex` 219–237 say this correctly. Ledger A10.
5. **One presupposition stronger than the public statement (technical).** `statement.tex` 107–110 speaks of integrals converging; `Hierarchy.Reconstructed.boundary` is stated with totalised Bochner integrals and has no integrability field, although the internal source has one (`YM.Boundary.Waves.Opens.int`). Patch P4 offers two faithful wordings. Ledger A7, C6.
6. **Words that suggest more than the public statement fixes.** (a) "Correlations" of fields: multilinearity is not a public clause (ledger A6 (c)). (b) "Trace": `Hierarchy.centeredTrace` is a bare element of the grade-four space; the public statement fixes its asymptotic coefficient but supplies no stress-tensor or Ward-identity normalisation identifying it, which exists only in the detailed theorem (`Headline.TraceExclusions.identified`). (c) "Character", "phases": `Hierarchy.Character` is a bare compact metric space and only the selected point carries a Wightman theory. (d) The label action is an arbitrary representation of the whole Poincaré group on labels; nothing publicly requires translations to fix labels. (e) The running slope is only required to be positive. Patches P5, P6. Ledger A8, C1, C2, C4.
7. **Imprecise wording** in `statement.tex`: "regular" undefined (P1); simplicity and what is not assumed of the group (P2); the stencil condition (P3); operator order in the boundary values (P4); no pointer to the penalty-indexed detailed theorem (P7).
8. **Source record.** The line of `source/build.md` reporting 9,507 manifest entries OK is not reproducible from the repository (ten `verification/*.log` files are untracked; no `.lean` file affected). Patch P8. `source-trust.md`.
