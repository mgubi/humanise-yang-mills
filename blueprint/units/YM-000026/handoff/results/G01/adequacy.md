## (a) What each public clause ties to the Wilson measure

All anchors are in `Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean` and `Lean/PublicWilson.lean`.

| Clause | What it connects (S) | What it does not supply (S, by reading every field) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wilsonLaw`, `finiteMoment` | The matrix group, the plaquette action normalised by the representation dimension and the Haar probability measure determine the lattice law; moments integrate products of the insertions against it. | Which functions the insertions are. `WilsonFamily.insertion` is chosen freely for each \((N,p,v,x)\). |
| `WilsonRegularity` | Positive running slope uniform in the period; continuity, gauge invariance and one finite stencil per token. | Non-constancy of an insertion, translation covariance in \(x\), any relation between different \((N,p)\), linearity in the token, the value of the slope. |
| `Properties.wilson` | For every token tuple the ordered limit of smeared moments is the pairing of the test with `correlation none` at the projected tags; `represents` makes `project` surjective, so every tuple of graded fields is reached. | Any relation between `correlation none` and `correlation (some selected)`. |
| `Reconstructed.root`, `.boundary`, `.axioms` | `correlation (some selected)` is the value of `continuation` at ordered imaginary times, and `continuation` has the Wightman functions of the one field system as boundary values on product tests. | Anything for `correlation none` or for `correlation (some χ)`, \(\chi\ne\chi_*\): no Hilbert space, no positivity, no continuation. |
| `Reconstructed.mixture`, `.characterContinuity`, `.fullSupport` | The \(\nu\)-average of the phase correlations is `correlation none`, pointwise at separated configurations. | Positivity of the individual phases, so one phase may compensate another. |
| `Hierarchy.correlation` (type) | A function `Option Character → (Fin n → Σ d, Field d) → Points n → ℂ`. | Linearity in the field components. No clause states that \(S^s_{\ldots,\lambda A+\mu B,\ldots}=\lambda S^s_{\ldots,A,\ldots}+\mu S^s_{\ldots,B,\ldots}\), for any \(s\), nor that the value depends on a tag only through its label. |
| `OperatorProductExpansion` | One system of channels, actors and coefficients serves the mixed state and every phase, with the stated bounds. | Identification of actors with prescribed observables; actors need not be distinct or independent; the bounds are compatible with adding channels whose contributions cancel in one state and not in another. |
| `TraceTest` | The selected two-point function of one centred grade-four field has the prescribed asymptotic. | Any statement about the mixed two-point function of that field, hence about lattice moments. |

Summary (S): the lattice measure enters `JointExistence` only through `correlation none`. The selected theory is linked to `correlation none` only by `mixture` and by the common OPE data.

## (b) Identically vanishing insertions are excluded

**Claim (I).** If `T.Properties order` holds for a hierarchy over a family `C` with `WilsonRegularity C`, then the insertions of `C` are not all identically zero.

Proof. (1) Arity zero. `finiteMoment` with \(n=0\) is the integral of the empty product, i.e. the total mass of `wilsonLaw`, which is \(1\): the prior is a product of probability measures and the density is a continuous strictly positive function on a compact space divided by its integral (the internal counterpart is an explicit instance, `IsProbabilityMeasure (law G n β)` in `Lean/YM/Wilson/Law.lean` 89, and `YangMills.law_eq` in `Lean/WilsonBridge.lean` 43–46 identifies the two laws). `smear` is then \(t(\ast)\), and the limit in `Converges` is \(\int_{\text{Points }0}t\,S^{\mathrm{mix}}_\emptyset\), where `volume` on `Points 0` is the Dirac mass (`YM.Points.volume_nil`, `Lean/YM/Space/Configurations.lean` 147, for the same space). Hence \(S^{\mathrm{mix}}_\emptyset=1\). (2) Arity one. If all insertions vanish, all smeared one-point moments vanish, so \(\int t\,S^{\mathrm{mix}}_A=0\) for every test \(t\) on \(\mathbb R^4\) and every tag \(A\) in the image of `project`, which is every tag. By `smooth`, \(S^{\mathrm{mix}}_A\equiv0\). (3) Take `remainder` with empty cluster and empty spectators, \(M=0\), \(\eta=c=1\), \(D=0\), \(\varepsilon=\tfrac12\), \(s=\) `none`. `Within` and `Clear` hold vacuously. The expansion is a finite sum of coefficients times one-point mixed correlations and vanishes by (2), so \(1=|S^{\mathrm{mix}}_\emptyset|\le r^{1/2}\) for all sufficiently small \(r>0\): a contradiction. ∎

So the clauses do interact. The construction in (c) shows that this interaction stops short of forcing the insertions to depend on the configuration.

## (c) A witness with constant insertions

**Formalised after this section was written (`formal/Adequacy.lean`, in the Variant form below; `formal/AdequacyFull.lean` for the constructed witness). Not independently reviewed.**

**Proposition.** Let \(g\) be a gauge group and suppose there is a family \(C_0\) and a hierarchy \(T_0\) over \(C_0\) with the following properties, where \(S\) denotes `T₀.correlation (some T₀.selected)`.

- (H1) `T₀.Reconstructed`, `T₀.OperatorProductExpansion` and `T₀.TraceTest` hold. (The clause `wilson` for \(T_0\) is not needed.)
- (H2) There is \(u\in\) `T₀.Field 0`, \(u\ne0\), such that for every arity \(n\), every slot \(k\), all tags in the other slots and every \(x\): \(S_{\ldots,(0,2u),\ldots}(x)=2\,S_{\ldots,(0,u),\ldots}(x)\), the displayed tags being in slot \(k\).

Then there are a family \(C\) with `WilsonRegularity C`, all of whose insertions are constant functions of the link configuration, and one hierarchy \(T\) over \(C\) such that `T.Properties p` holds for every strictly increasing \(p:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N\). In particular, for a group \(g\) satisfying `g.IsSimple` as far as (H1) and (H2) require it, the body of `JointExistence` holds for \(g\) with this \(C\), with \(q=\mathrm{id}\) for every \(p\).

**Proof.** Write \(\mathcal F_d\) for `T₀.Field d` and call elements of \(\coprod_d\mathcal F_d\) tags. Define \(m\) on tags by \(m(A)=1\) if \(A=(0,u)\) and \(m(A)=0\) otherwise. Since \(u\ne0\), \((0,2u)\ne(0,u)\) and so \(m((0,2u))=0\); also \(m\) vanishes on every tag of positive grade. For a tuple of tags put \(M_{\boldsymbol A}=\prod_im(A_i)\) (so \(M_\emptyset=1\)), regarded as a constant function on `Points n`.

*Step 1: the family.* `Token` is the type of tags (it lives in `Type`, as do the `Field d`). `period p` \(=p+1\), `denominator N` \(=N+2\); both are strictly increasing sequences of positive integers. `beta N p` \(=\log(N+2)\). `haar` is the Haar probability measure of `C₀`. `insertion N p A x U` \(=m(A)\) for all \(N,p,x,U\). The data are chosen before any \(p\) is given: they depend only on \(T_0\).

*Step 2: `WilsonRegularity`.* `running`: `mesh N` \(=(N+2)^{-1}\), so `Real.log (C.mesh N)⁻¹` \(=\log(N+2)>0\) and the quotient is exactly \(1\) for all \(N,p\); take slope \(1\). `continuous`: constant functions. `invariant`: constant functions. `localSupport`: for each token take the empty stencil; the conclusion `insertion … U = insertion … V` holds for all \(U,V\).

*Step 3: the hierarchy.* `H`, `labels`, `theory`, `Field`, `toLabel`, `continuation`, `centeredTrace` are those of \(T_0\). `project` is the identity. `Character` is a two-point space \(\{+,-\}\) with the discrete metric and its Borel structure; `phaseMeasure` gives mass \(\tfrac12\) to each point; `selected` is \(+\). The correlations are
\[S^+=S,\qquad S^-=2M-S,\qquad S^{\mathrm{mix}}=M.\]
`Channel d` is that of \(T_0\) for \(d\ge1\); for \(d=0\) it is that of \(T_0\) together with two new elements \(\alpha,\beta\). `actor` is that of \(T_0\) on old channels, and `actor 0 α` \(=u\), `actor 0 β` \(=2u\). Write \(c_a^{j,i}\) for the coefficients and \(E_{j,i}\) for the actors of \(T_0\). For a finite index type \(I\) and \(a:I\to\) tags define
\[h_a(x;o)=\prod_{k\in I}m(a_k)-\sum_{i\in\mathcal C^0_0}c_a^{0,i}(x;o)\,m((0,E_{0,i})),\]
the sum over the old grade-zero channels. `coefficient` is that of \(T_0\) on old channels, and \(c_a^{0,\alpha}=h_a\), \(c_a^{0,\beta}=-\tfrac12h_a\). These depend on \(a,x,o\) only, not on a truncation degree, a state, or spectators.

*Step 4: `wilson`.* Fix a strictly increasing \(p\), an arity \(n\), tokens \(\boldsymbol A\) and a separated test \(t\). The moment is \(\int\prod_im(A_i)\,d(\text{law})=M_{\boldsymbol A}\), the law having mass one as in (b). Suitability along the sequence: `period (p k)` \(=p(k)+1\ge k+1\to\infty\) and \(t\) has compact support, so \(t\) is suitable for all large \(k\). Rows: let `row P` \(=M_{\boldsymbol A}\int t\) for every \(P\). If \(t\) is suitable for the index \(P\), then with \(a=(N+2)^{-1}\) and \(\ell=(P+1)(N+2)\), the map \(y\mapsto a\cdot\)`valMinAbs`\((y)\) is a bijection from \(\mathbb Z/\ell\) onto the points of \(a\mathbb Z\) in \((-(P+1)/2,(P+1)/2]\), and \(t\) vanishes at every point of \((a\mathbb Z^4)^n\) outside the open cube, so
\[\texttt{smear}=M_{\boldsymbol A}\;a^{4n}\!\!\sum_{z\in(a\mathbb Z^4)^n}t(z)\;\longrightarrow\;M_{\boldsymbol A}\int t\qquad(N\to\infty),\]
by convergence of Riemann sums of a continuous compactly supported function. The sequence `row (p k)` is constant, equal to \(M_{\boldsymbol A}\int t=\int t(x)\,S^{\mathrm{mix}}_{\boldsymbol A}(x)\,dx\), since `project` is the identity. This is `Converges p …`.

*Step 5: `Reconstructed`, field by field.* `axioms`, `faithful`, `injective`, `acts`, `covers` mention only `theory` and `toLabel` and hold by (H1). `represents`: the identity is surjective. `normalized`, `fullSupport`: a probability measure on a two-point discrete space charging both points. `permutation`: holds for \(S\) by (H1); \(M\) is a product over slots, hence invariant; so \(2M-S\) is invariant. `smooth`: \(S\) is smooth on the separated set by (H1), \(M\) is constant. `characterContinuity`: every function on a discrete space is continuous. `mixture`: \(\tfrac12S+\tfrac12(2M-S)=M\). `analytic`: unchanged. `root`: refers to `correlation (some selected)` \(=S\) and the unchanged `continuation`; holds by (H1). `boundary`: unchanged.

*Step 6: `OperatorProductExpansion.coefficients`.* For old channels this is (H1). For \(\alpha\) (and then for \(\beta\), whose coefficient is smaller in modulus): fix \(I,a,M\ge0,\eta>0,\varepsilon>0\) and write \(w=w(a)\ge0\). By (H1) with \(\varepsilon/2\), for each of the finitely many old grade-zero channels \(i\) there is a threshold below which \(|c_a^{0,i}(x;o)|\le r^{-w-\varepsilon/2}\) on the cluster geometry. Below the least of these thresholds and for \(r<1\), using \(|m|\le1\) and \(1\le r^{-w-\varepsilon/2}\),
\[|h_a(x;o)|\le(1+K)\,r^{-w-\varepsilon/2}\le r^{-w-\varepsilon}\quad\text{once }r^{-\varepsilon/2}\ge1+K,\]
where \(K\) is the number of old grade-zero channels. This is the required bound \(r^{0-w-\varepsilon}\).

*Step 7: `OperatorProductExpansion.remainder`.* Fix \(a,b,M,\eta,c,\varepsilon,D\). The expansion in the new hierarchy, for a state \(s\), is the old expansion plus \(h_a\,S^s_{(0,u),b}(o,y)-\tfrac12h_a\,S^s_{(0,2u),b}(o,y)\); the grade-zero channels occur for every \(D\ge0\).

- \(s=+\). By (H2) the two new terms cancel. The remainder is the old one, bounded by (H1) for the state `some T₀.selected`.
- \(s=\) mixed. \(M_{E_{j,i},b}=m((j,E_{j,i}))M_b\) vanishes for \(j\ge1\). The new terms contribute \(h_aM_b\) because \(m((0,u))=1\), \(m((0,2u))=0\). The expansion is therefore \(\bigl[\sum_{i\in\mathcal C^0_0}c_a^{0,i}m((0,E_{0,i}))+h_a\bigr]M_b=\prod_km(a_k)\,M_b=M_{a,b}\), and the remainder is exactly \(0\). (`grouped` enumerates the slots in some order; \(M\) is a product over slots and does not depend on it.)
- \(s=-\). The remainder is linear in the correlation for fixed coefficients, so it equals \(2\cdot0-(\text{remainder for }+)\), with the same modulus as for \(+\).

In all three cases the bound \(r^{D+1-w-\varepsilon}\) holds below the threshold given by (H1), uniformly in the positions.

*Step 8: `TraceTest`.* `centered` concerns `theory`; `amplitudePositive` concerns \(g\); `shortDistance` and `excludes` concern `traceTwo`, which is defined through `correlation (some selected)` \(=S\). All hold by (H1).

Steps 2 and 4–8 give `WilsonRegularity C` and `T.Properties p` for every strictly increasing \(p\). ∎

**The hypotheses hold for the constructed witness, at the level of source text (S).** Apply `YangMills.constructed g hg 1 one_pos` (the theorem takes the group, its simplicity, the penalty and its positivity) to one fixed order, say `Order.full`; this gives `hierarchy F Q` with `Properties`, hence (H1). (H2): `YangMills.hierarchy` sets `correlation s v x := S.useAt (spectator F s) x v` (`Lean/HierarchyBridge.lean` 37); `YM.Table.useAt x v := S.classMoment T (fun i => (v i).1) x fun i => (v i).2` and `YM.Table.classMoment (d) (x) : MultilinearMap ℂ (fun i => S.Field (d i)) ℂ` (`Lean/YM/Euclid/Lift.lean` 58–59, 67). For fixed grades the correlation is therefore the value of a multilinear map at the field components, for every state `s` and every \(x\), which gives the homogeneity in (H2) for any \(u\). A non-zero \(u\in\) `Field 0`: `YM.Table.unit 0` with `YM.Table.alive 0 : S.Basic → S.unit 0 ≠ 0` (`Lean/YM/Euclid/Null.lean` 100–102); the hypothesis `S.Basic` is available as `F.room.kernel.laws`, and `YangMills.publicReconstruction` uses exactly `S.alive 0 F.room.kernel.laws` (`Lean/HierarchyBridge.lean` 85–86; `Seed.kernel : S.Hierarchy` in `Lean/YM/Spec/Closed.lean` 24, `Hierarchy.laws : S.Basic` in `Lean/YM/Euclid/Words.lean` 142). I did not elaborate these terms; that `F.room.kernel.laws` has type `S.Basic` for the table `S` of the bridge is read from those three definitions.

**Variant (I).** Replace (H2) by the hypothesis that \(S\) vanishes whenever some slot carries the tag \((0,0)\); this follows from multilinearity, which also gives \(S_{\ldots,(0,0),\ldots}=0\). Taking \(m(A)=1\) exactly when \(A=(0,0)\), and a single new channel with actor \(0\in\mathcal F_0\) and coefficient \(h_a\), the same proof goes through and needs no non-zero element of \(\mathcal F_0\).

**Attempts to find a violated clause.** I went through every field of `WilsonFamily`, `WilsonRegularity`, `Hierarchy`, `Reconstructed`, `OperatorProductExpansion`, `TraceTest`, `Properties` and the quantifiers of `JointExistence`, as recorded in the steps, looking in particular at: the order of choice (family and tokens before \(p\); handled by fixing \(T_0\) once and using the same \(T\) for every \(p\)); arity zero and the empty cluster (the case that excludes zero insertions; here \(M_\emptyset=1\) and \(h_\emptyset\) repairs the identity of (b)); independence of the coefficients from \(D\) and \(s\); the coefficient bound for the added channels; the requirement that mesh limits hold at every suitable period index and not only on the sequence; and the instance arguments of `Hierarchy`. I found no violated clause. Points not checked by elaboration, on which the argument depends: that `wilsonLaw` has mass one for the public definition (argued in (b)); the range of `ZMod.valMinAbs`; that a two-point discrete space carries the required `MetricSpace`, `BorelSpace`, `CompactSpace` instances (any two-point subset of \(\mathbb R\) does); the reading of `classMoment` as giving (H2). These are (I).

## (d) What this shows and what it does not

It shows, given the formal witness of `YangMills.constructed` (which satisfies (H1) and (H2) by the source reading above, not by elaboration): the public statement `JointExistence`, taken alone, does not force the selected Wightman theory to arise from the Wilson lattice measure. Given hierarchy data satisfying the three non-lattice properties and the homogeneity (H2), the lattice clause can be met by insertions that never read the configuration. The reason is structural: the lattice enters only through the mixed correlations; these are linked to the selected ones only by the phase average and the shared OPE data; correlations are not required to be multilinear in the fields, and non-selected phases are not required to be positive. In the witness of (c) the lattice moment of every token of positive grade is zero, including the grade-four field of the trace test, whose selected two-point function keeps its singularity.

**Addendum (revision 1): the public statement does not distinguish gauge groups.** This is finding M3 of the independent review (`review/hairer-claude-20261113/mathematics.md`), checked and formalised by the author sessions in `formal/GroupIndependence.lean`. Given a hierarchy over a family of one group \(g_0\) with (H1), the vanishing hypothesis of the Variant, and homogeneity of degree two of the selected two-point correlation of the grade-four field under one real scalar, the body of `JointExistence` holds at every gauge group \(g\) with \(\dim\mathfrak g>0\), again with constant insertions (`YangMills.group_transfer_witness`). The construction rescales the grade-four field by \(\sqrt{\dim\mathfrak g/\dim\mathfrak g_0}\), copies the hierarchy to a family over \(g\) built from the normalised Haar measure, and applies (c). The homogeneity is not a public clause, because `traceTwo` is defined through the function `correlation` and not through the field map; it holds for the constructed witness by multilinearity. In the full project this gives the body of `JointExistence` at every group of positive dimension, simple or not, from the constructed witness at a single simple group (`formal/GroupIndependenceFull.lean`). The gauge group, its representation and the Wilson action thus enter the public statement only through the lattice clause, which (c) meets without reading the configuration. The reading is the same as for (c): this is about what the public statement certifies and says nothing against `YangMills.constructed`, whose conclusion at each group concerns that group's own lattice family. Statements, hypotheses and build evidence are in `formal/README.md`, section "Group independence".

It does not show:

1. any defect in the Lean proof of `YangMills.jointExistence` or in the kernel evidence;
2. any defect in the detailed theorem `YangMills.constructed`, whose witness is a specific hierarchy and not an arbitrary one (below);
3. that a theory other than the constructed one satisfies the public statement: the construction does not produce a selected theory, it reuses that of \(T_0\). Whether the three non-lattice properties (with the vanishing hypothesis) can be met by an explicit theory without Yang–Mills input, for instance a generalised free field, is open; see the G01 review `review/hairer-claude-20261113/mathematics.md`, M4. (Revision 1: an earlier version of this item said the three properties were "as hard as before", which this note does not support.)
4. that the manuscript's statement of the target is wrong: `statement.tex` reproduces the Lean statement faithfully, including this weakness, and lines 74–77 and 134–137 already warn that mixed and selected correlations play different roles.

**The detailed theorem, at statement level (S unless marked).** `YangMills.constructed` (`Lean/Construction.lean` 15–31) does not quantify existentially over a `Hierarchy`: it asserts properties of the definite object `hierarchy F Q`, built from internal data, over the definite family `wilsonFamily C`.

- Multilinearity: every correlation of `hierarchy F Q`, for every state, is `S.useAt (spectator F s)`, a value of a `MultilinearMap`. The function \(M\) of (c) is not of this form.
- Phases as tables: `spectator F s := s.elim S F.α.ensemble` (`Lean/YM/OPE/Eye/Views.lean` 25–26), so each phase correlation is that of the table `F.α.ensemble i` and the mixed one that of the extracted table `S`; `publicReconstruction` identifies them with `(F.α.ensemble i).expect` on words (`Lean/HierarchyBridge.lean` 100–102).
- Positivity: `F.room.kernel : S.Hierarchy` contains reflection positivity of the mixed table (`positive : S.RP`, `onTests : S.RPSmear`, `Lean/YM/Euclid/Words.lean` 141–148); the phase certificate contains `states (i) : (C.ensemble i).Hierarchy` for every phase (`Lean/YM/Phase/Mixture.lean` 141); and `Headline.Reconstruction.characters` asserts `WightmanAxioms` for every character, including `positive : W.wordsPositive A.J` (`Lean/YM/Nonperturbative/HeadlineSpec.lean`, structures `WightmanAxioms` and `Reconstruction`), exported by `constructed` also as `∀ i, (view (L.actors i)).Axioms`. A phase with correlations \(2M-S\) is not offered by this structure.
- Insertions: the family is a `YM.Family` with `C.Makes` (`Lean/YM/Source/Construction.lean` 24–65); its insertions are `C.seen`, i.e. `C.O N p v` \(=\) `(C.factory d N p).cylinder (C.localSection d) v` (`Lean/YM/Extraction/LatticeFamily.lean` 26), linear in the label (`Family.linearO`, `Lean/YM/Source/Construction.lean` 117) and translation covariant (`Family.translation`, same file), and the mixed table is tied to them by the conjunct `Ordered C S`.

So the specific construction of (c) is not available for `constructed`: each of the three freedoms it uses (non-multilinear mixed correlation, a non-positive compensating phase, free constant insertions) is absent there. (U) Whether the conclusion of `constructed`, read as a specification, excludes every degenerate witness was not examined; that would require the definitions of `Makes`, `Final`, `Table.Hierarchy` and `EveryPhase.Almanac` in full, which is outside G01.

## (e) Strengthenings of the public statement that would block the construction

These are observations about which added clause would defeat the argument of (c), not requests.

1. Multilinearity: requiring, for every state \(s\), that `correlation s` be linear in each field component at fixed grades. Then \(M\) must be multilinear, \(m\) linear on each \(\mathcal F_d\), and the cancellation \(m((0,u))-\tfrac12m((0,2u))\ne0\) used in Step 7 is impossible. (A multilinear product-form mixed state remains conceivable; (I) I did not determine whether it can be made compatible with the common OPE.)
2. Positivity or reconstruction for every phase: requiring each `correlation (some χ)` to be reflection positive, or to have its own Wightman reconstruction as in `Headline.Reconstruction.characters`. Then \(S^-=2M-S\) is in general not admissible: for instance its time-axis two-point function of the trace-test field is \(-T(r)\), negative at short distance, which is incompatible with reflection positivity if that field is real (I; reality of the field was not checked).
3. A single phase, or equality of selected and mixed correlations. With \(X\) a point, mixed and selected coincide, and constant insertions that are also independent of \(x\) would give a constant two-point function, contradicting `shortDistance`. (Insertions constant in \(U\) but depending on \(x\) and \((N,p)\) would need a separate argument.)
4. Prescribed insertions: requiring the insertions to be specified gauge-invariant functions of the links (for example fixed lattice expressions with stated renormalisation), or at least linear in a token space with a non-degeneracy condition tying them to the configuration.
5. Exporting to the public statement the identification that the detailed theorem already has: that the hierarchy is the one read from an extracted table of lattice moments, with its phases given by the tables of a decomposition of that table.

Each of 1–4 blocks the particular argument of (c); none of them has been shown here to make the statement adequate in a stronger sense.
