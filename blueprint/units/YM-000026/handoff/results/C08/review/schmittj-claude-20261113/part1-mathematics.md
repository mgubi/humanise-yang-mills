## Findings, ordered by severity

### F1. GAP — the normalization identity $e^{E_{\rm coarse}+R_0+S_0}=\mathscr O\,a(0)$ is used without proof or named producer

- Location: `marked-block-integral.tex` lines 200-210; `leaf-expansion.tex` lines 161-171 (the middle display $e^{E+R+S}=h a_0$). Already present before PR #93; not touched by it. Manuscript.
- Quoted passage (marked-block-integral 202-204): "That regrouping uses the same fixed background and gives $b(\theta)=G_\theta e^{R_0+S_0}$. Its background normalization is $e^{E_{\rm coarse}+R_0+S_0}=\mathscr O a(0)$." Leaf-expansion 166-171 lists the three identities and then says "The two exact gas regroupings and their numerical majorants are inputs here, as is the exact block expansion in the last identity."
- Verified reasoning: I re-did the substitution. From $\mathcal T\rho+\theta\mathcal Tf=\mathscr O a(\theta)G_\theta$ (Sections forest-average-log and forced-exclusion applied to the affine values) and $b(\theta)=G_\theta e^{R_0+S_0}$ (soft-log-fusion, `eq:fusion-gas-identity`, applied twice with $\theta$-independent logarithms), one gets $\mathcal T\rho+\theta\mathcal Tf=\mathscr O a(0)e^{-R_0-S_0}\,\frac{a(\theta)}{a(0)}b(\theta)$. This equals the right side of `eq:leaf-tilted-identity` if and only if $\mathscr O a(0)e^{-R_0-S_0}=e^{E_{\rm coarse}}$, where $E_{\rm coarse}$ must be the exponent of the coarse representation for which $\rho_{\rm coarse}=e^{E_{\rm coarse}}\mathcal G(K)$ (leaf-expansion line 156, `eq:leaf-raw-identity`). So this is not bookkeeping: it identifies the coarse exponent of the next representation with log(overhead × regular exponential) minus the two soft corrections. In the source it is a theorem with hypotheses, not a definition: `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.cone` (`Lean/YM/PhysicalNext/RG_NEXT_COMPLETE_REPEATABLE_NONLINEAR_STEP/Mallow/Brisk.lean`) proves `exp((H.result x).exponent U) * exp(∑' Tap0.logarithm) * exp(∑' T'.logarithm) = overhead (prism …) * homesteadPool O U`, via `Mallow.prom` and `Song.creditRing` and the structural facts `H.owns`, `cruise_EX`, `(H.core …).exponent`. Neither chapter names this as an input or proves it; leaf-expansion lists the other two identities as inputs but not this one.
- Status: GAP (a substantive identity used silently). It does not invalidate any local computation.
- Requested change: in both places state explicitly that the coarse exponent $E_{\rm coarse}$ of the output representation satisfies $e^{E_{\rm coarse}+R_0+S_0}=\mathscr O a(0)$, name the producer (the chapter that constructs the output exponent, or prove it here), and add it to the list of inputs in leaf-expansion lines 170-171 and to the C08 ledger with its source `Mallow.cone`/`prom`.

### F2. INACCURACY — the product moment bound in the reassembly is an unnamed input

- Location: `gaussian-reassembly.tex` lines 219-224 ("The Gaussian moment estimate gives … $\int|\Phi_{K,I,J}|\,d\nu_F\,d\gamma_C\le\prod_v p_v$"); reused in `marked-block-integral.tex` lines 35-37 and 84-87. Pre-existing. Manuscript.
- Reasoning: the molecule $\Phi_{K,I,J}$ is a product of arbitrarily many factors with overlapping Gaussian reads. A bound by the product of individual prices is a substantive multi-factor (Hölder/Cauchy-Schwarz plus warning) estimate, not a consequence of one-factor bounds. Every later absolute-convergence and Tonelli step in these chapters rests on it. The chapter cites no section or label for it; the intro only says the "funded sector hypotheses … are retained", and marked-block-integral calls it the "all-species integrated product bounds as in Section gaussian-reassembly", which is circular as a reference.
- Requested change: cite the producing label (C07's integrated-price result, presumably `sec:inherited-activity-prices`/`sec:gaussian-moment-prices`) with its exact statement, and record the edge in `edges.md`.

### F3. INACCURACY — the "distinct connected-set criterion" invoked in forced-exclusion and marked-activity-jets is not stated anywhere

- Location: `forced-exclusion.tex` lines 156-157, 175-177, 191-193; `marked-activity-jets.tex` lines 249-254, 270-272. Pre-existing. Manuscript.
- Quoted: "Thus these prices meet the distinct connected-set criterion with allowance $\eta$ and tail ratio $1/2$, by (second-smallness)"; "The second inequality is the explicit connected-tree degree bound of Section cluster-expansion."
- Verified reasoning: `sec:cluster-expansion` states only the tuple estimates (`eq:cluster-marked-size`, `eq:cluster-local-tail`) and the rooted forest bound `eq:cluster-forest-bound`; `eq:ope-forest-price` in `sec:ope-grouping` gives $e^\gamma-1$, not a geometric tail. The bound actually needed is: if $\sup_x\sum_{i\ni x}w_ie^{(a+\delta)c(X_i)}\le\gamma$ and $4h_B\gamma/\delta\le q<1$, then $\sum_{S\ \rm distinct,\ connected,\ x\in\cup X_i,\ |S|=m+1}\prod_{i\in S}w_ie^{ac(X_i)}\le\gamma q^m$. I checked that it follows from `eq:cluster-forest-bound` with a distinguished root weight $v_i=\mathbf 1_{x\in X_i}w_i$: choose a label of $S$ containing $x$ as root, list the other $m$ labels in any of $m!$ orders, and use a breadth-first spanning tree as rooted parent map; each set is then counted at least $m!$ times, which the $1/m!$ absorbs. With $w=2v$, $\gamma=\eta$, $\delta=\varepsilon$, $q=1/2$ this gives exactly the $2\eta$ used at forced-exclusion line 193. So the claim is true, but its statement and proof are missing.
- Requested change: add this distinct-set corollary (two lines) to `sec:cluster-expansion` (C06 owner) or prove it in forced-exclusion, and cite it in both chapters.

### F4. INACCURACY — undefined objects in gaussian-reassembly: flat reference $A_0$ and mean $\mu$

- Location: `gaussian-reassembly.tex` lines 14-15, 30, 88-89, 154. Pre-existing; the PR #93 paragraph (lines 15-20) clarifies $A$ and $C$ but not $A_0$ or $\mu$. Manuscript and records.
- Reasoning: $A_0$ is only called "its flat reference". In the source it is `Coat.blank.plate` (`Dye.lean`: field `V:=1`, no seeds), so all free bonds are active and the identity completion is on tree coordinates only, unlike $A$, which is completed on all inactive coordinates. This is exactly the form used in forest-average-log ($A^0_F=M_F^TM_F+D_F^0$), but a reader of gaussian-reassembly cannot know it. $\mu$ is never defined in this chapter; in the source it is `Coat.μ = erect(driftM)`, `driftM = -(kernelA)ᵀ linearBill` with the unsymmetrized windowed inverse `kernelA = shutter.pad plate`, so $\mu$ is neither $-A^{-1}\ell$ nor $-C\ell$ and $\widehat h\ne0$ in general. The algebra of `eq:reassembly-square-cancel` is valid for any $\mu$ with zero inactive coordinates (I re-derived it), so no proof step fails; but the HANDOFF claim that "the draft distinguishes the E6 affine precision, symmetrized window covariance and mean operator" is not borne out in this chapter for the mean operator.
- Requested change: define $A_0$ (field one, no seeds, completion on tree coordinates) and $\mu$ (with reference to its defining section, `sec:mean-action-jets` or C07's local mean), and state that $\mu$'s inactive coordinates vanish as a cited fact (source: `Coat.μ_sleep`, which uses the window and funded-tube hypotheses).
