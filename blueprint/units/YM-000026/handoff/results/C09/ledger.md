## Stationary coefficient prices

`prop:ex-row-quadratic-price` consumes one row family with
`sum_i |c_{a,i}| exp(sigma length_{a,i}) <= C` for each plaquette type,
with `sigma > 0`. It supplies a common picture for every positive torus side,
with width `min(1,sigma/(8(t+1)^2))` before signed-motion averaging. The
two-index quadratic expansion is countable and the extension is complex
bilinear rather than Hermitian. The downstream averaged price also includes
the signed-motion factor; a raw price is not an averaged price.
