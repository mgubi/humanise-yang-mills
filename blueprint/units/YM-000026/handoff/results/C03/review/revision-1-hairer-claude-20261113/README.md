## Requests

- **R1 (minor, `phase-quantitative-producer.tex`, the stated merger input).** The statement takes the merged lead to be \(\sum_iP_i.x\) but does not say that this series converges. In the source, `merge` defines the lead as a `tsum`, which Lean sets to zero when the family is not summable. The docstring of `merge_format` notes that a separate price proof is needed to make it converge (`Lean/YM/Extra/SOURCE_HISTORIES/Merge.lean` 26–37). Add summability of the leads to the hypotheses of the outstanding input, or state how it follows from the remainder budgets. Everything else in the stated input matches `merge`, `merge_format` and `merge_budget`.
- **R2 (minor, `thm:ex-fullmesh-fixedbounds-producer`).** The proof now needs root-test budgets for the finitely many reflected and conjugated orientations of each trial, and says this "is part of the hypothesis that bounded primary rows have paid budgets". The statement does not say so. Put it in the statement, for example "…paid root-test budgets, for every bounded row family and each of its finitely many reflected and conjugated orientations, in the following sense…". Otherwise the proof uses more than the theorem assumes.
- **R3 (minor, `reflection-transport.tex` application paragraph and `../ledger.md` row I-C03-cutoff-covariance).** The derivation of \(b_{\mathrm{cut},t}\to b_t\) from the point limits is right in substance. The source lemma `YM.Bid.B04` also uses `C.Makes`, `S.Hierarchy`, `Grid.plain v` (conjugation-fixed tokens, `Lean/YM/Phase/Approx.lean` 24) and genericity of the grid configuration as a hypothesis. The first two are global and the last two are present in `ex-ordinary-reflection` (real basis labels; internally generic configurations). Say in the paragraph that it uses the proposition's real-basis and genericity assumptions, rather than "generic for large \(t\)", and list the four premises in the ledger row.
- **R4 (nit, `actual-slab-copy-bounds.tex`).** \(R_\rho\overline{R_sv}=R_{\rho s}\overline v\) uses the commutation of `reflection-transport.tex`. Cite it. The bound itself only needs \(\|R_{\rho s}\overline v\|\le\|R_{\rho s}\|\|v\|\).
- **R5 (nit, `../ledger.md` row E3).** The inserted clause "the union-level form … depends on E4 parts (2)–(3)" now runs into "with \(a_v=K=4(D+1)\)", so the sentence reads as if \(a_v=K\) belonged to E4. Split the sentence.
