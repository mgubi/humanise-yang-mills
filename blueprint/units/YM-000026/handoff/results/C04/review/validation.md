## Reviewer findings rejected or downgraded

No factual claim of the four agent reports that was re-checked turned out to be false: every quoted LaTeX passage and every quoted Lean declaration was found at the stated place (line numbers occasionally off by one or two). What is rejected is the severity or the framing of the following, so that the author is not sent on false errands.

1. D-I-1 "ERROR — The cutoff convergence asserted in the locality proof is false as written". Rejected as a mathematical error. The sentence at `locality.tex` 199–204 is false only under the literal parse that the cutoffs themselves converge to one in \(\mathscr S\); the intended statement (the products \(\chi(X/R)f\) converge to \(f\) and have support in \(\operatorname{supp}f\)) is true, is proved in the same manuscript at `lorentz-covariance.tex` 95–99, and makes the step valid. Kept as a required rewording (M1), not as an invalid proof.
2. C-II-1 "ERROR — Wrong defining file for `YM.Bid.C08`". Rejected as an error of correspondence. It is a wrong path and an incomplete description in a row that the author marks "statement name only"; the use `H.1` is stated correctly, and `lem:schwartz-motion` proves both conjuncts of `C08`. Kept as a reference correction (L1).
3. C-II-2 "ERROR — `Urs W K` is matched to the hypotheses of Lorentz covariance instead of its conclusion". Rejected as an error of correspondence. The quoted words are in a request to the reviewer, not in the table; the table (line 16) uses `Urs.translation` and `Urs.rotation` in the correct direction, and the manuscript corollary correctly assumes the hypotheses of the theorem and uses its conclusions. Kept as a wording correction (L2).
4. D-I-2 "GAP — The endpoint handoff does not identify the additional input establishing that some field is nonzero". Rejected as a gap in C04.
