Repaired gap (substantive)

F1. The Poincaré-invariance hypothesis of the word construction was never discharged. Proposition prop:word-motion (reconstruction.tex) assumes (eq:word-motion-input): K(gb,gc)=K(b,c) and continuity of g↦K(b,gc). The chapter said these were "to be established before this construction is applied", and no chapter in the 873-page draft established them (checked by grepping all \refs to the equation, the proposition and the input). The endpoint Definition ex-reconstructed needs the resulting strongly continuous unitary Poincaré representation, so this was a real hole in the dependency chain, not only a presentational one.

The pinned source proves exactly this statement as YM.Work.C28.answer (Lean/YM/Work/C28.lean; consumed through YM.Integrated.reconstructionArcs.cν in Lean/YM/Next/BOUNDARY/Stage.lean and by YM.Boundary.harvest via Surface.Strong/Surface.Transforms in Lean/YM/Fields/Lo.lean). reconstruction-evidence.md lists C01–C27 and C29 but not C28, which explains the omission.

Repair: new Corollary cor:word-motion-inputs in boundary-detection.tex, with a detailed proof following C28's three steps: (i) the star commutes with the motion (rev_po, using J ρ = ρ J from prop:lorentz-label-descent); (ii) invariance of the word functional, splitting g=(a,I)(0,Λ) and applying translation invariance then Lorentz covariance from thm:lorentz-covariance (expect_po, gpush_split); (iii) continuity by pinning all labels in one finite level, expanding in a basis with continuous coefficients, and using Schwartz continuity of the affine pullback (strong, continuous_pinned, continuous_push). It also records that the translation hypothesis of prop:word-motion is prop:heat-cups. The corollary is placed after cor:word-test-inputs, which discharges the other inputs of the same construction.

F2. Same-data requirement on the involution J (new edge C03-C14-to-C04-involution-identity). The word construction (heat-vectors, reconstruction) uses the reflected conjugate J of reflection.tex; prop:lorentz-label-descent proves commutation with the involution J_d of input:calibrated-actions. F1 needs these to be the same map. The text after the corollary shows this is forced, not conventional: with θ the time reflection and κ the real structure, θκ commutes with K_a=-iL_a, while κ alone anticommutes. In Lean the identification is the hypothesis YM.Boundary.starRead J h (J acts as the time-axis adjoint of the real spin) used through YM.Boundary.Letter.rewrites. C14 states that its Q_d preserve "reality and signed motions", which would give J_dQ_d=Q_dJ_d^0 for J=θκ, but the C14 author should confirm this explicitly.

Notation repairs (no mathematical change)

  • N1. The letter V denoted both the unitary time group of prop:complex-heat and the isometry H→H_E of prop:word-motion, and the two met in the proof of thm:field-spectrum. The isometry is now 𝒱 (\mathcal V) in reconstruction.tex, spectral-measure.tex and complex-heat.tex. The apologetic sentence in complex-heat.tex is removed.

  • N2. reconstruction.tex wrote U_E(a) for the joint Euclidean translation group, which complex-heat.tex and spectral-measure.tex call T_E (U_E there is spatial only). It now uses T_E, introduced in the statement.

  • N3. In prop:word-motion, "h_t(ab)" used a for a translation acting on a word, while the same paragraph used ab for concatenation. It now reads h_t(τ_a b).
