## A. Errors repaired

**A1. Incoming path attached to the wrong bed** (`path-attachment-producer.tex`, one-step interface and alias paragraph). The text said the incoming path is attached to the coarse bed and that "the coarse row" carries the floor, attachment and sworn conditions. In `YM.Stationary.Scud` (`Lean/YM/Next/STATIONARY/Level/Transition.lean`) the fields are `hill1 : Hull B ε w D p n G`, `attached : Attached B R`, `funded : R.Bounds p τ A Λ`, and in `YM.Stationary.florentine` (`Lean/YM/FullVolume/Canvas/Weather.lean`) the instance is `B = V.H N (m+1)` with fine sides, `T = V.H N m`. The output satisfies `Y.row = rowPath T ∧ Attached T Y` (`Fluxed`, `Canvas/Flux.lean`). Repaired in both places, and the output statement now says explicitly that the output path is attached to the coarse bed in the coarse clip.

**A2. Wilson derivative orders shifted by one** (same chapter, description of the annulus producer). "Orders one, two and three" are picture indices; `YM.Stationary.Recorded` (`Lift/Pathways.lean`) compares a picture of index `t` with `noteAt f (t+1)`, the Taylor coefficient of order `t+1`, and `Wilson_stream G t` (`Shape/ClassicalMatch.lean`) returns a `Picture G t`. Repaired: orders two, three, four.

**A3. Classical cubic/quartic coefficients described as determinant coefficients** (same chapter, path-budget paragraph). `pureStroke` and `pressStroke` (`Shape/Exchange.lean`) are recorded against `victory`, the tested action on the stationary graph (`Joint/Symmetry.lean`); `pressStroke` is the quartic response minus three contractions `quaver` of two cubic responses through `(V p)⁻¹`, which `shaftInv` identifies with the symbol of the inverse of the vertical matrix `commonM`. `exchangeYield` gives the bounds `A'/6` and `(C'+3A'(D A'))/24`. The numerical bounds in the text were right; the interpretation was wrong. Repaired.

**A4. Stage and level indices conflated in the loop threshold** (same chapter, factor-64 subsection). In `florentine` the hypothesis is `let j := a.clock.J (m+1); T j (L.L j) ≤ a.clock.loopCap m ∧ a.clock.loopCap (m+1)/2 ≤ a.clock.loopCap m ∧ 1 ≤ a.clock.loopCap m`. Repaired; the three inequalities are stated as schedule inputs (Lean producer `YM.Stationary.clocks`, `Level/Recurse.lean`).

**A5. Period-uniformity of the Hessian gap denied** (same chapter, end of the chart subsection). The text said the argument does "not assert that their neighborhoods or Hessian gaps are uniform in the period". `YM.Bid.F01` / `YM.Work.F01.answer` (`Lean/YM/Bid/Station.lean`, `Lean/YM/Work/F01.lean`) give one `η = min a K.penalty/(1+β+γ)`, `β = curlFactor L X C`, `X = box((j+3)L+4L)`, for every `c f S` with the given kernel, `L`, `j` and `Larger S`, every forest, bed, clip and path; `BudgetGap.ask` (`Level/SliceGate.lean`) is quantified the same way. The chapter's own formula already had this uniformity, and `uniform-stationary-chart.tex` (C09) and `actual-reader-short-covariance.tex` (C13) use it. Repaired: hypotheses (larger datum, forest slice, forest directions) stated before the estimates; uniformity of `η` stated; non-uniformity retained only for the implicit-function neighbourhoods.

**A6. Unsupported attribution of the regular reset purchase** (`physical-reset-reorganization.tex`, end of the cost subsection). The text said the payment of `sec:nonlinear-payment-selection` gives `E_new ≤ δ` and the absorption inequalities. That section proves `eq:nonlinear-soft-price` and `eq:nonlinear-payment` only. In the source the regular inequality is `Fees.regular` and is proved in `YM.AnalyticBridge.VerifiedReset.Time.cash` (`VERIFIED_RESET_STEP/Currency.lean`) from `Time.table` and `Time.authorized` (`Tables.lean`) and the late cubic purchase. Repaired by the new subsection "The stage constants and the early allocation" (see B4) and a corrected attribution.

**A7. "Specialization" of the C06 splitting** (`physical-reset-reorganization.tex`, definition of `d_i`). The C06 review's observation is correct: `YM.AnalyticBridge.VerifiedReset.Residual.fault` and `Residual.splits` (`Residual.lean`) mask the counterterm by `Healthy t {d.site}`, not by the stencil. The identity is correct but is not an instance of `eq:local-atom-identity`. Repaired: the identity is displayed and verified directly, and the text says it is not a special case. I also added why the singleton mask controls the clover (`flags` in `Lean/YM/RG/Masks.lean`: a site is flagged iff some plaquette touching it is unhealthy).

**A8. "Heat bound"** (`path-attachment-producer.tex`). `YM.Bid.HeatG` is `SameJet 2` of the tested action along `ρ` and `½ gaugeFiscal B x x`. It is an identity. Note `SameJet j` (`Lean/YM/PCalc/Chart.lean`) is analyticity of both functions plus equality of the `j`-th derivative only; the repaired text says exactly that (not "equal 2-jets").
