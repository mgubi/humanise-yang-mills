This is the threshold handoff contract. Ready scopes activate with the parent claim-release comment in issue26. G04/G06 have prerequisites and F01 is reserved. A package document is not a lock. Read the live issue, linked PRs and all claims before starting, and recheck after posting the claim required by your instructions. Coordinator: @hairer. Source revision: 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464.

One author and a separate reviewer

Each C package is one bounded author session over a coherent group of existing chapters. Audit its incoming interfaces, complete genuine missing arguments within that scope, correct errors and improve definitions/explanations. Do not replace a missing proof by a new assumption. Report upstream draft dependencies precisely. If a new producer needs a different chapter, propose a disjoint split with exact scope and await its live allocation; do not duplicate canonical work. An overlarge package may be split at a mathematical interface before work, with updated issue ownership and disjoint output paths.

After author handoff, a different agent claims the review phase of that package. The reviewer owns only results/Cnn/review/; the author owns the assigned prose and the other report files. Review mathematics and Lean correspondence separately, even if one qualified reviewer performs both. Record final content hashes and source anchors. Every substantial correction returns to authoring and then renewed review; no self-approval or updating another reviewer's approval. Existing bounded passes can be carried only after checking their exact scope and unchanged hashes. They do not prove dependency closure. Human acceptance remains separate.

All C packages can begin their own audits in parallel. Edits are disjoint, but mathematical completion may wait for another package. No group is presumed to have no incoming dependencies merely because it is early in the current PDF. Track dependencies at theorem/output level: C16, for example, contains both an early public-run constructor and the final assembly. Opposite edges between two packages are not by themselves a circular proof; a cycle between the actual mathematical premises and conclusions is.

Required input/output ledger

Give every cross-package dependency a stable edge ID, such as C14-to-C16-stress-normalization. Both producer and consumer acknowledge the exact statement and common-data match in their ledgers; unresolved edges remain visible until both sides and the independent reviewer agree.

For each substantive theorem or coherent proof group record:

  • Output statement, label and exact hypotheses/quantifiers.

  • Every incoming mathematical premise, its producer label/reference and current proof/review status. Definitions and reader prerequisites are identified as such.

  • Exact common objects: family/history, scale and extraction filter, field quotient, operator, stress normalization, coordinate/grade conventions and choice order.

  • Pinned fully qualified Lean declarations and paths, after reading their exact types, definitions/instances and relevant immediate dependencies.

  • Detailed explanation of the match, or a concrete discrepancy/missing proof.

  • Final SHA-256 values, actual commands/results, and independent review findings.

Do not substitute a theorem name, clean axiom report, checklist, or proof outline for mathematical justification. A missing literature match is neither a novelty claim nor permission to omit a step. Reuse the canonical exposition and shared literature notes, then make only a focused search in original sources. Prove the application and every additional variant. Keep implementation details and audit records in the blueprint, not the monograph.

Writing and editorial ownership

Use conventional mathematical language. Introduce each symbol and concept before use; give the mathematical reason for each substantive step and show estimates with their actual uniformity. Follow the Lean argument's main steps. Once G05 supplies the notation/order contract, each C author applies the agreed local notation and explanatory edits in its own files, then obtains renewed independent review. Report any proposed change to a shared definition rather than changing another group's chapter. G04 owns the outline; G03 the reference audit; G05 the symbolic index/order contract. F01 alone applies shared includes/preamble/PDF updates after the user's later instruction.

Validation and submission

Follow templates/proof-unit for new substantive proof units and keep their machine-readable source/claim/review record with the prose correspondence. Existing canonical YM-000026 chapters stay in that unit; do not rename or duplicate it. Put revision ledgers in your assigned results directory and provide the exact metadata additions for the eventual integrator. Machine-readable approvals remain unset until the applicable independent review process accepts the final content.

Run python3 scripts/check_blueprint.py for blueprint changes and git diff --check. When changing mathematical LaTeX, build a local wrapper containing your changed chapters and their referenced canonical inputs; inspect the changed rendered pages. Audit-only reports do not require an artificial LaTeX wrapper. Record actual commands and failures; label unrun checks as unrun. Do not rebuild the entire Lean tree or replay axioms per package. Preserve Lean, the pinned toolchain and dependencies. Reuse the source evidence and #37's separate clean-build work with their exact limitations.

Push one focused draft PR against the checkpoint branch, reference your issue and #26, and leave a concise handoff with proven results, outstanding external inputs, review hashes and next steps. No merge, relicensing or publication. Do not modify another contributor's credentials or release their claim. Work in a separate clone/worktree and use a unique session identifier.

Completion gate

All package labels being checked is insufficient. G06 must test the union of the ledgers for unproved substantive premises, mismatched common data, cycles and unsupported citations. External #33/#34 outputs and canonical22 review response must be integrated at their checked versions. Any failure creates an explicit bounded repair with an owner. F01 is reserved for the maintainer's later request; completion of a package does not authorize the stopped parent to resume.

After F01, G06 (or a new independent reviewer) must sign off on the final semantic diff and exact content hashes. F01 may apply reviewed patches and checked editorial changes, but any substantive assembly rewrite, renaming or changed mathematical claim needs renewed independent mathematics/source review. The integrator never refreshes another reviewer’s approval itself.

Read newly merged shared evidence, including the OS, Gaussian/nonlinear/rank interface, Balaban and running-coupling review notes. These upstream inputs may be newer than the frozen branch; preserve their ownership.
