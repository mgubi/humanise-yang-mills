## Scope and conclusion

The prose is `manuscript/existence/centered-construction.tex`. It follows the
producer actually used by the public construction and derives the uniform
centered root-power estimate conditionally from the paid single-source route. The
upstream uniform receipt production is stated as a hypothesis wherever the
producer chain forwards rather than derives a required bound. Its key
finite-volume step is the exact marked-copy estimate corresponding to the
source occurrence bound followed by the centered root-test estimate. It then
uses the opened-depth schedule, the vacuum treasury estimate, and the I04
factorial-column width bound to obtain the desired power of the lattice
spacing. It does not use the alternate multisource `Offer`/`Thick` path.

The proof's root-step prose is a mathematical compression of the proved
finite-census estimate; the table below records its precise assumptions and
source derivation. This unit remains a draft until independent review confirms
the compression preserves every hypothesis and quantifier.

## Actual route versus optional branches

The public `YangMills.constructed` route reaches the centered estimate by
`Run.again` → `ahead` → paid unlatched history → `hearthstone` →
`gardena`. `centered-estimates.tex` consumes this result afterward. This
correspondence is a route trace, not a claim that the forwarded `Geyser` fields
have all been proved from the earliest run-selection data in this unit.

The separately drafted `centered-budget.tex` and `centered-production.tex`
follow an alternate multisource `Offer`/`Thick` route and are not called by
`YangMills.constructed`, `Run.again`, or `ahead`. The associated normalized
overlay-density price proof is therefore optional for this public theorem.
The I04 width calculation is genuinely on the public route, through
`Baskets.cheap` and `gardena`, and the article reuses its exact final bound.
