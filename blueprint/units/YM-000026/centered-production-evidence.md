## Scope and result

The prose is `manuscript/existence/centered-production.tex`. It proves the
finite-volume weighted coefficient-price bound from the actual overlay density
expansion, the positive vacuum denominator bound, the source pay/coverage scale
arithmetic, and two elementary geometric/width estimates. The finite-volume
transfer/root step is in `centered-budget.tex`. The multisource lane/birth/raw
producer is stated as an exact open dependency, not claimed proved here.

The source does **not** construct the RG stop service records from the mere
existence of a Wilson history. The finite-volume price result is conditional on
actual radial lane data. The `Fuel.record` scale arithmetic is conditional on
ready/covering certificates. Construction of the physical lanes, their terminal
birth estimate, the raw adjacent-scale estimates, and the cutoff thickness
bound remains a substantive open producer task.

## Limits and open dependency

The radial lane estimate is proved, but its hypotheses are substantive: a lane
must exist at each relevant slab with common uniform radial budgets. Likewise, the eventual stop records require ready and coverage predicates.
More fundamentally, the offered lanes already assume raw one-step estimates,
and the terminal birth argument assumes thickness. `Forward.step` is an
optional conditional route to the raw estimate; its upstream component and
integration hypotheses are not established from the gauge setup here. The next
producer unit must trace actual callers of `Offer`, `Unbilled.Raw`, and `Thick`
back from the final supplier, prove the available upstream estimates, and mark
any still-open analytic premises explicitly.
