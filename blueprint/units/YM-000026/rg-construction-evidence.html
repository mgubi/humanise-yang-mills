Exact World producer

The source theorem is YM.RG08.Flat.World.overlap in Lean/YM/RG08/Flat/World.lean. Its hypotheses are:

  1. World.Provides H P: for every world point x, its horizon satisfies Suffices P; for every low x ≤ i < fine x, the actual order-two tower at cutoff fine x, level i, has the same coefficient kernel as the Wilson flight-duo tower for that schedule and penalty.

  2. World.Funding W P w θ: a common positive width w, bounded by the map width α; adjacent widths fit both prices; θ>0; each adjacent fold satisfies 2*w_i ≤ L_i*w_{i+1}; the exact neutralCoin charge at width w_{i+1} is at most the staircase drop; and the top allowance is a polynomial in the prescribed age.

Its conclusion constructs one packet for each (x,i) in the world interval, with folded cost bounded by A * age(x,i)^d * (a(fine x)/a(i))^c, for uniform A≥0, d∈ℕ, and c>0. The packet is neutral, unit-lattice covariant, and represents the quadratic-precision difference on every torus side tested by the world. The article expands the private helpers at_end, to_here, and existsRows instead of identifying the result with record unpacking.

Proof organization and limits

The terminal packet proof is reorganized from the constant-column comparison: the two metric zero modes agree, the strip bounds control both saddle packets, and the floor identities give the finite curvature representation. For the predecessor step, the local Schur identity and neutral-packet push estimate are proved in flat-step.tex; this unit verifies how the actual history supplies its order-two atlas bounds, carrier matching, city law, and travel condition. The cost recurrence is exact, not an asymptotic convergence argument. Its scalar consequence is proved in flat-estimate.tex.

No outside result is used to replace a construction estimate: all numerical steps here are finite sums, a Schur identity, and the stated scalar recurrence. The upstream FlatStep and atlas proofs remain conditional on the separate Wilson wire and train estimates audited in the free-kernel units. Those source producer chains must still be completed and independently reviewed before the full public existence proof is complete.
