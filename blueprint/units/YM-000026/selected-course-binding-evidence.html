Finding

The unconditional mathematical result in the manuscript is the same-family binding. The returned Run retains bought : Ahead R.b R.Q; specializing that continuation to the bolt, schedule, transfers and doors stored in the same run yields the binding for the literal R.family. The Chateau reception supplies its RankCovariance.Binding, and the public run assembly identifies the family levels with the directed alloy levels.

A separate elementary lemma proves Paid V a → Paid U a when the three wait requests decrease coordinatewise. It treats the period condition as a global inequality for every schedule index and proves containment of the entire plateau interval, including truncated natural subtraction. This is a general weakening lemma, not evidence that the public output has a particular paid wait.

The service construction has an intermediate upgraded wait and a universal franchise receipt internally. The exported selected-run interface does not expose that raw receipt for the final chosen run: it is consumed while constructing the calibrated company. This source observation alone does not show that downstream use is impossible: the exported Chateau rated, warrant and market outputs may suffice for later local estimates without recovering the raw frequent-stage receipt. The exact question for the next source pass is whether those outputs supply the quantified inputs used by earn_epoch, the bush construction and the later local consumers on this same R.family. Do not infer a new analytic obstruction from the absent raw receipt. In the internal franchise receipt, Metering.cost is a Launch on the same family, stage, current purse and price parameters, and its coup is the Corridor; no separate launch purchase is needed if that receipt is used.

Paid-wait weakening used

For waits over the same bar, U ≤w V means pointwise r_U(j)≤r_V(j) and w_U(j,s)≤w_V(j,s) for all stages and scales, and p_U≤p_V for the minimum-period request. Paid(U,A) has a separate global condition p_U≤P_A(i) for every schedule index i. Its plateau condition says that for every eligible stage j there is a scale index k with r_U(j)≤s_A(k), w_U(j,s_A(k))≤k, and J_A(i)=j throughout the whole truncated interval from k−w_U(j,s_A(k)) to k+w_U(j,s_A(k)).

Under U≤w V, the global period inequalities weaken pointwise. At each plateau, reuse the same k: if a=w_U(j,s_A(k))≤b=w_V(j,s_A(k)), then max(0,k−b)≤max(0,k−a) and k+a≤k+b. Thus the V plateau interval contains the full U interval, and its ready-time and window upper bounds imply the corresponding bounds for U. This proves Paid(V,A)→Paid(U,A); no reverse implication is used.
