Mathematical steps expanded

  • The radial lemma is stated with the necessary region, integrability, and coefficient hypotheses: a finite measurable region E_4 containing the unit ball, real integrable g, integrable pole h_G, positive c_wt, alphaPay=c_wt∫g, and c_wt∫h_G=-slopeConstant*log(mu^-1). The last identity is proved by poleSpin from polar_m and joy_two/joy_heights; the coefficient identity is polar_clock. The proof then follows from the exact Waves1/Waves2 inequalities. vol(B_r)=pi^2*r^4/2 gives the inner-ball contribution A*pi^2/2. On the exterior, |k|>=1 gives the contribution at most A*vol(E_4). On the annulus, int |k|^-3 = 2*pi^2*(1-mu) and int |k|^-6 = pi^2*(mu^-2-1); after multiplying the latter by mu^2, their total is at most 3*pi^2*D. The exact pole integral is -slopeConstant*log(mu^-1). This proves the displayed constant costZone(A,D) without suppressing the inner or outer regions.

  • The final ramp budget is the source inequality with its exact assumptions Lambda/128≤Lambda' and B*gamma≤1/256: (C + |x| V + B Lambda)*gamma <= Lambda'. From |x+y|+1<=d, derive |x|<=d+|y|; charge the first terms using 2*(C+(d+|y|)V)*gamma<=Lambda', and the annulus width term using B*gamma<=1/256 and Lambda/128<=Lambda'.

  • The finite history proof is ordinary downward induction for each fixed cutoff, then stores each finite column in one horizon. No diagonal limit or unproved compatibility between independently chosen cutoffs is used.

  • For the positive-root step, any convergent subsequence of bounded positive-root matrices has a positive semidefinite limit whose square is the prescribed limit matrix. Uniqueness of that positive square root forces all cluster points to coincide; finite-dimensional boundedness then implies convergence of the complete sequence.
