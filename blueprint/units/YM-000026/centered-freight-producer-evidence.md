## Scope

The prose is `manuscript/existence/centered-freight-producer.tex`. It follows
the public selected-run producer of the finite-volume transfer representation
used by the centered estimate. Its central mathematical work is the finite
backward construction of compatible voices and the algebraic normalization of
the transfer frame. It proves the finite induction and its exact finite-sum
integral identity from the selected one-step ticket estimates.

The finite interpolation ticket is still a stated quantitative input here.
The proof does not derive all local field interpolation and shipment prices
from primitive resource data. This chapter is therefore a checked producer
stage, not a claim that the complete public theorem has become unconditional.

## Mathematical compression and limits

The ordinary-math proof uses no infinite diagonal or compactness argument in
this producer step. It uses finite sums over the source tally
`Motion × Index`, linearity and motion covariance of the normalized push, and
finite downward induction on the scale interval. In the one-step assembly,
the tally coefficients obey the exact frame identity
`v = sum_j chi(v,j) alpha_j`; the pushed density identity is obtained by applying
the transition to this finite sum. The bounds on the absolute coefficient
weights are exactly the paid ticket, frame-interpolation, and orbit-cost
inequalities recorded in the selected history.

The finite interpolation coupons are produced in the selected history by the
clock/heading construction described above, from its principal-field,
regular-field, and forced-slot estimates. The public route also constructs the
door, terminal voice, and transfer/music package on the same history. This
unit still assumes the selected schedule-price and budget certificate, the
held spring with its endpoint fields, the arrival/rate and slot packages, the
terminal keeper, and the terminal birth receipt. It does not derive those
upstream packages from the original resource purchase. The centered bound is
therefore conditional on these precisely named producer inputs.
