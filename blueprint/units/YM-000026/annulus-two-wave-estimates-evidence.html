Expanded argument

  1. polar_clock identifies the coefficient functional alphaPay of the analytically assembled response picture with the normalized integral of its continued second response kernel on the fundamental four-dimensional momentum region. This is not the finite sampled PoleTest coefficient; the latter is the separate recording conclusion in HouseWord.done and profit. Differentiation under the integral is justified by the common analytic bound. poleSpin then evaluates the continuum term: rotational invariance gives the angular moments 1/4 and 1/24; the bracket has mean -11/12; the radial integral of r^-4 over mu<r≤1 is 2*pi^2 log(mu^-1). With cartWeight=(2*pi)^-4, this is exactly -11 Casimir/(24*pi^2) log(mu^-1), the source slope constant.

  2. The finite two-channel identity is Rally.coin together with haloRate. The manuscript expands the free halo calculation, including tr(ad(e_b)^2)=-Casimir: with rho=|p|^2, C=-Casimir, L=-4 p_1^2, and Delta(t)=rho+2p_0 t+t^2, the orbital and vector numerators are respectively LC/rho and rho^{-1}(4L-8t^2)C. The second numerator follows by multiplying the displayed 2-by-2 block matrices. Quotient differentiation gives their second derivatives, and orbital minus one half vector is 2 poleVec. The remaining interacting/reference split is exactly Rally.coin; the factors 1/2 arise from amena and keelRay. Subtracting the free halo identity yields the displayed response-minus-pole decomposition.

  3. Fleet.tadControl gives the upper-channel bounds u/T^2+v*mu^2/T^6 (for each of the interacting and reference upper channels). Fleet.bubbleControl bounds each whole lower face by A/T^4; Fleet.haloControl gives the lower-face errors after subtracting the corresponding free halos, A*(T+mu^2/T^2)/T^4 and B*(T+mu^2/T^2)/T^4. Their proofs use Fiesta product estimates and focus_bill, whose trace step is ||trace M|| <= dim * vflux(M). In the astronomy application these are applied at two different scales: Rally.register uses T1=|x|+mu on all centered records, while Rally.shell uses T2=|x| only on the subfamily mu<=|x|. The manuscript keeps these receipts separate. The local stationary-family proof that constructs their inputs is still an open prose-production obligation.

  4. With T1=|x|+mu, the full-frame identity gives the first wave |g|<=A1/T1^4. At T2=|x|, (AT5b) and the shell receipts give |K-Pi| <= (u+x0)/r^2+(v+y0)*mu^2/r^6 +(A+B)*(r^-3+mu^2*r^-6). Since r<=1, this implies the second wave. Passing to real parts preserves the bound.

  5. Lemma lem:path-radial-log in path-attachment-producer.tex integrates these two waves over the inner ball, annulus, and exterior of the unit ball. This unit proves the missing normalized pole identity and the finite-volume two-wave conversion; it does not claim a new bound on the local stationary chart's input price.
