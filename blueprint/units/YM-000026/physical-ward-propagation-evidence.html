Exact analytic boundary

The manuscript proves the finite-configuration propagation, the collinear stress-packet estimate, and the finite-level A01–A08 bridges from the stated mathematical inputs. The packet calculation uses the already reviewed positive line/point/exterior estimates in sec:physical-ward-packet-prices and the fixed-band cone estimate in sec:physical-ward-stress-cone; it derives the remaining packet rate by the weighted Cauchy decomposition displayed in the TeX. In particular, the four cove exponents are at most -119.5, -972, -146, and -221.5, and the complement costs -101. The exact profile factor exp(100 n) leaves an integrable shell bound C exp(-n). The exterior term uses positive-time damped spectral control, not an undamped zeroth moment. The cove lies where each normalized transverse coordinate is in [-1,1]; the transverse cutoff is one there and supported in [-2,2]. Its normalized dilations are included in both price weights.

The same-slice identity is applied only after the weighted Cauchy and damped exterior bounds establish spectral integrability, and the strict temporal-gap plus Schwartz bounds establish position-side integrability and Fubini. The finite-volume stress response then tends to zero, giving the collinear angular identity. This argument uses the actual finite signed cluster expansion and coordinate-reflection cancellation, rather than identifying an individual cluster with the stress insertion.

The remaining free-theory inputs are distinct and explicit: the collinear classifier YM.Maxwell.Free.Scenes.scanner and the internal-map erasure YM.Maxwell.Free.AwayTeam.erasing, with its low-motion, stress-annihilation, and commuting Ends/statistics hypotheses. The actual-stress-normalization unit supplies the common selected curve, all finite Track/ActLaw tail requests, finite-window jet convergence, stress-meter convergence, and the spin-side normalization. The manuscript adds the finite algebraic rank step: augment the finite selected collinear row map by one functional nonzero on the free derivative, pass injectivity from the free map by basiswise convergence, and compare the positional response with the derivative. Grade inclusion makes the resulting nonzero scalar independent of grade. No downstream rotation-action argument is used to obtain that scalar.
