Status: bounded source/manuscript audit; no new mathematical proof. Source pin: 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464. This is an audit of the C11 same-history interfaces, not a claim that C11 or the common history is closed.

Question and conclusion

Do the existing public-binding, common-funded-history, path-attachment, actual-phase-grid, and same-data compiler chapters already derive the exact path/clip, Alongside, inherited-law, and Tissue inputs at the local marked step from one public selected history?

Not yet. The chapters establish the same-history framework and several local implications, but the local compiler still assumes a same-step receipt that is not produced by the common-history prose. The missing edge is a same-data construction of the Seeks package (or an equivalent expanded receipt) for each step of the retained history. Tissue is not an additional analytic obstruction once a Lawful witness with the exact requested parameters is available: Tissue.cloth constructs it. The existence and parameter match of that witness are part of the missing binding.

Source-level seam

The following immediate declarations were checked in the pinned Lean tree.

  1. YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.Pump.Seeks in Lean/YM/PhysicalNext/AFTERNOON_INTERACTING_RANK_COMPARISON/Opacity/Offers.lean is the exact local package. For a Dyed object v, teller P, and scale x, it contains P.Funds S v.things, the arc identity, the equalities v.things.up=v.source.path and v.things.high=v.source.clip, the retimed threshold and period bound, Bid.Larger, the existential Aff.Alongside H K v.things, an existential v.source.Lawful P.top.width P.top.paths Γ δ w with Γ≤betaF(G,retime x,L)^(1/8) and δ≤(retime x)^(-1/2), and the head, walls, beta-band, and flag identities.

  2. YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.Pump.hoops takes Seeks as an input and upgrades it to Helps; it copies alongside, law, and path/clip identities into the latter. Pump.vouches builds Mesa by applying hoops, but still supplies Seeks at its arrange field. Thus these declarations are consumers of the exact receipt, not a producer of it.

  3. Aff.Alongside A B T, defined in Lean/YM/AnalyticNext/CONNECTED_ACTIVITY_BOUNDS/Station.lean, is stronger than a path/clip equality. It requires Attached A T.up, Attached B T.down, the diagonal identity for T.R, the two-sided roll identity, floor records for both beds at the corresponding high/low clips, and sworn conditions for both beds. None follows from Seeks.paths and Seeks.clips alone; Seeks.alongside is a separate field.

  4. Soft.Tissue R o C L q w, in Lean/YM/PhysicalNext/AFTERNOON_INTERACTING_RANK_COMPARISON/Soft/Tissue.lean, consists of Lawful for the white representation at the same tuple, nonempty moves for every signed/site pair, and the Petals R q regular activity budget. Soft.Tissue.cloth constructs this package directly from R.Lawful o C L q w; Petals.thin only weakens its price parameter in the increasing direction. Therefore, where the selected Seeks.law witness has exactly the consumer's (o,C,L,q,w) tuple, cloth discharges Tissue without another analytic estimate. A change in the tuple, or merely knowing upper bounds on Γ,δ, is not an automatic Tissue construction; one must state the source weakening and check all five parameters.

  5. Soft.Burl.made_soft consumes a Tissue input for its output representation and proves the pushed density equality. It does not produce the original Tissue or identify its representation with the one in the selected history. Soft.Bonfire also explicitly stores Tissue in its per-level Buoyant data; that constructor is downstream of the history-level receipt.

Immediate Seeks producer and public-route boundary

The source-level search found an actual local constructor, so the earlier statement that no inspected source constructs Seeks must be read narrowly: the remaining question is whether its hypotheses are supplied on the retained public history. YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.fitPost in Lean/YM/PhysicalNext/AFTERNOON_INTERACTING_RANK_COMPARISON/Opacity/Forestage.lean constructs Seeks (tabloid …) (tell B i) r X from the same B : Binding C, M : C.Makes, ShowHours, Marquee, Corridor, k≤N, and Bush data. Here its tabloid has source representation P, the tube tunnel B N i p, and the chart, arc, and bed selected in the same course. Its proof fills the receipt as follows:

  • ships B N i p M hs supplies funding for that tube; hP.exactTree and hP.exactClip identify its upper path and high clip with the source representation. The dressing and calendar_warm provide the arc and flag equalities, while Z.great.timing, B.sized, and ShowHours.yard provide the retimed threshold, period, and support-exponent identities.

  • The Aff.Alongside witness is explicitly assembled from the two horizon beds B.world.H N (i+1) and B.world.H N i; attachments come from B.paths, the diagonal/roll data from B.pays.crossings and B.pays.stack, floors from B.pays.all, and sworn conditions from B.sworn. This is a construction of the full two-bed record, not an inference from path and clip equalities.

  • The inherited-law witness is hP.rated from Bush, transported across the same-stage identity obtained from the corridor plateau and matched to the old-price tuple by ShowHours. The beta-band and walls fields use the Bush/Paint and bush_prices receipts.

There are two immediate consumers. YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.intoProp in the same file calls fitPost, obtains the applicable Mesa from Marquee.great.localizes, and invokes its arrange field at that exact S and Dyed object. YM.PhysicalNext.InteractingRank.Soft.lifted in Soft/Bonfire.lean also calls fitPost (with the neutralized tabloid) and feeds its result to Kraft.prescribes; Soft/Unlid.lean consumes that result. The only direct call to Pump.vouches found by targeted search is in Opacity/Auction.lean (Pump.billable), where it constructs a Mesa; this does not independently produce Seeks, but its local hoops/arrangement consumer requires the same kind of local receipt.

The actual public theorem has a distinct, now precisely identified route. YM.Independent.constructionRun in Lean/YM/Independent/ConstructionRun.lean invokes YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.R2I.RG05Joint.selectRunWithTreasuryPeriod_preselected_quote_orders_postnumeric and returns the original Run, its Campaign, Campaign.Pillars, funding, and ActualRunNativeComponentRecipe.Attached. The selected route's YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.Campaign.Album.makes construction in Lean/YM/FullVolume/Shroud/Clan.lean gives C.Makes on the stamped R.family from that campaign/history data, when this is the family chosen for the local course. But constructionRun does not itself return the extra local Binding C, ShowHours, Marquee, Corridor, and Bush witnesses as fields. ActualRunNativeComponentRecipe.Attached records the original sheet, quotes, numerical/post-length requests, and native realization; it is not itself a Binding C or fitPost package. The canonical common-history chapters may supply these through their separately paid same-history interfaces, but a same-object substitution from their exact output to every argument of fitPost is not established by the constructor statements read here. Thus the remaining join is narrower: bind the selected public run's retained R.family and funded history to one Binding C, ShowHours, Marquee, Corridor, and Bush on the same C, N, and indices, then apply fitPost and the relevant arrange. There is no need to reprove the Alongside field construction inside fitPost once those inputs are bound.

If the actual course instead supplies an arbitrary Dyed v, the precise correspondence needed is an equality/transport identifying it with the Forestage.tabloid on the same source, tube, chart, and token; no arbitrary- Dyed generalization was found. The separate full stationary PathData.Bounds/Attached and HorizCalc gates remain their own C11 path producer; this audit does not conflate them with local Aff.Alongside.
