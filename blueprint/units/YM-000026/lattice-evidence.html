Scope and notation

The prose uses the shared entries in blueprint/glossary.md: G ⊂ U(r_G), P_p, D_N, a_N=D_N^{-1}, \ell_{N,p}=P_pD_N, \beta_{N,p}, and the public Wilson family \mathcal W. The local symbols \ell, \Lambda_\ell, E_\ell, S_\ell, H_G, \rho_\ell, and Z_\ell mean respectively the site count, periodic four-torus, positively oriented links, plaquette action, probability Haar law, product Haar prior, and partition integral defined in the manuscript. RG scales q_j, RG clock \chi(k,i), weights w_i, level outputs \Phi_{N,i}, and limiting kernels F_i are local to the quoted flat-sector interface. In particular q_j has not been identified with public mesh a_N.

The manuscript gauge-invariance proof is newly organized in elementary matrix language. Its formal counterpart is also explicit in YM.Lattice.face_gauge and YM.Wilson.energy_gauge; the bridge YangMills.energy_eq identifies the public and internal action sums. The public definition itself does not prove that an arbitrary insertion token is gauge invariant: the relevant condition is separately required by YangMills.WilsonRegularity.invariant.

The finite normalization argument is newly organized from compactness, continuity, positivity, and the integral formula. The internal proof is in YM.Wilson.partition_pos and the probability-measure instance on YM.Wilson.law; YangMills.law_eq transports that law to the public definition. The manuscript does not cite the Lean proof as a mathematical reference.

The flat-sector displayed bound is a faithful reorganization of YM.RG08.Flat.Quorum.approximates; the frame bound and pointwise limit are its frames and converges fields. The actual supplier is produced by YM.RG08.Flat.entrance_gate, after a compatible level, sufficient horizon, price system, delay and block-length floor are supplied. I inspected the statement and immediate proof body, but did not trace the theorem's imported proof infrastructure or independently derive the estimate. No claim is made here that the RowCost estimate is proved by this prose.

Literature and editorial decisions

The shared literature directory contains only its README and the unrelated SU(2) simplicity note; no accepted lattice-construction result is available there. A targeted web search located Kenneth G. Wilson, “Confinement of quarks,” Physical Review D 10 (1974), 2445–2459, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.10.2445. The APS result inspected was the abstract/metadata, not the full paper. It is therefore used only as historical context, and no theorem, continuum limit, or RG estimate is attributed to it. The elementary finite gauge-invariance and normalization arguments are proved directly in the manuscript.

No source-specific nickname is used as a mathematical assertion. The public Wilson family is kept distinct from internal YM.Family; the public mesh a_N is kept distinct from the internal RG scale q_j; and the public coupling \beta_{N,p} is kept distinct from the positive blocking penalty \lambda_{\rm ker}.
