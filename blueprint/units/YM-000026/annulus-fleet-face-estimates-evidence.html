Local stationary face estimates

Status and scope

Draft, awaiting independent mathematical and source review. Source pin: 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464 from source/upstream.json. Owned prose: manuscript/existence/annulus-fleet-face-estimates.tex.

The prose proves the price-to-face calculation, the all-centered and shell scale substitutions, and the selected-bed row identities (F14)–(F16). It derives the scale-weighted input prices (F6a)–(F6d) from the selected path packages: tab budget (L), one-leg budget (L/T), and two-leg budget (L/T^2), with (L=T^2) for the interacting row and (L=\mu^2) for the regulated reference. The shell difference prices follow from the cubic tab contact, the common linear coefficient of the interacting and free marked rows, and the regulator contribution. It also derives metric normalization by cancelling the regulator tab in the same House identity, derives the free symbol identity from the degree-zero exterior Cartan identity, computes the vacuum marked row's first radial coefficient from the three plaquette placements and the fixed free film, and proves the total denominator's zeroth inverse bound from the bed floor and cold lower bound. The halo prices are shell-only because the unregulated reference inverse is undefined at the center.

  • For the halo difference, cool0 gives the inverse-denominator difference price rimEdge/T^2; sepJ gives the displaced-leg difference price T^2, which is enlarged to rimEdge*T because T≤rimEdge; brass combines these four receipts by the ordered product-difference identity. haloControl takes its trace price. These are exactly the four summands of (F9), in the order of the noncommuting factors, not an estimate for an unordered scalar product.

  • YM.Stationary.bedLum, howlin, inflamed, jarBurst, barePost, granite_tab, rockEdge, vectorPing: YM/Next/STATIONARY/OneLoop/Leading.lean. barePost follows by expanding the same-bed House equality and cancelling the regulator tab. The proof of bedLum then subtracts the leader identity: the metric difference is first-order at zero by Floor.starts, the two edge symbols add two powers, and rockEdge supplies the cubic difference between the exact lattice scalar and its quadratic part. For marked rows, granary is the local film plus the fixed free film; inflamed splits the two-leg coefficient into its local soft part (jarBurst, order two) and the grey vacuum-minus-vector part (howlin, order two). vectorPing proves the vacuum first radial coefficient: coronet sums the three Wilson plaquette placements and axePing gives the fixed free-film coefficient; their sum is exactly the displayed vector insertion. It is therefore derived in the prose, not retained as an independent identity assumption.

  • YM.Stationary.dais_floor, throne_floor, granaryTall, grainTall, solidT, getReals, glaze: YM/Next/STATIONARY/Spectral/AuxReferences.lean, OneLoop/Propagators.lean, and OneLoop/Bubbles.lean. Combining dais_floor with cold_safe gives min(epsilon,1)/2^16 times the square of the scale as a lower eigenvalue bound for the interacting denominator; throne_floor gives 2^-16 for the ghost denominator. solidT turns these stable lower bounds into the required total inverse control. getReals assembles both Floe/Week/Tall packages from the same Hstar sequence, Orion House/floor/fee/soft fields, common grey reference and centered ColdR data. The separate shell comparisons cool0, sepJ, brass, and haloControl require positive unregulated scale and are only applied on mu <= |x|.

  • YM.Stationary.price_step_metric, bed_gain: YM/Next/STATIONARY/Loops/Fourier.lean. The latter adds the two first-order edge-symbol factors to an order-one metric difference.

  • YM.Stationary.patrol_president, patrol_house, Patrol.marries, sipPatrol, sipLight, ashCost1/2/3, limo_cast: YM/Next/STATIONARY/OneLoop/Propagators.lean and Weights.lean. Fee bounds and Cauchy estimates give uniform compact derivative caps. Patrol records these caps through the required derivative order; sipPatrol restricts them to the selected line (x+t e), using “Rays” vanishing and “dawn”'s Taylor remainder estimate, while sipLight supplies the bounded-radius arch price. Radial vanishing forces lower homogeneous Taylor tensors to vanish because each is a polynomial zero on every real direction. This yields the (T^3) tab remainder, (T^2) insertion remainder, and unit-scale arch bound used in the manuscript.

  • YM.Stationary.Week, Week.pays, Week.spot, Week.basic, Fleet.basic, Fleet.bubbleHV, Fleet.tadControl, Fleet.bubbleControl, Tall.storms, Tall.beats, Floe.vector, Floe.ghost, sepJ, bloomJ, and Fleet.cool0: YM/Next/STATIONARY/OneLoop/Propagators.lean. In Week X T L, the tab has budget (L), each spot has budget (T^{-1}L), and each arch has budget (T^{-2}L). getReals supplies the interacting-plus-reference path with interacting tab budget (T^2), regulated reference budget (mu^2), and a Tall bound at (T^2). Hence the total denominator path has tab budget (O(T^2)), its inverse paths have budget (O(T^{-2})), the local and reference arches have budgets (O(1)) and (O(\mu^2/T^2)), and total legs have budget (O(T+\mu^2/T)=O(T)). On the shell, the free scalar denominator has inverse norm (T^{-2}); the local-minus-free tab costs (T^3), the regulator costs (mu^2), and the inverse identity gives (O((T^3+\mu^2)/T^4)=O(\delta/T^2)). For legs, the local row minus the free Wilson leg has Ray order two, while the reference leg costs (O(\mu^2/T)); together these give (O(\delta T)). These are the quantitative derivations of the stated F6 prices, rather than additional price assumptions.

  • The regulated reference's component prices are traced through cinderWeek, cinderTall, chertWeek, cannonWeek, stoneWeek, and glaze in Propagators.lean. The massive free film has tab budget (T^2) and scalar floor (T^2/64); the inverse-reference film's tab/spot/arch costs are (mu^2/T^2,mu^2/T^3,mu^2/T^4). Enlarging its tab budget to (1), taking the nine-factor product, weighting by (mu^2), and adding four constant directional sandwiches gives the actual reference path with costs (mu^2,mu^2/T,mu^2/T^2).

  • The exact inverse-film component identities and product slot formulas used in that calculation are Film.vSpot, Film.back, and Film.pay in YM/Next/STATIONARY/Loops/Syntax.lean. A back spot has two inverse tabs and one original spot; a back arch has the two ordered spot-insertion terms and the original arch term, each flanked by inverse tabs. A product spot has two summands and a product arch four. The manuscript expands these formulas and the inverse-reference costs before iterating the nine factors.

  • Jar.soft in OneLoop/Lattice.lean is the exact soft-3 convention: the unmarked layer has radial order three, the one-mark layer order two, and the two-mark layer order one. jarBurst composes the one-mark layer with a linear two-momentum map; inflamed adds the grey-vacuum/free second-order contact. This is the source of the marked-row contact, not a claim that every component of the action vanishes to degree three.
