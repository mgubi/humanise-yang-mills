Scope and conclusion

The mathematical section is manuscript/existence/centered-estimates.tex. It proves, under explicit finite-volume centered-budget, transfer, symmetry, seam, and planted-cell hypotheses, (i) a fixed-period local modulus on compact cyclic patches, (ii) a common expanding-period modulus on compact generic patches, (iii) an expanding compact bound away from full collisions, and (iv) a fixed-period compact bound on NoHit, which permits coordinate ties. It does not prove that the RG trajectory supplies the centered budget. The separate next obligation is the actual production of that budget from the Wilson/RG estimates.

The quantifiers are kept in source order. Fixed-period estimates allow a mesh tail depending on the period. Expanding estimates have an outer period tail and then a mesh tail for each period. For a finite list of observables, the common budget is the finite maximum at each chosen depth; no monotonicity in depth and no uniformity over all labels or test functions is asserted.

Remaining upstream quantitative obligation

YM.Extraction.Wcmain is proved by YM.Input.Metered.rootwise in Lean/YM/Overlay/Assembly.lean, itself constructed by YM.Extra.Multi.centered in Lean/YM/Extra/MULTISOURCE_BOUNDS/Application.lean; another producer candidate is YM.MergeProof.Accent.gardena in Lean/YM/MergeProof/Gardens.lean. Its exact target is a centered bound, for every grade d, radius R>0 and exponent slack epsilon>0, with constants A,m_0 independent of period, for all depths m>=C.ref d, eventually in volume index, all period indices, and all labels of grade d and norm at most R, with root bound A a_m^(-d-epsilon). The same volume tail works for all period indices, so this is directly strong enough to imply the fixed and expanding CenteredBook clauses by restriction to a fixed period or the cofinal period schedule.

The concrete producer has a substantial prerequisite bundle: C.Makes (Wilson selected representation, sound sections, grade-stage bounds, stencil placement, price bounds, calibrated transfer), plus the assembly hypotheses including Offer, Prepared, Thick, gauge simplicity, and the relevant multi-source centered estimates. These are not proved in this section. The next unit must unpack the actual producer and prove the budget estimate from its quantitative upstream assumptions, rather than cite Wcmain as an unexplained theorem.
