This chapter reorganizes the actual zero/nonzero branch supplier into ordinary estimates. It identifies the component decay powers and explains the quantitative closure. The history-indexed train induction is given as an explicit geometric side-ratio induction, including the coefficient-one principal-plank lower bound, the nozzle recursion, and the separate patch-mass and train-smooth factors.

Exact source objects and bounds

Moderate w f in YM.Next.Free.Moderate (Lean/YM/Next/FREE_KERNELS/Moderate.lean) means that one common positive complex-cube width and one common constant bound every member of the family by C*w i; the members are holomorphic on that cube. Firm w f supplies a lower bound c*w i on the corresponding real enlarged cube. Moderate.kept proves the common-cube lower bound needed to take reciprocals, with radius 

min (η/4) (min θ (c*η/(8*C)))

and lower constant c/2. Moderate.inv then gives the reciprocal estimate. The article's common-cube shrink argument is this quantitative operation, not an unquantified pointwise continuity argument.

Its second term has weight grade(l)^(-4) * mix(l)^(-j), which is also at most mix(l)^(-j). The formal branch closure uses these inequalities (and in the Levy estimate uses the two mass weights before raising its price to one).

Source chain into the exported tower receipt

The thread Flight.wire is defined by its zero/nonzero branch in Lean/YM/Next/FREE_KERNELS/Wire.lean. For arbitrary index type ι, flight family F : ι → Flight j t, and selected alias l : ∀ i, Site (F i).L, Flight.wire_budget assumes only k>0, 4≤j, and 4≤t and proves an entrywise Pay receipt with alias weight decay j (l i). The proof calls copper_budget on zero aliases and stake_budget otherwise. Pay packages the finite matrix-entrywise bounds uniformly over all such choices.

The proved overlap identity is Secured.relay, instantiated for the wire by secure_both; it says that shifting momentum by a reciprocal vector is compensated by adding the corresponding wrap residue to the alias. Reality is the separate Secured.pearl law. Secured.propels converts the weighted entry bound to a spread/alias-average cost. Its proof uses differential_bound (each finite difference adds one scale inverse and one alias-grade factor), residue_cost (the exponential phase is bounded uniformly), and decay_cost plus absorbs for the alias sum. enters and traverse identify Fourier coefficient differences with the true cover-difference operators including centered carries. bankCost combines the finite type labels, and limited_lengths/honored uniformize all word lengths up to j-2 by finite minima of widths and maxima of costs. The order-two tower comes from Flight.duo.T = meshBook (wire k) 2 [] P in Lean/YM/Next/FREE_KERNELS/Primal.lean; its base and first-difference costs are the L^-2 and L^-3 receipts used by the atlas producer.

Classification and remaining work

The article now gives the finite train recursion and its quantitative history induction, including the coefficient-one principal-plank lower bound, geometric side-ratio lower estimate, inverse-square nozzle recursion, and successive reciprocal continuation for halo, lid, bulb, and bend. It also records the actual zero and nonzero branch identities and their exact matrix order in free-kernel-estimates.tex; these calculations reorganize Flight.copper_match, Flight.stake_match, and Flight.wire_match. The reciprocal overlap proof is written from the residue exchange and the two-variable script shift, corresponding to Flight.knots_shift and Secured.relay.

The remaining work is to check these prose calculations against the source in independent review and integrate them with the actual selected-run chain. This chapter does not claim to prove the finite-scale funding or horizon response estimates used by the caller; those are separate construction inputs recorded in the RG construction unit.
