Status: author draft; independent mathematical and source-correspondence review pending. Pinned source revision: 3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464.

Scope

The prose is manuscript/existence/centered-birth-receipt.tex and its main result is thm:ex-selected-birth-moment. It records the selected public route from the terminal census birth polynomial to its finite-volume normalized mode moments. The exact backward integration and compactness argument are proved in the manuscript. The selected service, origin geometry, finite response price, and vacuum denominator estimates remain conditional inputs; this unit does not call them automatic consequences of the birth identity.
