Scope

The prose is manuscript/existence/centered-budget.tex. It proves the exact finite-volume conversion from a radial transfer lane and its stopping-depth bill to a centered copied-product root estimate, including the all-site translation step. It states the lane price estimate as a local input; its proof is now in manuscript/existence/centered-production.tex. The upstream production of ready/covering schedules and service certificates remains open.

The output matches the centeredBudget target: for a grade g label, one centered tiling at depth m, all sites, chamber interiority, and root test bounded by the specified right-hand side. In the source YM.Extraction.Wcmain has one eventual volume tail that works for every period, so the same source estimate supplies both fixed-period and expanding-period budgets by restriction. The prose keeps this quantifier order explicit.

For markers filling the depth-m torus, q=|Lambda_m|. The exact four-dimensional site-count identity is |Lambda_r|=|Lambda_m|(a_m/a_r)^4.
Root extraction YM.Stock.est; YM.Cells.Tiling.rootTest, .powering; Lean/YM/Overlay/Terminal.lean, Lean/YM/Cells/Tiling.lean The full centered mode has one copy per cell. Its moment is M; the root test is ||M||^(1/|Lambda_m|). The lane scalar is (a_r^g)^|Lambda_m|, and the constant weight toll is W^|Lambda_m|. Taking the positive root gives exactly exp((a_m/a_r)^4*tau_r+u+c+gamma*b-g log(a_r)-log W). Since a_r=exp(-scale(r)), the grade term is g scale(r).
All-site chamber interiority and translations YM.Stock.clear_interior, YM.Stock.translate_s; Lean/YM/Overlay/Supplier.lean, Lean/YM/Overlay/Terminal.lean A fixed finite stencil fits inside the centered chamber for every period and site once volume is sufficiently large. Exact conclusion and limits

The conversion proves a finite-volume estimate for a given transfer lane. Its important numerical factors are all explicit: a_r^g once per copied marker, W once per marker, the site-count ratio (a_m/a_r)^4, and the additive radial cost u+c+gamma*b. No factor depending on the number of transfer-matrix states is inserted. The lane needs to fill the entire centered cell set so the root exponent agrees with the full-mode marker count.

The remaining substantive task is to prove the actual stop package from its RG prerequisites: a lane service certificate for all bounded labels and all periods, plus an eventual stopping-depth bill bound of (g+eta) scale(m). The section records that requirement but does not infer it from the existence of a lane or from C.Makes alone.
