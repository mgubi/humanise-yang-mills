Wire branch producer ledger

YM.Next.Free.Flight.wire_budget is not an axiom or a type-level record assumption: it is a proof. Its two branch theorems are:

  • YM.Next.Free.Flight.copper_budget, Lean/YM/Next/FREE_KERNELS/Cable.lean: the zero-alias term. The proof combines the foot and flux product, then the brace/latch/mouth product and pulse/latch/tail correction, multiplies by the dual exterior multiplier, and adds the estimates.

  • YM.Next.Free.Flight.stake_budget, same path: the nonzero-alias term. The proof combines the heart/signal/port-mask/flux/dual-multiplier product and the levy/flux/notch product, then adds them. The alias weight is preserved or weakened by the grade bounds.

  • YM.Next.Free.Flight.wire_budget, same path: partitions the alias site into zero and nonzero cases and invokes the two branch estimates.

The directly invoked component estimates include latch_budget, mouth_budget, foot_budget, brace_budget, copper_budget, notch_budget, levy_budget, stake_budget, and wire_budget in Cable.lean. The products further use flux_budget, sig_budget, portD_budget, and tail_budget from Buffer.lean; heart_budget from Heart.lean; and train bend, bulb, rail, mass, denominator, and alias controls from the adjoining files. The source proof uses the closure operations on Pay and Moderate (sum, product, scaling, transpose/reversal, reciprocal under a proved lower bound, and finite branches). The two article chapters reconstruct the lower train-factor inequalities and the branch identities; their precise source crosswalk is in free-kernel-components-evidence.md.
