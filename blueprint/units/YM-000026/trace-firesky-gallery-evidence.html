Exact selected public path

The exact chart receipt is YM.MergeProof.East.FiresSky: it gives panels P(s,i) for i:Fin 3, base arcs b(i), and a common check budget; each arc is b(i)+s, while currency and angle do not change with s. For every sufficiently large level and every s∈[0,1], it supplies a witness Y satisfying PoisedSky. This is the output of YM.MergeProof.East.embalms from the paid request; it is not yet the station-admissibility receipt.

constructionRun calls the native preselected postnumeric period-clock selector, which uses RG05Joint.plentifulWithPrediction with the native funded prediction and price supply. In the selected Bardo construction, submits produces the paid multi-scale chart request. embalms uses the request and the later budget to obtain a delayed wait and a family of one-parameter chart receipts. The proof constructs the parameter-dependent charts by clipping a real parameter into [0,1]; only each arc is shifted, while the currency and angular data remain fixed. Forward chart estimates and the simultaneous staging estimate supply the finite-level chart, invoice, legacy-cost, actuation, and stamp clauses. Bardo specializes that family to the actual funded configuration and passes it as the final FiresSky family argument to Trace.Over.gang.

The nearby argument hS.costs.night in that call is the separate Moonlit receipt used by the nonlinear local construction; it is not the FiresSky family. The latter is the specialized output hU of embalms. This distinction is needed to read the actual caller correctly.

Scale-selection derivation

For fixed base arc b, the source defines gap A b k = a_k m_k^b, with a_k>0 and m_k the clock monitor. gap_rate proves convergence to zero. The adjacent-scale estimate near_skies proves eventually gap A b k <= gap A (b+1) (k+1). The manuscript's least-crossing argument then proves the interpolation statement: for small positive s, choose the least k>J with gap A b k <= s; minimality and near_skies place s between gap A b k and gap A (b+1) k, and continuity in the arc parameter gives an offset in [0,1]. The proof does not assume the requested scale is already a clock gap.

YM.MergeProof.East.boatsSky combines the chart family with the strict level curve. Its exact conclusion includes an index k, level i, base arc b, price c, and charts R, with s k = Station.gap A b i; the associated TimberSky contains the level and reference-grade conditions, chart and invoice receipts for every N,p with i<N, a SeqPair at costHint(l), and wok … .toStation.Admissible at rank l, size sizeHint(l), and error costHint(l). Internally fliesSky builds the strict curve from clock interpolation; the private await step supplies the SeqPair; podiumSky supplies Granular for the smooth table and real spin; and supply_wheel combines it with F.Ordered, point limits, and the reference hierarchy to obtain the station receipt. The quantitative three-panel estimate is the separate manuscript dependency sec:three-panel-weak-comparison.

YM.MergeProof.East.wakesSky iterates the one-step conclusion grade by grade, requiring the next scale to be below its predecessor. It gives strict scale decrease, strictly increasing sequence indices, and levels tending to infinity. The manuscript proves the scalar interpolation and the elementary recursion from this exact one-step output; it does not identify the one-step output with FiresSky alone.

Finite-cutoff limit and gallery assembly

At each fixed cutoff, the lift bound puts the coefficient maps in a closed ball in a finite-dimensional product space. Compactness gives a proper subfilter limit, and maximality identifies it with the selected ultrafilter. Continuity passes reality and signed covariance; eventual unit identities pass by uniqueness of limits. For a fixed tuple of labels, the finite-volume measurement is a multilinear map depending on period and phase. Continuity of one varying functional is therefore not enough. The theorem calibrated_smear first gives convergence on fixed basis-label tuples from F.Ordered; basis_tuple expands moving vectors in finite bases, and tendsto_multi combines coordinate convergence with those fixed-basis moment limits. The arity and window hypotheses remain in force. The finite-level inequality passes by closedness, then its positive slack tends to zero.

At growing cutoff d, each fixed test eventually lies below the cutoff and inside the expanding window, while the paid error factor is 1/(d+1). This proves tested approach for each fixed test. Finite separating tests give eventual injectivity at each fixed grade; the selected table dimension bound supplies the reverse inequality and hence surjectivity. The start of the isomorphism tail may depend on the grade. The source fills early indices grade by grade; it does not claim a uniform tail over all grades. Unit, reality, and signed covariance hold on each fixed grade's eventual isomorphism tail; arbitrary early isomorphisms need not preserve them. The separation input is the new all-grade free-detector chapter, not only the low-grade trace calculation.

pays_sky packages this scaling with its chart limits. gang fixes the ultrafilter and reference Ward tensor and calls Store_l in both its fixed-scale and arbitrary-null-sequence cases. Store_l produces the full Scribe record using the physical Marvel receipt and field-array inputs. Besides the chart and hierarchy clauses, the record includes: convergence of the recentered physical stress to the material tensor; for each table respecting the selected table, an eventual two-coordinate scalar decomposition of the rescaled energy tensor, with coefficients converging to the prescribed stress coefficient and zero; and convergence of the zoomed scalar observable to the prescribed unit multiple. These are actual separate source fields (Scribe.main, Scribe.gallery, Scribe.goal), not consequences of the scale-selection proof. The correction, rank-one kernel, stable inverse, coefficient limit, and Ward normalization used in the handoff are treated in trace-calibration-producer.tex only under its displayed receipts; this chart unit does not claim to derive those physical inputs. The metric/chart estimates used to construct the one-scale receipts remain assigned to their analytic source chapters.
