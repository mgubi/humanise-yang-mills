## Exact public receipt chain and `hS`/`hF` roles

The selected construction does not treat the three receipts as one hypothesis.

1. `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Legato.resoling`, `Lean/YM/MergeProof/Leaping.lean`, first obtains an arena using `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.yearEnd`, then applies `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.brevity` to obtain `Arrives` on a common enlarged delay. 2. `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.Arrives` is the source object called `hS` at local `Many.ripe`/`Photo.bidding` interfaces. Its first clause is the amnesty/activity witness. Its first payment clause is: for `c,k >= 0`, `k <= b^(-1/8)`, and `c <= exp(-mark(x))`, there is a rate-payment witness at spatial scale `radiusB(x)` with rates `stems` for `c,k`. Its second clause concerns `softNeed` at a reset-compatible profile; `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.offerS` bounds that request by `b^(-1/4)`, and `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.mingle` pays it at the `straw`/`stems` scales.
3. `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.Pungent`, the source object called `hF` at `Many.crowd2`, is supplied as the hold field of `Minty` by `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.Tray.bravo` and `YM.PhysicalNext.Repeat.Mallow.scarce`. It has the same marked payment but with the larger cap `exp(Trio.coverTicket(halo))*b^(-1/8)`. Its reset clause is for the opened request `Spicy.opens`, using the base profile's clip, width, and paths and `Time.credit(profile)*deltaE(x)`; it requires the same reset-size constraint and yields a rate-payment witness at `straw`/`stems`.
