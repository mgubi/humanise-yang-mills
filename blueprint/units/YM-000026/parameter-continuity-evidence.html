Important checks and remaining boundaries

The rescaling direction is lambda=g(beta)/g(beta'), so g(beta')lambda=g(beta). Full Gaussian coordinates and the same covariance are retained when comparing parameters. The Gaussian moment bounds alone were insufficient for a pointwise common dominator; the manuscript explicitly adds the uniform exp(z^TQz/8) envelope. The anchored compensation exponent vanishes at the base point, avoiding an unsupported exponential-polynomial moment assumption.

The perturbed cluster support factor is exp((a+delta)c(Y)), exactly as in Elixir.comforts. Its root price changes from gamma to 3gamma/2, and its ratio from 1/2 to 3/4. The comparison radius may depend on the fixed torus. Common representation, dictionaries, path, clipping, masks, rooms, incoming activities and projected-covariance premises remain explicit.
