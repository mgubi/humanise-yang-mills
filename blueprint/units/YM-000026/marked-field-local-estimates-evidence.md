This unit proves the eventual reserve estimates for each fixed degree and
stencil, and gives the local marked invitation from a paid section, terminal
Keeper, read/nonwrapping geometry, and the advertised budget inputs. Its
inputs are attached later to the selected public run by the field calibration
consumer. It does not prove the recentering/propagation result
`YM.MergeProof.figment`. That source proof constructs one label using
`YM.Extra.Histories.Relay.antilles` from a `Cream`, a single selected
`Music`, its `Sung` estimates, and all later `Tones` and `Carol` receipts;
then `YM.MergeProof.toast` adds the selected marked action to the
backward-dualized Music voice; finally `YM.MergeProof.Accent.filling` transfers
the resulting `Yeast` estimates to all earlier root tests. The finite
numerical steps in `Figment.figment` use the exact choices
`w=(ζ_{d,k}/4)/(||alto_k|| escrow_{N,k}(E))` and the `diet` recurrence.
Those history and height producers remain a separate unfinished proof
obligation and are the next source target. No implication from local
invitation to all-scale root tests is claimed.

The section theorem feeding this result is conditional on a gauge-equivariant
mark imprint. The actual `YM.Push.Mark.Linear.unsym` campaign producer is
still explicitly outstanding in `marked-portfolio-calibration-evidence.md`.
