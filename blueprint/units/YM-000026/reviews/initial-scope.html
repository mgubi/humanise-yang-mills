Exact endpoint and external hypotheses

YangMills.jointExistence in Lean/Solution.lean proves YangMills.JointExistence, defined in Lean/ChallengeSpec.lean. Its sole explicit input is a YangMills.GaugeGroup with GaugeGroup.IsSimple. Lean/PublicTheory.lean makes this a closed subgroup of a specified finite-dimensional unitary matrix group, together with the real subspace of matrices whose every real exponential lies in the subgroup. The generates equivalence ties that subspace to the group; it is not an arbitrary Lie algebra. IsSimple explicitly requires a nonzero commutator and no nontrivial proper real ideal. Connectedness of the group is not a separate hypothesis.

The precise quantifier order is: for each such group, one Wilson family C; regularity of C; for every strictly increasing volume-index map p, a strictly increasing q and a hierarchy T for C satisfying the four properties along p composed with q. C precedes p. The hierarchy and refinement may depend on p, but are chosen before arity, observable, test, OPE configuration, and truncation. The theorem does not assert equality or uniqueness of the hierarchies chosen for different p.

The proof takes the internal penalty b equal to 1. The more general YangMills.constructed accepts arbitrary b > 0; this penalty is neither an additional hypothesis of jointExistence nor the public bare coupling. The public regularity predicate only requests some positive logarithmic running slope. YangMills.regular in Lean/HierarchyBridge.lean supplies the particular slope 2 * YM.Pert.Asymptotics.b0 g.core.

Meaning-determining checks

  1. Wilson origin and limits. YangMills.wilsonLaw, WilsonFamily.finiteMoment, and WilsonFamily.smear in Lean/PublicWilson.lean use the normalized exponential of the actual plaquette action against product Haar measure, followed by products of insertions and mesh-weighted lattice sums. WilsonRegularity requires continuity, gauge invariance and a token-dependent finite lattice-offset stencil uniform in cutoff, volume and anchor. WilsonFamily.Converges requires the full mesh sequence to converge on every suitable fixed-volume row. A further increasing volume subsequence converges to the unselected hierarchy tested off collision diagonals. This is not a diagonal-only convergence assertion and is not convergence to the selected phase.

  2. Public insertion scope. The public token type and insertion functions are existential data. The public predicate does not separately identify tokens with predetermined Wilson loops or curvature polynomials, and the grade-four field in the public trace test is not defined there as a stress-tensor trace. This is a limit on what can be read from the public statement, not evidence that the constructed observables are arbitrary. In fact YangMills.wilsonFamily uses YM.Family.seen; Lean/YM/Source/Construction.lean defines these through calibrated local sections and transfer data, and proves Family.translation, reality, constant, invariant, localO, and continuousO. Any stronger physical identification in the article must explain these definitions or use a separately justified internal result.

  3. Wightman content. Lean/PublicTheory.lean contains actual common-domain operators, a normalized dense cyclic vacuum domain, continuity in Schwartz tests, the smeared adjoint identity, spacelike commutativity, a strongly continuous unitary translation action, and a projection-valued energy-momentum measure with its characteristic-function relation to translations. W0 requires support in the closed forward cone and uniqueness of translation-invariant vectors up to the vacuum. W2 fixes the ordinary affine test pullback and full proper orthochronous Poincare coordinates; it is not covariance under an unspecified trivial symmetry group. Reconstruction additionally requires faithful nonzero fields, injectivity of each finite-dimensional field-space map, coverage of all labels, and surjectivity of token projection. These inspected clauses rule out the immediate empty-label/all-zero-field reading. No positive gap is required.

  4. Analytic bridge. Hierarchy.Reconstructed states holomorphy in the complex time coordinates for each fixed collection of spatial coordinates, on strictly ordered imaginary times. It identifies the selected Euclidean correlations at purely imaginary times and the boundary limits after smearing against products of Schwartz tests. It does not state a general full complex-spacetime tube theorem in this public interface. Permutation symmetry and smoothness concern separated configurations. The phase space is compact metrizable, with a probability measure of full support and correlations continuous in the phase; integration gives the unselected correlations.

  5. OPE quantifiers. Hierarchy.OperatorProductExpansion uses one coefficient system for every phase and every truncation. The coefficient threshold can depend on labels, degree, channel and geometric constants. For fixed labels and truncation, the remainder threshold precedes all admissible positions and all phases. The finite cluster is contained in a ball of radius Mr and has pairwise separation at least eta r; spectators are separated from each other and the center by c. There is no threshold uniform over every truncation degree or every field label. Both full and shortened words are evaluations of this same hierarchy.

  6. Trace test. The selected two-point function of a centered degree-four field satisfies the precise nonzero asymptotic r^8 log^2(1/r) T_2(r) -> 3 dim_R(g.lieAlgebra)/pi^4. The excluded comparison class consists of finite sums of finite products of the explicitly defined mass-shell kernels, including the stated differential numerators. Exclusion of all generalized free fields is not this conclusion. Internal YM.Nonperturbative.Headline.TraceExclusions additionally contains the physical centered stress-trace identity and conditional Maxwell comparisons. Those stronger clauses must not be silently attributed to the four-field public property record.

What the inspected proof establishes, and where the main work lies

The endpoint proof is a short specialization and projection, faithfully preserving the common witness. In Lean/Construction.lean, YangMills.constructed obtains one internal witness from YM.Nonperturbative.existence and combines regular, wilsonLimit, publicReconstruction, publicOPE, and publicTrace. The corresponding bridge files actually identify the lattice law, field system, continuation and word evaluations; they do not create an unrelated public witness.

The next layer is also assembly: YM.Nonperturbative.continuum_with_position_tests in Lean/YM/Nonperturbative/Continuum.lean consumes YM.Independent.constructionRun and YM.R2IGoals.fromFunded. The latter's Broad.refine in Lean/YM/R2I/Campaign/Accepted.lean already supplies full-mesh ordered convergence, a Spec.Final, every-phase reconstruction, a trace result, and OPE inputs. In particular Spec.Final in Lean/YM/Spec/Closed.lean already contains contours, a Wightman system and its realization. Assuming a Final and then proving W0–W3 by unpacking its fields is a valid interface lemma, but is not a reconstruction proof from the lattice.

Minimum substantive obligations for a complete article, beyond the public bridge, are:

These are mathematical inputs to an assembly exposition, not extra assumptions of the formal endpoint. An article may state them as outstanding inputs while drafting, but cannot call the resulting conditional assembly a complete proof of existence. Contacts, nested OPE, all-character Wightman theories and leading perturbative position tests are stronger retained results; determine whether to prove them as necessary internal steps or omit their separate conclusions from the existence article.
