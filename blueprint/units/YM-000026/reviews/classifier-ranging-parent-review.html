Scope and result

Bounded pass for the new general separation argument, its order-one application, nontriviality of each sheet, and the finite-dimensional dual-span conclusion. The rest of the classifier is covered by the separate review, not this verdict. The required radial single-charge theorem is the previously independently reviewed canonical radial-flux chapter, with unchanged normalization.

Read full Lean/YM/Next/MAXWELL/Flavor.lean, Born.lean, and Hidden.lean; read the meaning-determining Sheet/Flavor/color maps in Paint.lean, polynomial wave and normalized coefficient identities in Jets.lean, radial_flux in Affine.lean, K_sample/K_right_D in Kernel.lean, and kernel_pole in Pole.lean. All qualified declarations are in YM.Maxwell.Free.

  • Sheet h is the real Maxwell solution-jet space; Flavor h is its real dual. This is not an identification of invariant labels with all colored polynomials. fill embeds a chosen scalar sheet in one fixed color.

  • payment tests the color-resolved pulse against all zero-order coordinate probes. Vanishing of those pairings makes every component of the pulse zero off its marked point. The compact cutoff derivative is supported away from that point, so the current flux vanishes. For any sheet A of order h, wavePoly/asWave_jet give a global homogeneous Maxwell wave whose normalized h-th Taylor coefficient is A. radial_flux then gives zero = -(h+2)p(A). Since h+2 is nonzero and A is arbitrary, p=0. No positivity of an across-order pairing, and no distributional product at the pole, is assumed.

  • For the inductive step in ranging, start with a lower-order probe q of order ell-1. Its derivative pairing is the higher-order test, with the sign from x-y absorbed in the derivative direction. The hypothesis kills these derivatives. kernel_pole and Euler's identity yield -(4+h+ell-1)K(x)=0. The coefficient is nonzero; lower-order vanishing follows, and induction applies. The candidate now has the correct direction of this implication. It does not differentiate an already zero higher-order pairing to obtain a lower one.

  • Born.has_sheet argues by contradiction from Flavor.cell_alive: if an order-c sheet were zero, all its probes would vanish, forcing the nonzero zero-order coordinate functional to vanish by the just-proved separation. This justifies choosing a probe and sheet with evaluation one at each of the distinct orders used in the determinant specialization.

  • Hidden.probe_radius_span uses all nonzero displacements and a fixed partner order. The zero-annihilator argument, the color decomposition and ordinary finite-dimensional duality justify the asserted span. It is not a span at one momentum or one displacement. Applying the resulting linear map to a basis yields all the polynomial partial derivatives, as in group_meal.

  • In the panel argument the special case h=1, ell=0 is now tested against arbitrary A in S_1. Its radial factor is -3, so it annihilates the whole order-one functional. The erroneous earlier claim about zero-order waves testing the order-one sheet is absent from this candidate.
