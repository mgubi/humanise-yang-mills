Opened and read the original versioned PDF on 13 November 2026: Brian C. Hall, An Elementary Introduction to Groups and Representations, arXiv:math-ph/0005032v1, 31 May 2000.

  • Chapter 2, Definition 2.2, printed p. 9: a matrix Lie group is a subgroup closed in the general linear group. A closed subgroup of the unitary group satisfies this because the unitary group is itself closed in the general linear group.

  • Chapter 3, Definition 3.13, printed p. 36: the exponential-generator definition uses every real parameter, matching YM.Gauge.Generates and the carrier of YM.Gauge.lie in Lean/YM/Gauge/Lie.lean.

  • Chapter 3, Theorem 3.16, printed pp. 40–41: the set is closed under real scalar multiplication, sums and matrix commutators. Zero belongs because its exponential is the identity; finite dimension follows from the ambient matrix space. No connectedness or simplicity assumption is required for these facts. The manuscript retains its separate nonabelian simplicity assumption.
