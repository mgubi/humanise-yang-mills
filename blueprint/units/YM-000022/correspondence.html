Its sole outstanding mathematical input is the uniform running estimate eq:running, supplied by the projection chain hc.wilson.running (qualified declarations listed below). The uniform extraction proposition weakens the construction's hypotheses to the exact numerical interfaces used. The source's Calendar.bar_pos requires a positive intercept, while extraction works for any fixed real intercept. The source's zeroUp_bound needs C.Makes but no simplicity assumption; comparison additionally assumes G.Simple. The latter is used here solely through YM.Gauge.slopeConstant_pos; the section supplies its elementary positivity argument explicitly. Neither the construction hypothesis nor the simplicity predicate is redefined to mean only the consequence used here.

Lean defines Real.sqrt and inversion on every real input, including negative arguments and zero. The definition of regulatorRun has no hypotheses, as its elaborated type confirms. Our ordinary positive-tail definition agrees with that totalized expression eventually, which suffices for the exact conclusion of comparison. There is no assertion here that all finite-prefix terms are positive. The introductory source comment expressly limits the interpretation to the numerical proxy.
