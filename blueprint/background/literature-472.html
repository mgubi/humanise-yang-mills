Fernández–Procacci, arXiv preprint, §4.1, Proposition 5, (4.3) The alternating sum over connected spanning subgraphs of a finite graph equals the signed number of Penrose trees. Apply it to the graph of numbered occurrences, so repeated polymer labels are distinct vertices and their incompatibility is an ordinary edge. A disconnected graph gives zero on both sides. The weighted rooted forest estimate and passage to the infinite alphabet stay in the manuscript.
Hall, arXiv:math-ph/0005032, Definitions 2.2 and 3.13, Theorems 3.6 and 3.23 The local homeomorphism theorem and the two ambient log/exp identities give the neighborhood statement used in the normal chart. The local logarithm identifies a small ambient logarithm with the algebra logarithm, giving the reverse implication. Compare the archived v1 locators already checked in the G03 ledger. This applies even for disconnected G. It does not give the nearest-point property or uniform chart estimates by itself.
DLMF §10.32, §10.31, §10.25, §10.27 The integral uses the positive argument ms and order (j+2)/2. The integer and noninteger series have integer exponents after extracting the prefactor because twice the order is integral. We differentiate actual analytic series factors and control their tails rather than differentiating an unspecified asymptotic expansion. The massless case is elementary. The final exclusion argument is specific to this manuscript.
Munthe-Kaas–Verdier–Vilmart v2, Theorem 1 The pairing formula is for centered real Gaussian vectors. The indicated polynomial-moment limit covers singular covariance and multilinearity covers complex coordinates. This citation is for the pairing rule, not the weighted moment bounds.
Jaffard, journal scan, Proposition 2, p. 464 Exponential decay of the inverse under summability of exponential distance weights and invertibility on the coordinate Hilbert space. The displayed metric and multiplicity check explain the qualitative match. The proof with explicit constants uniform over torus periods and principal windows stays.

Osterwalder–Schrader I, §3 The reflected form follows their Euclidean construction. The checked background record gives the comparison of test spaces and hypotheses. The contractivity proof and later reconstruction remain on the manuscript's tables.
