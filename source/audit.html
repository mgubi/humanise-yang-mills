Audit method and limits

They started at the named declarations and expanded only through immediate meaning-determining definitions. The source inspections used separate clones at repository commit 3a20fee84ce46e8f83824b9e3da7078a8313e987 and the immutable imported source revision above. The parent integrated their reports and compared the results with a pinned Lean #check run covering every root. The full command, result, and output digest appear below. #check is the elaborated-type evidence; source-text readings identify the structures and abbreviations within those printed types. The pretty-printer can hide inferred types or abbreviate dependent arguments; the cited definitions remain authoritative.

The separate accepted issue #2 evidence reports transitive axioms propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound for all 17 roots, with no sorryAx or project-defined endpoint axiom. It also records kernel replay of 100,168 reachable project declarations. That result used a checksum-verified prebuilt artifact, not a clean source rebuild on this host. Its structural reachability inventory is not a mathematical dependency graph, and neither its axiom report nor this type audit establishes the physical adequacy of a definition.
